Contact the Warhammer Survivors guide editors

Reviewed 13 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • An evolution recipe, build or boss answer that no longer works after a patch;
  • A champion, stage or relic unlock that differs on your platform;
  • A broken link, image or video on a Warhammer Survivors page.

Include the page URL, your platform (Steam, Xbox/PC Game Pass, Switch, iOS or Android) and the game version from the title screen. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not Auroch Digital or game support, so we cannot restore saves, transfer progress, refund purchases or grant in-game items. For those, use the developer's support or the store you bought from; the official FAQ lists the current known issues. Do not send passwords, purchase keys or account details. There is no promised reply time.
