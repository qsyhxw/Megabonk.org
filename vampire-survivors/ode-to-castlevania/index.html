INDEPENDENT PLAYER GUIDE · VAMPIRE SURVIVORS

Ode to Castlevania progression checkpoints

Own the DLC and unlock Inlaid Library, then select Ode to Castlevania in Stage Selection. Progress has two connected tracks, castle gates and character requirements. Reaching the Throne Room as Richter advances the normal-stage showdown.

By Megabonk.org · checked 10 November 2026 · Vampire Survivors is by poncle.

If the stage is missing

Reach level 20 in Mad Forest to unlock Inlaid Library and Stage Selection. Check that this edition owns and has installed the DLC, then look down the stage list. The old browser demo cannot host this expansion.

Fight the boss and pick up the relic it drops. A combat win with the relic still on the floor leaves a different state from having the gate unlocked. Use the unlocked warps on later runs while working on character conditions.

Work toward Richter

Check the locked characters’ conditions between runs. The later chain includes Shanoa and Juste, completing a stage with each to unlock Maria, then evolving Maria’s Guardian’s Targe to unlock Richter. These are progression requirements, so buying the DLC does not make Richter immediately selectable.

The character guide below supplies the earlier branches. For Sonia’s independent weapon task use our Sonic Whip route.

Normal-stage showdown and Black Disk

With Richter selected, reach the Throne Room at the top of the eastern Clock Tower. Other characters reaching the room do not trigger the same confrontation. After the scripted loss of powers, prioritize avoiding projectiles and surviving the sequence. Its conclusion grants the Black Disk and opens further progression.

This is a checkpoint guide for the normal DLC stage. Adventure has separate objectives, and an instruction naming a particular weapon, time or Adventure stage must be followed in that mode. The 30-minute base-game Reaper is also a separate encounter. See the Red Death build.

After the Black Disk

Return to the Unlocks list for the newly revealed character conditions and castle areas. Later relic gates include Pile of Secrets. This page covers entry, the initial gates and the showdown checkpoint, with spoilers. It does not provide every later secret or Adventure completion route.
