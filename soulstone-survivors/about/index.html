Soulstone Survivors guide

About the Soulstone Survivors guides

Reviewed 21 November 2026

This is an unofficial guide section for players of Soulstone Survivors, written by the Megabonk.org editors. It is not made by Game Smithing Limited and it is not game support.

Sources: the Steam store page and the official game website for product facts and platforms; the READMEs of the online and local co-op mods for installation and version limits; and dated community reports for builds and the winter event. Each guide lists the sources it relies on, the date we read them and what they do not show. We have not installed the mods or played these builds ourselves, and we say so where it matters.

Checked on 2 November 2026 against Steam App 2066020 (released from Early Access on 17 June 2025). The co-op READMEs target game 1.5.3.2 (online) and 1.5d2 (local); a newer game version may need a newer mod release.

Covered here: the beginner progression loop, skill-chain builds, a Barbarian route comparison and a Russian build page, online and same-PC co-op, mod loaders and the Bumble winter crystal order. To suggest a correction, send the page URL and a public source through the contact page. Players also discuss the game on Reddit · r/SoulstoneSurvivors ↗ and keep reference tables on the Fandom wiki ↗; community data can lag behind a patch. Game names and artwork belong to their owners.
