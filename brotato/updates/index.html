Brotato Updates — New Dawn, 1.2 and the Beta Branch

New Dawn, update 1.2 and Primal Dread are different content labels. The 12 November Steam Balancing Patch brings the tested Evolution changes to the default branch. Use Item Sets to check enabled content.

Developer update history documents the November changes.

Reproduce a guide with Item Sets

The 1.2 announcement introduces switches for expansions and free updates. It gives disabling Paws & Claws while leaving other content active as an example. Open Item Sets from Character Selection and compare the setup with the guide.

If a weapon never appears, inspect enabled content before spending more rerolls. Installed content and enabled content are separate checks.

Check optional participation

Steam Library → Brotato → Properties → Betas shows participation. The 6 November announcement separates balancing from the default branch. It says this balance beta retains progression when changes move to main, and asks players to deactivate and unsubscribe from Workshop mods. Beta instructions

That progression statement applies to this announced beta. Keep your own backup before an experimental setup.

Check the current default-branch rules

The 12 November Balancing Patch releases the tested changes for Steam players. Gain one ban token every four waves through wave 20. Mutation effects reach 70% strength at 40 Evolution and full strength at 100. Mechanization loses extra piercing and bounces. The announcement also rebalances Jungle waves and fixes co-op shop navigation. Official release notes

The publisher plans another weapon-balancing patch. Match future build advice to the installed version and record your active Item Sets.

Match hotfix 1.2.0.3 to the symptom

The 13 November hotfix removes Boomerang from Caveman’s starting weapons. Snowball gains Elemental Damage from purchased or crate items, while Tumor mutations stop granting extra Elemental Damage. Weapons can now target and hit the Brachiosaurus foot, and the wave-end foot crash receives a fix for death and victory. A gamepad shop-selection issue and moving Ammosaurus attacks are also fixed. Official 1.2.0.3 notes

Report a useful discrepancy

Include exact version, branch, map, difficulty, active sets and mods. A tooltip screenshot helps establish which rule you actually have. Use the developer-linked bug forum for reproducible issues.
