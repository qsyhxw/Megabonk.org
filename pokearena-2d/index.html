Set up a training match

From the title menu, select Play → Training. Online matches have their own entry on the title screen.

  1. Choose your fighter. Pick an unlocked Pokémon from the top roster for Player 1.
  2. Set the dummy and arena. Fill the dummy slot, then choose an arena along the bottom of the screen.
  3. Read your kit and select Fight! Try moving and aiming before working through the attacks shown on your fighter’s panel.

Battle controls

In the Alpha 0.64.0 Training selector, Bulbasaur’s kit uses the following inputs. Check your own fighter’s panel for its moves.

WASD + mouse

On a phone, rotate to landscape. For gamepad options or a refresher on inputs, open Settings or Tutorial on the title menu.

Keep your League progress

The game stores League progress and settings in your browser. Return to the same site with the same browser profile. Clearing site data removes the local save. The project lists accounts for online modes. Game privacy notice ↗
