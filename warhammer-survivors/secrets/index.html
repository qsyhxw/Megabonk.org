WARHAMMER SURVIVORS · PLAYER GUIDE

Warhammer Survivors Secrets menu

Unlock Secrets through the relic for your selected universe. The Book of Tzeentch opens the 40K menu, while the Eye of Tzeentch opens the Age of Sigmar menu.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 12 November 2026 · Release v2.1

Find the correct relic firstUse an incantation or follow a riddleContinue through regular unlock objectives

Find the correct relic first

  • 40K — find The Book of Tzeentch on Death World Invasion.
  • Age of Sigmar — find the Eye of Tzeentch in Gnarlwood.

Collect the corresponding relic, then inspect that universe’s Secrets menu.

Use an incantation or follow a riddle

In Secrets, look for the incantation entry field. Type the word exactly as shown, submit it, and check the named reward. Riddles instead describe objectives to carry out during play. Confirm the selected universe and its relic before troubleshooting a missing entry field.

IncantationReward

A complete code catalogue and every riddle solution remain outside this page’s checked coverage. Broad unlock codes can change many unlock states, so choose the reward you intend before submitting.

Continue through regular unlock objectives

For a regular character unlock, rescue the Legend of Catachan. For item objectives, use two followers or Dice Cube. Read the objective’s universe and whether it asks for one run before choosing a loadout.
