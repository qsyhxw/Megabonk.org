Which Pokémon game plays like Megabonk?

If you mean a Pokémon-themed 3D survivor in a browser, check PokeBonk first. If you saw a 2D Pokémon bullet-heaven, check PokéSurvivor. They are separate fan projects, and neither is a Megabonk mod. Choose by the interface and systems you want, then use that project’s own entry and guide.[1][2]

Keep access, saves and versions separate

PokeBonk’s original legal notice describes a browser-only fan project. An APK or another domain with the same title is not made official by its spelling. The author’s current entry and legal links are the identity reference.[1][4]

Our PokeBonk and PokéSurvivor Play pages use disclosed third-party sources. Their accounts, versions and progress are separate from the original projects. For a first visit, identify your intended host; for an existing save, stay with its exact host and account before trying another game.

Both original projects describe themselves as unofficial fan work. They are not official Pokémon releases. “Games like” here compares a player task and format, not ownership or endorsement.
