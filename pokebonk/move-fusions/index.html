Find a PokeBonk move fusion

At Bill’s Machine, two eligible moves each need level 8 or higher. The checked matching order is signature → reaction → same type → MissingNo. A fusion consumes both ingredients, occupies one slot and frees the other.[1]

Prepare the pair before using the machine

  1. Learn both ingredient moves; a saved Build plan does not unlock or force an offer.
  2. Raise each to at least level 8. TM Case can raise ordinary caps to 10, but the readiness check still uses 8.
  3. Inspect Bill’s Machine’s actual pair/result and missing requirements. Weather, buff and already-fused archetypes are not eligible ingredients.
  4. Confirm only when the displayed result fits your plan. The pair’s upgrade modifiers combine; damage-like multipliers add their extra portions, while cooldown multipliers multiply. This does not mean every displayed particle deals the full listed damage.
  5. Use the freed slot for the run’s remaining need. The fusion itself normally caps at five stars; TM Case adds a sixth.

Check a listed kit pair

Select two different listed kit moves to find their recipe.

This tool covers the base moves listed in the 79 League kits. It identifies the result from definitions; it does not simulate damage, verify availability in your run or turn an ineligible buff into an ingredient. Same-move self-pairs are rejected.

All 106 results and useful stat targets

79 signatures + 8 reactions + 18 type fusions + one fallback = 106 results. Many different pairs can lead to one result; 106 is not the count of possible input pairs. The table processes each fusion’s part-scaling definitions rather than copying a damage ranking.[1]

Example: Flower Beam is more than one damage shape

Razor Leaf + Solar Beam gives Flower Beam before any general Grass pairing. Its Area targets the beam and final bloom; Duration targets the beam; Projectiles targets the leaf barrage. An Area choice and a projectile choice therefore change different parts. If your problem is coverage, identify which part is missing enemies before selecting the stat.

For Charmander, Fire Spin + Flamethrower yields Fiery Whirlwind; for Squirtle, Rapid Spin + Hydro Pump yields Hydro Spiral. These are source-based routes, not recorded DPS comparisons.
