About Megabonk.org

Megabonk.org is an independent guide website maintained by its publisher. It covers Megabonk and selected related survivor games; it is not operated by the game or mod developers.

How we check guides

We read original project pages, public game definitions, release notes and identified community reports. We distinguish listed behavior from our recommendations and from actual playtests. A menu or code inspection is not a full clear, a device benchmark or proof of account recovery. Pages state versions, dates and material limits.
