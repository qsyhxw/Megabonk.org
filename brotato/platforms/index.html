Brotato Platforms and Prices — PC, Console and Mobile

Base Brotato is listed on Steam for Windows, Mac and Linux, on Xbox, on Nintendo Switch and Switch 2, on PlayStation 4 and 5, and as a mobile port on iOS and Android. Primal Dread is PC-only at the time of checking; the publisher announced console and mobile versions for early 2027.

Price and listing checks rely on Steam price-history trackers, the Main Gamers platform listing, gg.deals and the Wikipedia and launch-news reports. Prices vary by region and date.

Primal Dread platform status

Primal Dread is expansion content. At the checked date it is available on PC through Steam. The publisher briefing announced console and mobile versions for early 2027, without an exact day. A PC DLC purchase does not give mobile ownership, so check each store before buying.

Before you buy

Check your region, the platform price, and whether the copy is the base game or the Primal Dread expansion. For mobile, read the current store listing, because the free and premium model conflicts across sources. Storefront prices move often.
