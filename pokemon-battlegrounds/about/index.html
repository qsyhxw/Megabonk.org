About the Pokémon Battlegrounds guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 22 November 2026 · Sign-in and practice flow checked 5 November 2026

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org. We do not make PB Ranked, we do not run the Pokémon Battlegrounds channel, and nothing here is endorsed by either. Pokémon names and artwork belong to their owners.

PB Ranked is a free fan game; its own privacy page names its maker. The guide points you to the game and explains what you meet before your first match. It does not host the game, your sign-in or any progress.

What it covers

The guide home gives the game’s current address, then the first-battle path from sign-in to an Easy practice match, the Auto, called-move and Manual choices, what the game keeps for your account and a table for telling PB Ranked apart from the channel website and from separate fan games.

It does not rank trainers or Pokémon, and it publishes no team lists.

Where the answers come from

  • The original client: sign-in, the in-game help, the team screen and an Easy practice battle through its result screen, opened on 5 November 2026. The address change was read from the old address’s own notice on 22 November 2026.
  • The game’s published pages: privacy ↗ and terms ↗, both updated 1 November 2026, supply what the game stores and how seasons work.
  • The creator’s announcement: the channel video ↗ of 30 October 2026 identifies the ranked game.
  • Images: the sign-in screenshot and the task-card crops are captures of the game’s own screens, and the “which game” crop is the channel website’s home page. Competitive play and account transfer were not tried.

How the page stays current

The home page shows the dates it was checked. When the game changes its address, sign-in options or help text, the affected section is checked again and its date changes only after the answer was confirmed.

Which game this guide is about

It covers PB Ranked at pbranked.com ↗, the real-time browser game from the Pokémon Battlegrounds animation channel. The channel website, Pokémon Apex Arena and Poké Arena 2D are different projects and are marked as such on the home page.

Send a correction

Send the page URL and what you saw through the contact page. A screenshot of the game screen helps us confirm the fix quickly.
