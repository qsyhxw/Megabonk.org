Check the status before choosing the follow-up hit

A combat reaction needs a status already on the target and a compatible later hit. This is different from combining moves at Bill’s Machine. Use the checker to identify a possible reaction, then verify that your kit can create its prerequisite.[1]

Make the prerequisite real

  1. Find a selected move or item that can apply the required status. An attack’s type alone is not proof of its status effect.
  2. Apply the status first. In the checked hit routine, reaction evaluation precedes the current hit’s own status roll.
  3. Use a compatible following hit while the status still exists. Boss resistance, status duration and consuming effects can change the opportunity.
  4. Inspect the resulting behavior in your run. The planner flags potential routes, not a guaranteed proc.
