Check the replacement before taking new jewelry

Rings, trinkets and amulets are in-run jewelry recorded in the Codex, separate from permanent Armory gear. Inspect the offer and any replacement warning before changing a build-defining amulet.[1]

Discovery is not permanent equipment

The current Codex displays 142 entries: nine general rings, fourteen general trinkets, sixteen general amulets and class-specific legendary spell amulets. This is the v0.130.0 menu count, not a future catalog guarantee. It records items offered on any hero; an undiscovered silhouette does not prove that an item is unavailable to your current run. Caches, elites and guardians can supply offers. Legendary spell amulets require the corresponding known spell.[1]

The decision before an amulet replacement

  1. Read whether the offer empowers the worn item or takes its slot.
  2. Compare the spell you are actually using with the spell altered by the proposed legendary amulet.
  3. If a confirmation warns that the worn amulet is lost, compare both displayed effects before accepting. You wear one amulet at a time.
  4. If your plan depends on a particular effect, keep it until the replacement solves the actual failure point; a rarer label alone is not the answer.

Current text includes rank-specific added targets. It also retains generic empowerment wording, so do not assume every old blanket “all numbers triple” summary describes every current spell amulet. Use the particular rank preview; we have not measured all rank formulas.[1]
