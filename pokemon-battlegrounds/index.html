Use the new address: pbranked.com

PB Ranked has a new address. On 22 November 2026 the old ranked.pokemonbattlegrounds.com address showed a notice that your account, rank, teams and settings come with you, and that the old address forwards to the new one on its own. Use pbranked.com directly.

Where to play: in a web browser at pbranked.com, on a computer or a phone. The official pages describe no download or install step.

If you see a gallery of animated battles or a Masters Eight ballot, you are on the channel website, not the PB Ranked client. The channel site is a different destination; see the table below.

Start with the right account and mode

Start with an Easy practice battle to learn your team without risking rating. You will need to sign in before choosing your Pokémon and entering practice.

  1. Open the game. Use the button at the top of this page; it opens pbranked.com in a new tab.
  2. Check whether you are returning. Existing players should sign in with the same Discord or Google account. New players are prompted to pick a trainer name of 2–16 characters and a trainer before signing in. Some trainer choices show rating or star requirements; do not assume every portrait is available immediately.
  3. Read the help before queuing. The top-bar ? opens How battles work even on the sign-in screen. Use the controller icon to inspect current key bindings.
  4. After sign-in, prepare your team, then choose Practice vs bot → Easy. For the inspected 3v3 format, Your team shows three slots. Use the Pokémon list to fill your team and click a team member to inspect its moves. The practice chooser offers Easy, Casual, Normal, Hard and Hardest, with no rating on the line. Read the selected moves and try Auto before switching to Manual. Use Find battle when you intend to seek an opponent; practice is a separate button, not the Ranked/Unranked selector.

If you cannot reach the team screen, use the original client’s help or choose another browser game. PB Ranked requires sign-in; a guest route has not been verified.

Ranked games and rating

The official terms say ratings may be reset at the start of a season. Friendly matches and tournaments say “No rating change.”

The time limit ends a battle with the team that has more HP left. Ranks, win streaks, mastery and stars are explained in the in-game Guide; this page does not copy those rules, and the season name and rank thresholds are not given on the official pages read on 23 November 2026.

Choose Auto, called moves or full Manual control

PB Ranked’s in-game help describes three control options. Try Auto first, then take over attacks or movement when you are ready.

Your choiceWhat the client says it doesWhat to do next
AutoYour Pokémon moves, dodges and chooses attacks itself. You can still call one of its four moves.Watch cooldown and range. A glowing move button means ready and in range; a called move waits for the current attack to finish.
Auto movement, your attacksTurning off Auto attacks for me keeps automatic movement and dodging, but attacks only when you call a move.Look under your trainer settings after sign-in. This is a different choice from full Manual.
ManualPress Auto to switch to Manual. You steer, call attacks and trigger Dodge yourself.The inspected defaults show WASD movement, 1–4 moves and Space dodge. Read Controls if you change bindings.

Controllers (Xbox, PlayStation, Switch Pro) can be plugged in; the client lists this under Keys and controller. The help shows Z/X/C for bench switches and M for Mega Evolve or Greninja’s Battle Bond, when applicable. The client lists controller support and recommends landscape orientation on phones. Neither was tested on a physical device in our check.

Leaving the tab is not a pause. The help says your team keeps fighting if you leave a match, and you lose if you are absent when it ends. Do not use tab switching as a pause strategy.

Keep your account separate from other fan games

The project’s privacy notice (updated 1 November 2026) says PB Ranked keeps your trainer, rating, win/loss record and matches on its server, while settings, teams and a sign-in code use your browser’s local storage. It describes Discord/Google sign-in as sharing an account ID, rather than giving the game your password.

Return using the same sign-in provider and account. Do not assume that PB Ranked, Poké Arena 2D, Apex Arena or PokeBonk share trainers, teams, saves or ratings. The project says ratings may reset at a season boundary and that the game can change or stop during development.

For account or game issues, use Report a problem in the original client, or PB Ranked developer email ↗. Do not send passwords or sign-in codes. Megabonk.org cannot recover an original-game account.

Common questions

What is PB Ranked?

PB Ranked is the real-time browser battle game made by the Pokémon Battlegrounds animation channel. You build a team, fight other trainers live and climb a ranked ladder.

Full answer →
Do I need an account to play PB Ranked?

Yes. The client asks you to sign in with Discord or Google before you pick a team or start a practice match. A guest route was not seen when the page was checked on 5 November 2026.

Full answer →
Did PB Ranked change its address?

The game is now at pbranked.com. The old ranked.pokemonbattlegrounds.com address shows a notice that your account, rank, teams and settings come with you and that it will forward to the new address.

Full answer →
Should I play Auto or Manual first?

Start with an Easy practice battle in Auto. The in-game help says Auto moves, dodges and chooses attacks for you, and you can still call one of the four moves. Switch to Manual when you want to steer and dodge yourself.

Full answer →
Is PB Ranked the same as Poké Arena 2D or Apex Arena?

No. They are separate projects with their own accounts, teams and controls. Apex Arena's creator describes it as a separate fan project inspired by the channel's videos.

Full answer →
Can I play PB Ranked on a phone?

Yes, in a phone browser. The client’s own help says to turn your phone sideways for a bigger battle. The official pages describe no download or install step, so this guide does not give one.

Full answer →
Does my rating reset?

The terms say ratings may be reset at the start of a season. Friendly matches and tournaments do not change rating. The season name and rank thresholds are not on the official pages this guide read, so none are listed.

Full answer →
When was PB Ranked released?

The official pages do not give a release date, so this guide does not state one. The dates here are the 5 November 2026 play check, the 22 November 2026 address change and the 23 November 2026 wording check.

Full answer →
Is PB Ranked free, and does it have ads?

Its terms describe a free, non-commercial fan game with no ads, purchases or donations. This guide page itself carries the site’s own advertising, which is separate from the game.
