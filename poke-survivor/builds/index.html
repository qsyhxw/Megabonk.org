PokéSurvivor builds: physical, special and bonds

Build around your partner’s stronger attack stat. A physical attacker wants physical moves plus Swords Dance or Dragon Dance; a special attacker wants special moves plus Nasty Plot. The level-up card tells you which side fits: “With Scorbunny it deals 84%: it hits harder with physical moves.”

PokéSurvivor guide · Updated November 16, 2026

Physical or special: the one rule that decides a build

Physical moves hit with your Pokémon’s Attack and special moves with its Special Attack. Moves on the strong side hit at 100%; moves on the other side hit in proportion, so a Pokémon with 120 Attack and 60 Special Attack deals half damage with special moves. A move’s type (Fire, Psychic…) is separate from its physical or special category.

Older videos that pair Swords Dance with Mewtwo’s Psychic or Confusion come from before the physical/special split (18 October 2026); in the current game only Nasty Plot raises those moves.

Bonds: two moves from one slot

Every legendary you beat in a rift gives a bond: link two moves so they fire together from a single slot, up to four bonds. Rifts open at 4, 8, 12 and 16 minutes; the minute-25 rift gives a bond only if you missed one, so the plan is to reach Eternatus with all four.

  • Bond your two strongest damaging moves of the same category first.
  • Use the freed slots for a third and fourth attack before adding a buff.
  • Win the rifts: losing sends you back to the map with no bond and no ticket. Rift arenas and rewards.

Evolve your main move

123 of the 176 moves can evolve. Raise the move to level 5, then take the upgrade card it asks for and a golden evolution card appears.

Upgrades and items that shape a build

  • Repertoire (1,500 PokéCoins): start with 2 moves; then Starting move and Second starting move (400 each) pick them.
  • Ban / Veto (from 300): block up to five moves you never want, such as every off-side move.
  • Card reroll (from 150): one more reroll per run, per level bought.
  • Held items: Choice Band gives +40% damage but locks your move; Quick Claw cuts cooldowns by 20%; Leftovers heals 1.5% HP per second. Second item (10,000) and Third item (30,000) let you hold more at once.
  • Taught the wrong TM? A Blank TM from the Kecleon Shop erases it.
