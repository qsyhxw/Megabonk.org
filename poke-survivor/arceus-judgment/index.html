PokéSurvivor Judgment of Arceus: how to last longer

At minute 30 Arceus takes you to the Hall of Origin. It cannot be beaten: your score is how long you last and how much damage you deal. It breaks one held item at 2, 4 and 6 minutes, ignores Protect, Sturdy and Focus Sash, and is faster than you after 3 minutes; lasting 10 minutes (about 40:00 of run time) is a top result.

PokéSurvivor guide · Updated November 16, 2026

Before minute 30

  • Beat Eternamax Eternatus at 20:00 for a power (Mega Evolution, Dynamax, Z-Ring or Terastal).
  • Collect every bit of experience, coins and heals on the ground before 30:00: it disappears when you travel.
  • Amulet Coin, Exp. Charm and Luck Incense still pay out at the end even if Arceus breaks them.
  • On Normal the Judgment is unchanged; only the earlier part of the run is easier.

Rules that end most Judgments

RuleDetail
SpeedMatches your speed after 1 minute, faster than you at 3, 140% of your speed at 10
TouchTakes 45% of your HP; from 10 minutes it knocks you out
ItemsBreaks one held item at 2, 4 and 6 minutes (and at 8 if you hold a fourth)
DamageIgnores types; damage reduction from Armor, items and upgrades cuts its attacks by at most 30% (only with the 80% maximum); Protect, Sturdy and Focus Sash do not work
FloorEvery 4 s a chunk cracks and falls, up to 30% of the floor; standing on it hurts
ChasingRun far and it appears in front of you; three jumps in 45 s break the move you flee with
Co-opThe first fall can be revived; a second fall means you are judged and out

Arceus holds a random Plate that sets its type for the Judgment, announced when you enter. The ranking counts both time survived and damage dealt. At Halloween the witch Gengar takes Arceus’s place. Builds for each side: physical and special builds.
