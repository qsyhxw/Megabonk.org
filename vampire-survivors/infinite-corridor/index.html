Infinite Corridor unlock and evolution route

Max Clock Lancet to level 7 and both Silver Ring and Gold Ring to level 9, then open an evolution-capable chest. Yellow Sign reveals the ring pickups. If a ring is missing or the chest gives another reward, follow the matching state below.

By Megabonk.org · checked 9 November 2026 · Vampire Survivors is by poncle.

Clock Lancet’s level-seven cap is the important exception to the common “level eight” shortcut. The three component levels are cross-checked against Nikola L’s GameSkinny guide ↗ and the Wiki Clock Lancet reference ↗.

If the rings are missing

Your first gate is Yellow Sign. If you do not own it, unlock Hyper mode on four normal stages to reach Moongolow. Complete the hidden-event route there with an eligible non-secret character, enter Holy Forbidden, follow the corridor and collect Yellow Sign beyond the apparent endpoint.

Adam Carr’s Gameranx route ↗ explains the sequence from Moongolow through Holy Forbidden. Once Yellow Sign is acquired, return to a suitable regular run and inspect the map for the two rings. Follow the icons shown on your selected stage because their directions are stage-dependent.

If you already own Yellow Sign, start your diagnosis at the map and inventory. Repeating Holy Forbidden is not the next step for that state.

Make the evolution during the run

  1. Take Clock Lancet and keep a damaging weapon alongside it. Clock Lancet’s freezing role does not supply the ordinary damage needed to clear waves.
  2. Plan a path to both ring icons. Build your basic damage and defenses before committing to a long trip through the map.
  3. Collect Silver Ring and Gold Ring, then level each to nine. Picking up a ring is only the start of its requirement.
  4. Finish Clock Lancet at seven. Check the pause inventory for all three maxima.
  5. Take an evolution-capable chest. Once the recipe is satisfied, it can award Infinite Corridor. If another eligible evolution takes the reward first, leave this set intact and take another qualifying chest.

For the general recipe and chest checks, return to why a weapon will not evolve. This is a source-based route checked on 9 November 2026. We have not performed a current full-run or Reaper-kill test.
