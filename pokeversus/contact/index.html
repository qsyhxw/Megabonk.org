Contact the PokéVersus guide editors

Reviewed 22 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A login, starter or hub step that no longer matches the current client;
  • A mode description, rule or limit that the mode list now words differently;
  • A save or account statement that your own experience contradicts, with what you did;
  • A broken link, image or layout on the PokéVersus page.

Include the page URL, the version shown in the client, whether you played as a guest or signed in, and your browser or operating system. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not the game’s author or support, so we cannot recover a lost account or guest save, read the storage of another website, change matchmaking or restore a banned account. For those, use the contact routes inside the original game.
