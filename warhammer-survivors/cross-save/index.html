WARHAMMER SURVIVORS · PLAYER GUIDE

Warhammer Survivors cross-save and progress transfer

Xbox Play Anywhere supports progress between the Xbox and Microsoft PC versions. For Steam, mobile, PlayStation and Nintendo combinations, a general cross-save connection has not been confirmed. Check the exact two versions before buying another copy.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 11 November 2026 · Release v2.1

Match the two storefronts first

The official Xbox product listing ↗ carries both Xbox Play Anywhere and Xbox cloud saves. Microsoft describes Play Anywhere as carrying saves and achievements between Xbox console, PC and supported handhelds. Together these establish the Microsoft-version route. Microsoft Play Anywhere rules ↗

Switch devices through the supported route

  1. Confirm that both installations are the Microsoft version, or that both belong to the same Steam account.
  2. Finish the session and exit the game on the first device. Keep it connected while its platform uploads the save.
  3. Open the second device with that same account and allow the platform to synchronize before starting a new run.
  4. If the expected progress is missing, check the account and storefront before creating more progress on the second device. Compare the platform cloud status and contact support with both device/store names.

This is a platform-based checking sequence. We have not performed a two-device save-transfer test for this game.

What the developer has said about broader transfers

In the October 27 AMA, the developer said it had nothing to announce about cross-save at that point. That answer is relevant to broader platform requests. The current Microsoft product listing provides a more specific, documented Xbox Play Anywhere route. Auroch Digital developer AMA

The Steam product lists Steam Cloud. That describes the Steam copy’s cloud capability. A purchase on another store still needs its own supported connection. Steam product features ↗
