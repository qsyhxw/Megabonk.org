About the Midgard Survivors guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 12 November 2026

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not nearQ or Pawgres Studios, who make Midgard Survivors, and we are not Gravity, which owns Ragnarok Online; nothing here is an official announcement.

What it covers

The section answers the questions players hit around a first install: which file to take for each device on the download and start page, how to fix a black screen or crash with the Android Vulkan and OpenGL APKs, how to open the alpha on a Mac in the damaged-app guide, how to keep and equip gear between runs in the equipment guide, and how rooms and accounts work in co-op.

It does not rank classes, publish a best-build list or give a safe refining threshold; the sources checked do not support those.

Where the answers come from

  • The developer’s own pages: the developer website ↗ (opens in a new tab) and the itch.io listing ↗ (opens in a new tab) supply the downloads, platform notes, controls and the Mac and Android installation guidance.
  • Developer replies in community threads: launch and devlog replies on the Ragnarok Online subreddit supply the damaged-app command, the equipment route and the co-op and cross-save status at launch.
  • The update manifest: the client version shown on the start page is read automatically from the developer’s public update manifest on GitHub.

Screenshots are developer-distributed gameplay images that we downloaded and looked at. We have not run the clients ourselves, tested phones, completed a full run or played co-op, and the pages do not claim to.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was checked, currently 12 November 2026. When the developer ships a new client, the pages that depend on its menus, platform files or save rules are rechecked first. The version number on the start page updates automatically; the instructions on each page do not, until we have reread them.

Which game is this?

Searches for the name also surface Tastygraph’s Norse-themed game and RO Classic+’s Midgard Survival. This guide covers nearQ’s game only, and does not apply those projects’ mechanics.

Found something wrong?

Send the page URL, your device and client version, and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or the developer’s own statement are usually applied in the next update.
