Route 1-1: how to beat Shaymin

Keep damage on Shaymin after Natural Cure removes your status effects. If its health climbs between attacks, move a sustained attacker into range of a longer stretch of the path.

Choose your tiles before the late waves

The opening map has seven field, seven grass, five water and just one mountain tile. Use X on desktop to show ranges before committing that mountain spot. Wave 24 sends 30 Doduo and wave 36 sends 50. If the last birds escape while the first fall quickly, try a second firing window or bouncing attacks before spending on single-target power.

Shaymin at wave 100

Wave 99 mixes Trevenant and Chansey with Illumise and Volbeat. Wave 100 sends Shaymin alone. Its 120,000 HP regenerate at 1,000 per second. Watch what happens when your lead attacker loses range. A short burst followed by an empty stretch gives that regeneration time to undo your work.

Natural Cure periodically clears status conditions. A Burn starter can still help, but check whether direct hits keep driving the health bar down after the effect disappears. If you already clear wave 99 reliably, keep that lineup and adjust one attacker's position for the final fight.

How to recruit Shaymin

To recruit Shaymin, defeat it with your attacking Pokémon unequipped. The Lopunny line is exempt. For a first clear, equipment is available unless you have separately enabled a challenge that restricts it.

For other collection requirements see hidden Pokémon unlocks. For roster substitutions use the team role guide.

Replay a cleared wave with the Bicycle

Segmental’s Bicycle costs 1,000,000,000 in the shop. Before buying it for a particular route, check that you have beaten its wave-100 boss. On a cleared map, the Wave Manager appears beside your team at the lower left. Choose the wave there, or use R beside the manager to repeat the current wave. Owning the Bicycle alone does not skip an uncleared boss.

Bicycle shop entry ↗ (new tab) · Wave Manager instructions ↗ (new tab)

For gold farming, give Meowth ↗ (new tab) or Persian a field or grass tile where it can hit repeatedly. Greed pays 10% of the target’s gold per hit. Compare two repeatable waves by recording your balance before and after the same number of minutes. Include time spent restarting a failed attempt. The better wave for your roster is the one that actually earns more per minute.
