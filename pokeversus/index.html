From the login window to your first match

These steps follow the original v2.37 client inspected on 5 November 2026, entered as a guest. This is a startup and menu check — no full or ranked match is claimed.

  1. Click Play above and wait for the login window: Sign in with Google, Sign in with email, or Play as guest. The window states that an account keeps progress in the cloud and works on any device, while a guest saves only in this browser.
  2. Choose a trainer name — the game says it must be unique.
  3. Pick your 3 starter Pokémon, one of each type: Grass, Fire and Water offer nine starters each (27 total, from Bulbasaur, Charmander and Squirtle to Sprigatito, Fuecoco and Quaxly). All three evolve during matches.
  4. You land in the hub: your lead Pokémon stands between two Invite slots, and the side menu lists Pokémon, Missions, Club (clan wars), Gachas, Achievements, Friends, Ranking and Chat. A season Pass sat above the mode selector at the time of checking (Season 2 on 5 November).
  5. The mode panel shows what is live right now — it defaulted to Random, which pools everyone into one mode every 5 minutes and excludes Toxic Fog and the duel sizes. Tap it for the full list below.
ModeWhat the client says it is
Online / Vs botsLive matches, or a bot practice lobby.
RandomEvery 5 minutes all queued players go to the same mode so lobbies fill; Toxic Fog and 2v2/1v1 duels are excluded.
Catch the Balls3v3: take 10 Poké Balls from the central machine and hold them for 15 seconds.
Toxic FogFree-for-all for 10 players (Solo or Duo): break crates for Rare Candies while the toxic fog closes in; last one standing wins.
Wake Up Snorlax3v3: each team protects its sleeping Snorlax; first team to knock out the enemy Snorlax wins.
Voltorb Ball3v3: carry the Voltorb into the enemy goal, first to 2 scores wins — it explodes if you hold it more than 10 seconds.
Territory3v3: stay in the central zone with no enemies inside; first team to 100% wins.
Team Duel3v3 (the panel also lists 2v2 and 1v1): most knockouts in 2.5 minutes, or first to 15.
Knockout3v3 with no respawns; knock out all three rivals to take the round, best of 3.
Gigantamax RaidWeekends only — the panel said “Come back on Saturday!” on a Wednesday check.

Controls are advertised as WASD + mouse on desktop, touch controls with a landscape prompt on phones, and gamepad mapping in settings. We inspected menus at desktop and phone widths; this is not a physical-phone or controller benchmark. (PokéVersus.net · independent guide ↗)

What is saved where

The login window is explicit: guest progress is saved only in this browser, so keep the same host and browser profile and do not clear site data mid-run. With an account (Google or email), progress is stored in the cloud and the game advertises playing on any device.

Friends, Club (clans), Rankings and the season Pass are account features advertised in the hub; the header also links the project's Discord server. We have not tested account creation, clan wars, matchmaking or cross-device saves ourselves. If the framed window stalls, open the original in its own tab before troubleshooting anything else — an outage on the original site cannot be fixed from this page.
