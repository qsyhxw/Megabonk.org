Brotato Veteran Build — Ranged Route and Healing Checks

Start with a coherent ranged plan and read healing restrictions before buying sustain. A launch-week player recommends Machine Gun with flat Ranged Damage, Range and piercing. Treat it as a reported route.

Megabonk.org editors · Checked 11 November 2026

Support the gun you can use

The jldark89 report recommends Machine Gun with ranged scaling and piercing for crowds. Its author plays Nightmare with wave retries enabled. That context matters when judging how forgiving the route is.

Buy for the next problem. Groups reaching you call for crowd coverage and attack uptime. An elite living too long calls for inspecting weapon scaling and time spent firing. The advice here is editorial interpretation of that reported route.

Check restrictions before buying recovery

The official launch post presents Veteran as a swarm-fighting character without time to heal. Read the exact current character sheet before buying HP regeneration, Life Steal or between-wave recovery. A bonus to a blocked recovery method may offer little value.

Check any missing-health bonus on your installed version before intentionally fighting hurt. Leave enough health to survive an unexpected hit. We do not reproduce unverified numerical traits from launch-week summary sites.

A Veteran shop checklist

  1. Concentrate weapons around the damage stat you are buying.
  2. Check whether crowds die before reaching you.
  3. Preserve a retreat lane with movement and defense.
  4. Read restrictions before buying recovery.
  5. Record map, difficulty, branch and active Item Sets when comparing builds.

Choose Evolution after the core works

A weapon-focused route gives a reason to inspect Mecha reinforcement, while Mutation develops passives. On optional beta-1, mechanization grants +3% weapon damage per mecha power point and supplies no extra piercing or bounces. Check crowd coverage before investing in reinforcement. Use the Evolution guide against your current tooltips.
