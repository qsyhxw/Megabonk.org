Choose bonuses that affect your actual kit

Check the move’s damage class and type before choosing its bonus. Protein affects Physical damage; Calcium affects Special damage. A type item and a general damage multiplier can cover different parts of the same kit. The tables expose static modifiers and caps; conditional effects need their own card.[1][2]

Checked October 26, 2026 · Beta v0.8.55 · Source-based guide

Check your version first

Version boundary: this guide covers the original pokebonk.com v0.8.55, checked October 26. Our Play window uses a separate third-party v0.8.27 source with separate progress. Three starter examples, with the decision explained

KitOffer to inspectWhy / limit
BulbasaurProtein for Vine Whip/Razor Leaf; Calcium for damaging Special moves; Miracle Seed for Grass movesA mixed kit means one damage-class vitamin does not cover every attack. Sleep Powder’s status role is a separate benefit.
CharmanderCharcoal: Fire damage bonus plus general modifier; inspect each Fire move’s classSame type does not establish identical shape, hit frequency or scaling.
SquirtleMystic Water for Water moves, defensive choices when contact is the problemRapid Spin is Normal, so a Water-only part does not cover it. Armor/shield and offense solve different problems.

These are mechanics-based offer decisions, not a guaranteed item sequence. Discovery, unlocking into the pool and archiving are separate states; a searchable record does not guarantee it can drop in your run.

126-item static-stat reference

Numeric modifiers come from each public item record; tags expose explicit type relationships. Default stack cap is ten unless the record overrides it. Additive fractions are shown as raw stat units, not blindly relabeled as percentages. “No static modifier” does not mean no effect. Timed, trigger and special scripts are outside this table’s static field; inspect the game card for their complete effect.[1][2]

Slots, stacks and caps are different numbers

NumberNormal checked limitException / meaning
Equipped move slotsThree before the permanent fourth-slot purchaseA fusion occupies one slot after consuming two moves.
Ordinary move levelsEightTM Case raises the cap to ten; fusion readiness still tests eight.
Fusion starsFiveTM Case allows six.
Vitamin levelsTenMax Ether adds two; the number of equipped vitamin types is a different slot limit.
Copies of one itemTen by defaultPer-item caps override the default; Awakening 3, Flame Orb 5, TM Case 1 and Max Ether 1.

Do not multiply a listed proc chance by stack count and publish the result as a verified probability. Static modifiers, trigger scripts and caps are separate pieces of the mechanic.
