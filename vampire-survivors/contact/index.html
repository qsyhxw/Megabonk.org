Contact the Vampire Survivors guide editors

Reviewed 10 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A recipe, passive level or unlock condition that changed in a newer game update;
  • A DLC pack, price or free-pack listing that no longer matches the store;
  • A co-op or storefront pairing that works differently from the co-op page;
  • A wrong screenshot, link or character name on a Vampire Survivors page.

Include the page URL, your platform, the game version and what happened. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not the developer or the game's support team, so we cannot restore a save, fix a store purchase or change how the game works. Use the developer's support pages ↗ (opens in a new tab) or your platform's support for account and purchase problems.
