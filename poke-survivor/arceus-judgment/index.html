Survival and damage after minute 30

At minute 30, the original game enters Judgment of Arceus. Hitting Arceus for a damage score is different from defeating it. Choose whether you are pursuing survival time or damage, and recheck old advice that assumes endless rifts, doubled legendary scaling or a permanent held item.[1]

By Megabonk.org editors · Checked October 26, 2026 · Source-based; no comparative gameplay test

Three boundaries that change the plan

Run timeChecked rulePractical consequence
28:00New rifts stop openingDo not wait for another portal as your remaining Bond plan.
30:00Judgment transition; waits for an active arena/card choice to finishFinishing the current choice is different from avoiding the phase.
35:00Arceus breaks held items, including Focus SashA held-item-dependent plan is not permanent protection.

These boundaries come from the fixed source. We have not completed a run through them. A native port can explicitly change the Arceus outcome or rewards, so identify the edition first.[1]

Pick the score you are trying to improve

The source tracks damage dealt to Arceus separately from survival. It allows attacks to register damage for scoring while Arceus remains invincible in this encounter. “You can hit it” must not become “you can kill it for a reward.”[1]

GoalRead before changing the buildLimit
Survive longerMovement soft cap, contact damage, item break and incoming warningsMore speed on the ordinary map does not scale identically in Judgment.
Deal more damageCurrent Plate/type, actual physical/special fit and target selectionBase attack alone does not establish a best damage build.
Beat new legendariesTheir increasing HP/damage and the time they enterJudgment legendaries do not carry the normal rift reward assumptions.

This is a plan-reading method, not a tested strongest route. Keep the goal and the same-version conditions with any run comparison.

Why older clips and guides can disagree

The checked implementation adds a legendary every five seconds and uses successive HP ×1.25 and damage ×1.15, rather than the older doubling description. It applies a movement-speed soft cap and later increases contact danger. The source also reduces the damage registered on Arceus; a raw move number is not your final score.[1]

Read each rule at its own scope. A main-map poison or burn strategy cannot be assumed to keep the same boss-health scaling in Judgment, and a port that says Arceus can be defeated is describing different code.

What the player question actually asks

Daraen41 asked why a run that had previously lasted much longer suddenly entered the “hold out” Arceus phase at half an hour. Visible replies discuss very different survival times and setups. Those reports establish confusion about the phase, not a validated best build or an average survival target.[2]

DylanZM separately reported FPS dropping when Arceus appears in original-project issue #347 on October 26. If the problem is frame rate or frozen input, use the symptom checklist rather than assuming a change of Pokémon solves it.[3]

Before claiming a route works

  • Record the exact game address/version and whether the run is solo or co-op.
  • Keep permanent upgrades, nature/ability and held item with the move list.
  • Separate time survived from damage scored; keep the selected Plate/type if comparing damage.
  • Do not label a theorycraft, source reading or another player’s clip as this site’s completed run.

Rules below refer to JPTER78’s source snapshot 680c3e12c670, reviewed October 26, 2026. Other hosted editions and native ports can differ. We have not completed a comparative run or verified a universal fix. No infinite-survival, kill or high-score guarantee is made.
