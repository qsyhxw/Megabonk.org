Plan your Lavender Tower climb

Lavender Tower Night runs from October 21, 2026 at 16:30 UTC to November 4 at 17:30 UTC in the checked event definition. Open its event tile in the original menu, clear the seven-floor route, and inspect the Book of Candles for rewards. Beyond and reward claiming are separate steps.[2]

Checked October 26, 2026 · Beta v0.8.55 · Source-based guide

Check your version first

Version boundary: this guide covers the original pokebonk.com v0.8.55, checked October 26. Our Play window uses a separate third-party v0.8.27 source with separate progress. Newer co-op, Treasures and Archive changes may be absent there. Mechanics are SINGLE_SOURCE; no full-run, reward-claim or multiplayer completion test is claimed.

[1][2]

Play window versus claim window

Checked October 26: the public status marked the event live and claimable.[3] Scheduled end: November 4, 17:30 UTC (November 5, 01:30 China Standard Time). The definition allows 14 further days for claims, through November 18, 17:30 UTC if the server schedule is unchanged. A claim window does not mean new event runs remain open. Follow the live event screen if the server overrides the schedule.

Use this route checklist, not a promised clear

  1. Open the original menu’s event tile and read the currently available mode and your chosen partner.
  2. Clear the current floor’s required waves before trying its stairs. A timer alone does not prove the floor is clear.
  3. For unidentified ghosts, use the event’s Silph Scope reveal mechanic and inspect the relevant reveal task. Ordinary damage and revealed-ghost progress are separate.
  4. On floor 6, the named task is calming Marowak’s ghost; on floor 7 it is defeating Gengar.
  5. After the main route, Beyond has floor milestones at 15 and 25. Do not confuse reaching those floors with the seven-floor clear.
  6. After accepted results, inspect both Candle-track rewards and individual task claims.

We have not completed this climb or measured a safe build. The October 23–25 notes describe several stairs, unfinished-floor and trapped-boss corrections. If stuck, compare the version, remaining enemies and stairs prompt before assuming a clear was recorded.

If a reward is missing

Keep the same original account; compare the track threshold, the task counter, and the individual Claim state. For a leaderboard cosmetic, the rank and settlement timing are additional conditions; a current rank is not a settled award. Give support the version, account display name, run end screen and reward card, never your password.
