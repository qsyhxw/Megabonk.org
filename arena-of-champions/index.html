From the window to the arena

  1. Click Play above and wait for the original game’s sign-in screen. A loaded window does not mean the game has finished downloading its resources.
  2. Choose Play as Guest to begin without entering account details. Read the current hero and ability choices in the game.
  3. Before joining a match, open the game’s Controls and Key Bindings settings. Practice targeting and abilities, then use its current queue interface for your preferred bracket.

The original client lists 1v1, 2v2 and 3v3 queues. This is not a promise of available opponents, a particular ping or a completed match on every device.[1] Expand gives you more room. Hide and Return keep the same frame and do not pause or disconnect it. Leave a match in the game before stepping away; close this page to end its embedded session.

Choose browser or Windows before moving progress

Browser play needs no separate game installation. If you prefer a desktop client, open the original game and use its Download for Windows control; obtain updates from that project rather than a lookalike download page.[1]

Already playing as a guest? The project’s text says to use Save Progress in town before moving to the app, then sign in with that account. These are the project’s instructions; we have not tested account migration, Windows compatibility or recovery of lost progress. Do not clear your guest browser data while deciding. A framed session and a separate browser tab may receive different storage treatment.

If the game does not start

First distinguish the outer window loading from the game’s own resource download or connection message. If it stalls, Arena of Champions · open the same original in its own tab ↗. Record the exact message and whether you reached sign-in or town. Conflicting player access reports do not establish that the game has shut down.[2]

The game has keyboard and mouse controls. A phone-sized page is not proof of touch support; use a desktop or laptop for this route. We do not promise that the Windows app fixes your latency or that every browser and graphics adapter works.

For project announcements and support, follow the community control inside the current original game. Invites can change. This window connects to the game host only when you click Play; that host controls its own services, accounts and storage. See our privacy notice.
