Use templates without mistaking them for damage forecasts

Damage and cooldown are level-one runtime-template values before partner stats, upgrades, hit count, target defenses or actual hit timing. They are not DPS. Runtime class can differ from the underlying Pokémon reference: a move labelled Status there can execute as Special here. The class filter uses the runtime class; the Applied status filter searches the actual template effect.

A status chance belongs to the template; target rules, later modifiers and reaction order still matter. “None” means no status field in this template, not that every related passive is absent.

[1]

From an unfamiliar move to a usable draft

  1. Search a name or choose a required status.
  2. Open a listed partner to inspect its unlocks, passive and remaining kit.
  3. Use Plan beside that partner to load the move into slot one.
  4. Check the second ingredient in the fusion finder and the required Lab capacity.

Listed partners come from the current League kit definitions, not main-series learnsets. Additional learning through a special passive or Daily Mutation is outside this membership list.

[2]

Inspect a level and its recorded card effects

The lookup starts from neutral level-1 templates. Select a move here to inspect later levels with explicitly recorded upgrade effects; use the planner for your selected partner’s modifiers.

Cards contain two distinct effects, or three at Epic/Legendary, bounded by the move archetype’s available effects. These controls describe possible cards; they cannot guarantee an offer. Missing effects are not guessed. Random, weather and buff moves need custom runtime interpretation.
