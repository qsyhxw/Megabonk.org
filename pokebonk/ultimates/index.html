Before you rely on an Ultimate

The default desktop action is R; Y toggles automatic casting. Check Settings if you remapped the controls, and wait for the ready indicator. Auto casting also checks for a nearby enemy, rather than promising an immediate cast anywhere.

While no Ultimate is active, the checked client replenishes charge over time. Kills and received damage also add charge; ability, item and Lab effects can change its rate. Deliberately taking damage is not a safe charging strategy. The client does not simply charge the meter from every point of outgoing damage.

Names and effects are ordered as the client lists them. Multi-stage entries change with form progression; they are not three independent moves occupying your move slots. A single named entry is not evidence that its strength is identical at every player level.

[1]

Match the effect to the problem

Read effects for the situation you need: an area clear, control, movement or recovery. Do not infer sustained DPS from a dramatic description. Return to the partner’s passive and move templates before changing the rest of the build.

Ultimate charging, enemy overlap and player level are not simulated by our planner. Record what actually happens in your run before treating a practice draft as a recommendation.
