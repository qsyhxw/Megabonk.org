A few things worth knowing

Is this the original game website?

No. This is the PokeBonk guide section of Megabonk.org. The original browser game is at pokebonk.com.

Is it an official Pokémon release?

No. The project identifies itself as an unofficial, non-commercial fan game.[3]

Is PokeSurvivor another name for it?

No. JPTER78’s PokéSurvivor at pokesurvivor.com is a different browser project. PokeBonk is Charcher’s game at pokebonk.com; its controls, progression and accounts must be checked separately. Find the PokéSurvivor game and player guides → The original author identifies that project and domain (checked October 26, 2026).[4]

AI and asset credits

Was PokeBonk made with AI?

Checked October 28, 2026: the author’s legal notice attributes scenery, 3D assets, effects, interface icons and music to image/audio generation tools and Blender. It separately credits community Pokémon sprites. That statement describes asset provenance; it does not establish which proportion of the code used AI or certify the safety of other websites. [3]
