Choose a pack by its setting and progression style, then check ownership in your game’s store. The Steam catalogue checked on 10 November 2026 lists six paid gameplay packs and two free packs. The soundtrack is a separate purchase.

This is a theme-based choice guide, not a tested power ranking. Free status is specific to the checked Steam listings. Prices, bundles and entitlements in other stores need their own checkout check.

Pick one branch before adding more

  • Still learning the base game? Continue the main-stage and relic roadmap first if the expanded Unlocks list feels crowded. This is a pacing suggestion.
  • Here specifically for Castlevania? Choose that DLC and use the entry and gate checkpoints. Richter and later content have their own unlock requirements.
  • Already own several packs? Choose one pack’s unmet objectives for the next few runs. A purchase order and an in-game unlock order solve different problems.

Check the edition before paying

  1. Open the store used by your installed full-game copy.
  2. Check the DLC product name, base-game requirement and owned status.
  3. Inspect bundle contents individually. A bundle containing the soundtrack does not turn it into gameplay content.
  4. For online play, separately check both players’ device and storefront compatibility.

The official Steam pages list Vampire Survivors as the base-game requirement. That does not establish cross-store ownership, mobile subscription entitlements or every platform’s content parity. The historical roster image illustrates Castlevania, not a universal roster included with every pack.
