Revenant Survivors Hard Mode: unlocks, Hard 0–10 and rewards

Since update 0.6.4 (16 November 2026) there are no Tiers: clear Worlds 1–7 in order, and clearing World 7 unlocks Hard 0 in every world. Clearing a Hard level in any world unlocks the next one in all worlds, up to Hard 10. Higher Hard levels drop more rewards and more Mythic gear; at the same level every world has the same difficulty and rewards but drops different gear.

How worlds and Hard levels unlock

You clearYou unlock
World 1–6The next world (a random map each time you enter)
World 7Hard 0 in every world
Hard N in any worldHard N+1 in all worlds, up to Hard 10

A notification appears in town when a new Hard level unlocks. Progress from before 0.6.4 was converted automatically: worlds and Hard levels you had already unlocked stay available, and achievements that asked for Tiers now use world progression. Update 0.6.4 announcement

What Hard Mode drops

  • Mythic gear, the rarity above Legendary: final bosses have a higher chance at it, and at high Hard levels a Mythic drop is guaranteed. Magic Find raises the Mythic chance too.
  • Mythic Essence from every Hard Mode enemy, more from final bosses. It is the currency for the Forge’s Mythic options.
  • Better rarity odds at each higher level, and a guaranteed gear drop from every mid-boss.
  • More rewards per level: Hard 10 pays more than Hard 0 at the cost of harder enemies, 8 extra Hard-only curses (such as Elite Discipline and Mini-Boss Raid) and healing orbs that restore less.

Hard Mode final bosses have less health than before 0.6.4. Update 0.6.4 announcement · Major Update 0.6.3 announcement

Which world to farm

  1. Still clearing Worlds 1–7? Push the next world first: World 7 is the only route to Hard Mode, and later worlds already drop better gear.
  2. Hunting a Mythic set? Each of Worlds 1–6 drops its own Mythic set with 3-, 6- and 9-piece bonuses; World 7 drops Mythic gear outside the sets. Check the gear-drop preview on stage selection, then farm that world at the highest Hard level you can clear.
  3. Want the most rewards? Difficulty and rewards are identical across worlds at the same Hard level, so pick the world whose boss you clear fastest.

Older Hard Mode advice

Guides written before 16 November describe Tiers and a Hard Mode clear as the gate to World 5. Update 0.6.3 (13 November) expanded Hard Mode to 11 levels, Hard 0 to Hard 10; update 0.6.4 removed Tiers and moved the gate to World 7. Earlier notes about Tier 2–4 boss health from hotfix 0.6.2.1 no longer apply.
