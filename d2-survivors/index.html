Start with the screen in front of you

The checked original menu displayed build 20261102.2 and a class selector. Current reference pages also describe a camp and waypoint. Our beginner guide gives both branches, so you do not hunt an older menu for a newer control. (D2 Survivors · original reference ↗)

Fix the decision before replacing a build

Trying to stop one Druid cast? Resting and re-drafting are different operations. Comparing two speed items? IAS and FCR share a system here. These pages answer specific player questions; they are not a universal best-build list.

Browser, desktop and phones

The original offers browser and desktop routes. Its setup warns that temporary hosted files are going away; its FAQ currently recommends a computer. Older phone reports can describe a different transition state. A mobile app request, a home-screen shortcut and a verified native app are different things. Choose the supported setup path.

Hardcore and a ladder are listed by the original, but neither establishes co-op. Follow the game’s current mode descriptions rather than treating a leaderboard as multiplayer.

Other games, separate progress

Azeroth Survivors brings a Warcraft-inspired survival loop; Soulstone Survivors has its own skill-chain decisions; PokeBonk starts with Pokémon partners. Their developers, rules and saves are separate.
