About the game

Ball x Pit mixes brick-breaking with a survival roguelite and a town you rebuild between runs. It is made by Kenny Sun and Friends and published by Devolver Digital. Steam lists Windows and macOS; the official site also lists Switch, Switch 2, Xbox, PlayStation, App Store and Google Play.

These guides cover the game itself, not an online ball-pit toy or a Megabonk mode. Prices and platform lists change, so check the store page for your region: Steam ↗ · Official site ↗.

How we check

Recipes come from attributed community charts and the official update notes, checked against each other. We have not benchmarked builds or played every route. Each guide shows its checked date.

Common questions

Is Ball x Pit still getting free updates?

Steam lists the Naturalist update (6 Aug 2026) as the latest free update, after Regal and Shadow. Risen Ballbylon is a separate paid expansion. Our recipe tools cover the base game and the free updates, not the expansion yet. New evolutions →

How do I make an evolution?

Carry the listed ingredients, upgrade them fully, then choose Evolution at a fusion core. A fused ball with a generic name is not the named evolution. Evolution finder →

Which character should I start with?

The Warrior is the starting character, and a good one for learning pickups and the town loop. Later picks depend on what you have unlocked and who you pair them with. Character picks →

Is Ball x Pit on mobile and consoles?

Yes. Steam lists Windows and macOS, and the official site lists Switch, Switch 2, Xbox, PlayStation, App Store and Google Play. Save transfer between platforms is not promised here. Beginner's guide →

Looking for a key or the controls?

For a purchase key use the official store listing for your platform and check region and edition. For controls, follow your version's on-screen prompts and the beginner notes. This site does not sell keys or host a playable copy. Controls notes →

How long is a run through the game?

Separate a first clear from full trophy completion and Endless play. Dayngls estimates 25–30 hours for the base-game trophy route; that is a route estimate, not a first-clear time. Trophy checklist →

Which game name am I looking for?

球比伦战记 is the Simplified Chinese name on Steam, and ボールピット appears in Japanese searches. The guides here are in English and use Burn, Freeze, Dark and Egg Sac consistently. Recipe names →
