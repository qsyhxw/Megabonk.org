Fire: critical hits into kill explosions

Choose this when Fire damage and critical-hit interactions are already available. Ignite and Hot Streak support the route; Pyroclasm changes the starter to Pyroblast. A new Fireball Mage does not yet have that endpoint.[1]

  1. Start with the Fire spell actually on the bar. With Pyroclasm allocated, support the Pyroblast opening.
  2. Prioritise Fire damage and critical-hit support that feeds the allocated talents. Do not add Frost solely to fill a slot.
  3. Use another Fire spell offered by the client when it supports the same plan. A specific card is not guaranteed.
  4. If crowds clear but a boss stalls, compare repeatable boss hits; explosions requiring kills need nearby victims.

[1]

Frost: control first, elemental when unlocked

Our editorial starting direction for a player wanting more space to move. Summon Water Elemental changes Fireball to Frostbolt and grants an elemental plus a periodic free Frost Nova. Until it is allocated, those effects are unavailable.[1]

  1. Before the capstone, keep the Fireball opening functional and take usable Frost control when offered.
  2. With the elemental allocated, support Frostbolt and the available Frost interactions. Read Frostbite and Splitting Ice in the tree.
  3. Compare health or coverage when contact is the failure. Control buys room; it is not a guarantee of safety.
  4. If the elemental is missing, inspect the allocation and starter before treating it as a broken summon.

[1]

Arcane: repeated spell strikes

Choose Arcane when you want to develop Arcane Missiles and repeat-strike interactions. Arcane Power changes the starter to Arcane Missiles; Presence of Mind changes its missiles, while Arcane Concentration adds a free Arcane Explosion interaction.[1]

  1. Before Arcane Power, use the actual Fireball starter; take Arcane Missiles when offered if committing to this route.
  2. Support Missiles and the milestones already allocated. More missile effects and a chance to strike again are different benefits.
  3. After Arcane Power changes the opener, draft for the Arcane engine rather than waiting for a Fire starter.
  4. If the planned card never appears, strengthen an attack you have or take needed survival; a distant milestone cannot deal present damage.

[1]

The current Arcane Power tooltip adds a bounded cascade: a spell that strikes twice can roll to strike again, up to three further strikes in a row. Arcane Reach and the capstone are not an unlimited repeat loop. The amount of damage gained has not been benchmarked here.[1]

What to prioritise during the run

  1. If you are still learning movement, choose a route that gives you room to deliver damage. A higher theoretical ceiling is useless when contact ends the run.
  2. For Fire, establish Fire damage and critical interactions before expecting Pyroclasm to carry dense waves.
  3. For Frost, distinguish ordinary Frostbolt upgrades from the summon and free-nova effects granted by the capstone.
  4. After the chosen engine works, use the current failure point to choose health, control or damage. Do not draft three competing schools without a reason.

This is an editorial choice order, not a guaranteed card sequence. The offered upgrades and your current permanent investment still determine which step is available. [1]

When the intended build is not ready

Before a capstone changes the opener, use the default Fireball start in your plan. Against a boss without nearby enemies, evaluate sustained casts rather than wave-chain explosions. A missing offensive offer can be a reason to take survival, not to abandon every existing synergy.

Before changing the whole plan, identify the failure: no room to move, too little recovery, or too little damage on the target. Change the part that addresses that failure, then compare another run with similar talents and equipment.

Check the starter and trigger before copying a build

Baseline starter: Fireball. Talents can replace it; use the route conditions above rather than treating this as the opening for every developed character. [1]

A Frostbolt opening can be correct for a Water Elemental character and wrong as a universal statement about every Mage. Always record the selected talents when comparing runs.

Record the game version, zone, difficulty, selected capstone, permanent equipment and the card that changed the run. Comparing two different progression states cannot establish that a single spell was better. Use the meter carefully →

Mage build questions

Which Mage build is best for a beginner?

Frost is our control-oriented starting direction. A new Mage still starts with Fireball; build toward Frost without assuming an unallocated elemental already exists.

Should I take every spell school?

Choose offers that support the active route or fix a survival problem. An unrelated spell can consume a choice without strengthening the intended interaction.

Where should the remaining talent points go?

Check earned points and legal prerequisites in the game, then address the observed failure. These route plans do not claim a verified optimal 51-point allocation. Read the point-budget checks →
