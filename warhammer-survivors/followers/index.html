How to get two followers in Warhammer Survivors

On the 40K side, try Malum Caedo with the unlocked Lasgun and Shield of Faith. These weapons bring their users into your squad. The launch item guide reports that having two followers unlocks Bugman’s Tankard. Keep the two-follower objective separate from the later five-follower target.

Prepare the two weapon choices

  1. Rescue Trooper Bartosz Kozlowski on Imperial Bastion and unlock his Lasgun for future runs.
  2. Unlock Sister Superior Amalia Novena. The character guide lists a 400-coin purchase.
  3. The item guide reports unlocking Shield of Faith by surviving five minutes with Amalia. Check the associated Objective after that run.
  4. Start a new 40K run as Malum Caedo. Take Lasgun and Shield of Faith when they are offered.

Those prerequisites combine the launch character, item and follower guides. Each requirement applies to its named task. Character unlock report · Item unlock report

Check the two-follower objective

The reported route recruits Kozlowski through Lasgun and Amalia through Shield of Faith. Check your follower count after taking both. If one weapon has not appeared, check its unlock Objective first. A completed unlock makes the item eligible for future choices rather than guaranteeing the next offered selection.

Bugman’s Tankard is listed against the requirement to have two followers. Check that objective in your selected universe after reaching the count. The reports do not establish that this 40K route completes the other universe’s objective. Bugman’s Tankard unlock report

Extend the route toward five followers

Destructoid’s follower guide describes the same Malum Caedo loadout and upgrading Lasgun to level 6. It reports that Kozlowski’s additional soldiers, together with Amalia, complete the five-follower target and unlock Figure Case. Use the displayed objective count to distinguish the initial two recruits from the later expanded squad. Reported five-follower route, 10 November

This is a published route, with no full-run test by this site. If the displayed counter differs, record your build, universe, weapon ranks and unlocked objectives before comparing advice for another version.

Use the unlocked companion in a recipe

Bugman’s Tankard is reported beside Shield of Faith in 40K and Nebulous Sea Fog in Age of Sigmar. Use the evolution table for your selected universe.

For human player count, open the co-op guide. For missing Dice Cube, use the universe-specific unlock checks.
