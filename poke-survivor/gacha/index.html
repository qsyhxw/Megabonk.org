Check tickets before choosing a pull

A ten-pull needs 10 ordinary tickets or one ×10 ticket; a hundred-pull needs 100 ordinary tickets or ten ×10 tickets. Check the selected button and balance first. A rarity guarantee does not guarantee the particular Pokémon you want.[1]

By Megabonk.org editors · Checked October 26, 2026 · Source-based; no comparative gameplay test

Four buttons, four eligibility checks

Chosen pullOrdinary-ticket button×10-ticket button
10 results10 ordinary tickets1 ×10 ticket
100 results100 ordinary tickets10 ×10 tickets

These costs are read from costOf and the matching interface buttons, not inferred from an icon. The two balances are checked separately: do not add “4 ordinary + 1 ×10” and assume any button accepts that combined total.[2]

Worked checks: 9 ordinary tickets alone do not qualify for the ordinary ten-pull button. One ×10 ticket qualifies for its ten-pull button, not for the hundred-pull button. Ten ×10 tickets meet the latter cost. This arithmetic establishes source-defined eligibility, not that every reported disabled button is user error.

What the guarantees do and do not promise

The checked source has separate pity per regional banner. A ten-pull guarantees at least an Epic-or-better result; the five-star counter governs rarity, not a named species. In the checked pull loop, the counter increments first and requests five-star rarity when it reaches 70. That is the 70th pull since the last five-star if none appeared earlier, not a promise about a particular species. The source comment’s “next pull” wording is less precise than this executable condition.[1]

Switching region does not mean one shared global pity counter. A five-star result is not proof it must be shiny, and a shiny roll is separate from rarity. A hundred-pull is handled as ten ten-pull batches in the reviewed code; it is not a new guarantee of a specific legendary.

When the balance and result seem wrong

FeujKiller reported having tickets but being unable to use gacha on October 25 (#335); erenlim0521-hue reported an apparent ×10 amount mismatch the same day (#334). Neither report alone establishes a root cause. On October 23, EremFP asked about not receiving Sandslash after many pulls (#314); that is a desired-species question, not a controlled drop-rate experiment.[3][4][5]

Use earned tickets in the right edition

The original creator describes tickets as earned through play, not bought for money. Regular enemies and bosses/rift rewards can contribute tickets; exact reward events are not a guaranteed farming rate. The game’s original guide and itch release both describe this free-play boundary.[6][7]

A similarly named edition offering ticket packs is not established as this original project. Check the address and save system before following its prices, banners or account instructions. This guide offers no ticket sales, codes or purchase buttons.

Before leaving after a pull

Inspect the collection and Save state on the current device before expecting the result on another one. If the new partner is missing elsewhere, follow save and sync checks; repeated pulls do not diagnose an upload problem.

Rules below refer to JPTER78’s source snapshot 680c3e12c670, reviewed October 26, 2026. Other hosted editions and native ports can differ. We have not completed a comparative run or verified a universal fix. No live gacha pull, shiny test or probability simulation was performed.
