Poké Arena 2D

Move, aim and evolve during real-time Pokémon battles. Open the game at pokearena2d.com, then start with Training to explore a kit before challenging an opponent.

The game opens on its own website. Its publisher blocks embedding on other sites. This page provides the starting route and other games to try.

Set up a first session in Training

These instructions follow the original Alpha 0.64.0 menus inspected on 4 November. This is a short startup check, with no completed League or online-ranked run claimed.

  1. Open the original game using the button above. Wait for its title menu. Use Play for the on-device mode list. Online is a separate title-menu choice.
  2. Choose Training. The same Play menu also lists League, VS CPU, 2 players and Arena. Training lets you choose a Pokémon and a dummy.
  3. Choose an available Pokémon in the top roster for Player 1, then choose the dummy. Read the selected kit and input labels before pressing Fight! Choose an offered arena along the bottom.
  4. Practice movement and aiming first. Follow the move labels for your selected kit, then return to the menu to choose a different mode when ready.

Locks are visible on part of the roster. This guide does not promise that every advertised Pokémon is available on a fresh save, or give an unverified unlock route. Open the game's Tutorial and Settings for its current explanations and input options.

Read the selected kit before fighting

The inspected Training selector shows WASD + mouse, F to evolve and P to pause. The selected Bulbasaur panel labels LMB, RMB, Space, Q and E for its attacks. Follow the current panel for your Pokémon instead of applying another browser game's keys.

The original advertises keyboard/mouse, gamepad and phone play. Its game page requests landscape orientation on phones. Mobile-size inspection is separate from a physical-phone or controller test, which we have not performed.

Keep the host and browser for your progress

The project's privacy notice says that session data, settings and League progress use browser local storage, and that clearing browser data removes them. Keep the same site and browser profile while continuing a League. Cross-device recovery or transfer has not been tested here. (Poké Arena 2D · project privacy notice ↗)

The project says you can play without an account, with accounts used for online modes. Its online 1v1 connections use WebRTC according to that notice. We have not tested account creation, ranked matchmaking or connection fixes. Use the original site's own support links if its service stalls.
