About the Brotato guides

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 18 November 2026

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not Blobfish, who makes Brotato, or Evil Empire, who publishes it, and the guides are not endorsed by either company or by any platform that sells the game.

What it covers

The Primal Dread setup check, Evolution choices, the Caveman and Veteran character routes, co-op setup, mod compatibility, the update and branch guide and a comparison with Vampire Survivors. Each page answers one job a player has before or during a run.

Where the answers come from

  • The developer and publisher: the Steam announcements ↗ external link, new tab supply patch notes, beta instructions and the Evolution demonstration; the publisher press briefing ↗ external link, new tab gives the console and mobile window.
  • Steam store pages: the base game listing ↗ external link, new tab and the Primal Dread listing supply platforms, co-op modes and the base-game requirement.
  • Player reports: the Caveman and Veteran routes rest on named launch-week community posts, and each page says so beside the claim.
  • Screenshots: images are official Steam screenshots or frames from the developer's announcements, labelled where they appear. They illustrate a task and do not come from our own runs.

How pages stay current

Every guide shows the date it was checked; the section as a whole was last reviewed on 18 November 2026. When Blobfish posts a balance patch, hotfix or beta, the pages that depend on its numbers are rechecked first, and beta-only values are kept apart from the default branch.

Send a correction

Send the page URL, your platform, the game version and branch, and what you saw through the contact page. A screenshot of the tooltip or patch note helps us confirm the fix.
