Build Abra around its Psychic kit

For Abra, start from its curated Psychic/Special kit and reserve space for Psybeam plus Future Sight if you want Prismatic Premonition. Magic Guard covers specific ground hazards and reduces projectile/area damage; it does not remove every survival problem.

Megabonk.org · Updated October 28, 2026 · v0.8.62

Use the League kit, not the raw Pokémon learnset

Original-game scope: pokebonk.com v0.8.62, checked October 28. Our Play window uses a separate third-party v0.8.27 edition and separate progress.

The League record supplies this four-move kit. A raw main-series Abra learnset focused on Teleport is not the playable run’s complete attack pool. Abra’s listed challenge is reaching level 30 in one run, then completing its capture; meeting that challenge does not itself select Abra for the next run.

At Bill’s Machine, the signature definition pairs Psybeam and Future Sight. Raise both to the required maximum, inspect the actual fusion option, then decide. No fixed offer order is claimed.

Check what each upgrade actually affects

The listed kit and signature result use Special damage and Psychic type. Calcium’s Special target and a Psychic-specific effect describe different scopes; neither means every physical or off-type attack you add receives the same benefit.

The signature’s inspected part definition marks enemy-targeted rays; Area and additional-count behavior affect particular parts. Use the live fusion card after combining instead of importing a general “Projectiles improves everything” rule.

Magic Guard’s description covers damage from specified ground hazards and reduces projectile/area damage by 30%. The passive’s static modifier also lists a Special multiplier. It is not blanket immunity to contact, all boss attacks or poor positioning.
