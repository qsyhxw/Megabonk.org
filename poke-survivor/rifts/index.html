PokéSurvivor rifts: times, arenas and bonds

Rifts open at 4, 8, 12, 16 and 25 minutes of a run. Step in to fight a legendary in an arena of its type: a win gives a ×10 ticket, a bond that links two moves into one slot, and a held item; a loss sends you back to the map with nothing. The minute-25 rift only gives a bond if you missed an earlier one.

Dual-type legendaries pick one of their types for the arena. Terrain matters: lava burns, ice is slippery and water slows you. Legendaries fight in three phases with five moving attacks per type and an ultimate; since 15 November their whirlwinds (Gust, Whirlpool, Fire Spin) pull you in less.

How to win a rift

  • Bring a move that is super effective against the arena’s type; the weather in the arena also changes type damage.
  • Keep moving in wide circles and step out of ground markers before they fill.
  • Enter with full HP: take a Sitrus Berry card or heal before the 4- and 8-minute rifts if you can.
  • Beat each legendary to collect all four bonds before Eternatus; which moves to bond.
