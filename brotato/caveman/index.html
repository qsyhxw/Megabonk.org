Brotato Caveman Build — Plan the Primitive Weapon Slot

Plan the shop cycle before filling every slot. Run five working weapons and keep a sixth slot for buying and burning a Primitive weapon. Spears are the usual pick for this slot plan.

Keep the purchase slot usable

The jldark89 report uses five Spears as the working core and a temporary sixth weapon for the cycle. The earlier Caveman discussion describes a similar slot plan with axes. Both are launch-week player reports. Confirm the current trait and qualifying weapon class on your character sheet.

  1. Read which weapons qualify as Primitive.
  2. Build a core that can clear the next wave without its temporary purchase.
  3. Lock a suitable future purchase when available.
  4. Complete the burn action in the shop and inspect the effect before leaving.

Spend on weapons you will keep

Spears are the starting hypothesis from the newer report. Axes are an alternative in the earlier thread. Invest upgrades in the working core and preserve enough materials for the cycle. These reports establish a practical slot-management approach, not a universal best weapon or a guaranteed Nightmare clear.

Account for permanent fog

The 12 November balance patch includes the visibility tooltip for Caveman’s permanent fog and adds Boomerang to his starting weapons. The reported Spear route remains a launch-week strategy. Reduced visibility makes chasing drops riskier. Judge an exit before committing to a path. Developer notes

Mutation after the balance patch

The 12 November default-branch patch excludes Mutation copies of Caveman’s weapon-burning effect. Keep the shop cycle grounded in the character’s own ability. 12 November balance notes

If the cycle keeps failing

Check weapon class, spare slot and purchase budget. If the cycle works but the next wave still kills you, improve the working core or learn the fog on a lower difficulty. A successful shop interaction alone does not show that the loadout can survive.
