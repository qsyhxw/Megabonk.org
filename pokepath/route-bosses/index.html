Route boss counters and recruitment conditions

Pick your goal before equipping the team. A setup that wins the fight can miss the recruitment reward: Moltres is easier to damage from far away, but recruiting it requires most of your direct damage to land inside its ring.

Megabonk.org editors · Build 1.7.3 · Updated 25 November 2026

Worlds 1 and 2

Route 1-2 · Celebi
Teleports back, sheds residual damage and heals 7,000 HP. Move direct damage toward the first waypoint. To recruit: Finish within 7.5 seconds of combat after the first waypoint.
Route 1-4 · Zapdos
Nearby attackers may lose attacks to paralysis. Use longer reach and cover its armor. To recruit: Allow at most three paralysis effects.
Route 2-1 · Moltres
Nearby attacks deal half damage. Try a longer firing distance for an ordinary clear. To recruit: Deal direct damage equal to at least 88% of its maximum HP from inside the fiery ring.
Route 2-2 · Regirock
Each incoming hit loses 200 damage. Try fewer, stronger hits when rapid attacks barely register. To recruit: Leave Poison, Burn, Nightmare and Scorch off the attacking team.
Route 2-3 · Groudon
At half health it gains 75,000 armor and becomes invulnerable for three seconds. Keep armor damage in range after the transition. To recruit: Keep attacks off Grass tiles.

For the opening map see Shaymin. On Route 1-3, count reveals when attempting to recruit Lunala.

Worlds 3 and 4

Route 3-1 · Registeel
Hits above 1,000 are reduced. Try faster attacks if large hits are underperforming. To recruit: Keep every single hit at or below 500.
Route 3-2 · Regice
It ignores critical damage and has 200,000 armor. Watch armor loss when comparing attackers. To recruit: Let it block at least 50 critical hits before the kill.
Route 3-4 · Articuno
Nearby attackers take 50% longer to recharge. Check whether a longer-range placement gets more attacks. To recruit: Finish with Hail active.
Route 4-1 · Kyogre
Plan for a three-second invulnerable phase and 75,000 new armor below half HP. To recruit: Land the final blow from a Field tile.
Route 4-3 · Tapu Fini
After eight hits it dodges the next three. Time large hits around the dodge sequence. To recruit: Allow at most ten dodged attacks.
Route 4-4 · Nihilego
Deal damage from Water tiles. Check where the attacker is actually standing. To recruit: Use only attackers whose deployment terrain is exclusively Water.

Regigigas needs the +50% Tough Enemies setting for recruitment.

World 5

Route 5-2 · Tapu Bulu
It regenerates 20,000 HP per second. Hits from healing-capable Pokémon cut regeneration by 1,000 per second apiece. To recruit: Leave regeneration intact and concentrate damage.
Route 5-3 · Tapu Lele
An item holder deals 35% less damage. An empty-handed attacker deals 15% more. Compare the same attacker unequipped. To recruit: Use attackers with Power of 100 or less.
Route 5-4 · Keldeo
Burn, Poison, Stun and Slow each grant it a 5,000 shield when applied. Inspect passive effects as well as direct attacks. To recruit: Keep Justified inactive.

For Route 5-1, use the Tapu Koko stun-window guide.

When the challenge selector is locked

The project FAQ lists three prerequisites: earn 540 stars, own at least 30 Pokémon in your box and defeat the wave-100 boss on the map in question. If challenges appear on one route but not another, check the second route’s boss clear.

Each linked bestiary entry describes the enemy. The project unlock guide lists the separate recruit conditions, including bosses beyond this selection. The placement suggestions follow those mechanics; confirm clears in the game.
