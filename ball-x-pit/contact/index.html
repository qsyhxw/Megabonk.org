Found a wrong recipe, an outdated update note or a broken tool? Email contact@megabonk.org with the page URL, your platform and game version, and a public source or a short description of what you saw.

Useful reports: a recipe that does not match your game, a character unlock that is described incorrectly, a price or platform change on the store page. Please do not send passwords, purchase keys or game credentials.

We cannot restore saves, issue refunds or give in-game items. For purchase or account problems, use the support link on the store where you bought the game. There is no promised reply time. Steam · Ball x Pit store and support links ↗
