Find the next zone for the class you selected

The original v0.130.0 picker lists twelve zones. Defeat the preceding zone’s final boss to open the next for that class. Another class’s progress does not automatically open your selected hero’s route.[1]

Use the displayed clock, starting with Elwynn

The current Elwynn panel and Drop Tables show Hogger at 3:00, Mother Fang at 6:00, Rhahk’Zor at 9:00, Mr. Smite at 12:00 and final boss Edwin VanCleef at 16:00. Arrival time is not a guaranteed kill or finish time. We have not independently captured every later-zone timer, so this page does not turn community 16/18/20-minute summaries into universal schedules.[1]

When progress looks stuck

  1. Recheck the class on the picker and its Zones open count.
  2. Read the locked zone’s named predecessor and boss. Surviving part of the previous zone is not the same as defeating its final boss.
  3. If the next step feels too hard, inspect class talent points, worn gear and the current build trigger before raising difficulty.
  4. For loot, choose the relevant Drop Tables row instead of assuming the newest unlocked zone drops every missing piece.
  5. In co-op, check that the selected class qualifies for the host’s zone; a code does not transfer another class’s progression.

Separate a zone unlock, a Heroic unlock and a gear-star permission in your notes. Read the difficulty scopes →[1]
