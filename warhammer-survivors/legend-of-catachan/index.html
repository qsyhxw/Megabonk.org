Unlock the Legend of Catachan

Rescue the Legend of Catachan on Imperial Bastion in the Warhammer 40,000 universe. Check character selection after the run to use him as your champion.

Complete the Imperial Bastion rescue

  1. Select the 40K universe and start Imperial Bastion with an available champion.
  2. Explore for the captive and approach with room to fight the guards.
  3. Defeat the highlighted enemies surrounding him and complete the rescue.
  4. Check character selection for the unlocked Legend on your next run.

The rescue location is reported by Prima Games ↗. The guard-clearing sequence is described by How to Video Game ↗. Neither establishes a fixed compass direction from every spawn.

Frag Grenade and the rescued-character bug

His starting weapon is Frag Grenade. Steam hotfix v2.1.6, published 11 November, fixes a rescued Legend incorrectly using a Chainsword. The developer schedules the changes on other platforms from the following week. Check your installed version if the rescued ally still carries the wrong weapon. Developer hotfix announcements

For the Frag Grenade evolution, match Bandolier in the recipe finder. The companion unlock and the evolution attempt are separate checks.

If you already played him in the demo

The October demo offered the Legend as a playable hero. Demo progress does not transfer into the released game, so rescue him in your full-game save. Official demo announcement ↗

The portrait above is an in-game asset reproduced by Wikily ↗. This guide combines published sources and has no site-run rescue test. Continue with first-run planning or Secrets access.
