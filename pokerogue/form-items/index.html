How to use the Dynamax Band and Mega Bracelet

The Dynamax Band unlocks Max Mushrooms for eligible Pokémon. The Mega Bracelet unlocks the matching Mega Stone. Both routes need a second reward before the Pokémon changes.

Megabonk.org · Updated 25 November 2026 · Patch v1.12.2.8

Match the unlock to the Pokémon

GoalFirst rewardThen look for
Gigantamax GarbodorDynamax BandMax Mushrooms for your own eligible Garbodor. Seeing the boss in Trash to Treasure does not give your party that form.
Mega RayquazaMega BraceletRayquazite for your own Rayquaza. Teaching Dragon Ascent alone will not activate the PokéRogue form.
Mega MagearnaMega BraceletMagearnite. The same stone works for standard and Original Color Magearna and leads to the corresponding Mega form.

If the Band did nothing

  1. Check the species in your party. The Band enables rewards for eligible Pokémon. It does not give every team member a Gigantamax form.
  2. Keep the eligible Pokémon and watch later reward choices for Max Mushrooms. Picking the Band does not guarantee mushrooms on the next wave.
  3. Give the form item to the eligible Pokémon. In Check Team you can deactivate its form item and reactivate it. Switching out does not end a Mega or Gigantamax form.

Decide before spending a reroll

If your only target is Magearna and you already have the Bracelet, your missing piece is Magearnite. A Dynamax Band does nothing for that goal. If your team needs healing now, budget for it before chasing a random form item.

Facing the event boss instead? See Garbodor’s opening moves and the reward choice. The opponent and the reward screen are separate decisions.
