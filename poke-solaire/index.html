From the click gate to your first room

These steps follow the original v1.8 client inspected on 5 November 2026, entered as a guest. This is a startup and menu check — no full run is claimed.

  1. Click Play above and wait for the original to load. The first screen says CLIC PARA CONTINUAR — click the screen once to pass its audio gate.
  2. A first-run notice (Aviso antes de jugar) explains that PokéSolaire is in development and runs on free services; your progress (Pokémon, eggs, mastery, missions) saves only on this device, while an in-game account also keeps it in the cloud. The Continue button unlocks after a short countdown.
  3. Switch the language if you want English: Ajustes → Idioma → English (Português and Français sit next to it). As of v1.8 the settings page notes that encounter dialogues remain in Spanish.
  4. Choose JUGAR: your starter Pikachu waits at the bonfire — tap it or press CAMBIAR to pick another companion — then press EMPEZAR. The first-run notice says you unlock the full first generation by finding eggs and evolving mid-run.
  5. You start in El Laberinto, Sala 1. The original ships its own interactive guides («Cómo pelear» and the movement panel); replay them any time via Settings → Ver las guías otra vez.

For controls, Ajustes has TECLADO (keyboard) and MANDO (gamepad) tabs. The gamepad tab lists Roll on A/✕, Sprint on B/○, Block/parry on RB/R1, Light attack on X/□, Heavy attack on Y/△, Special/interact on LB/L1, Switch type on View/Share, Lock on R3 and Pause on Start/Options (English UI read 5 November 2026). The developer's Reddit post adds that the Tab key switches your active elemental type mid-run. (r/playmygame · developer post (u/Far_List_3384)) We checked menus and the first room as guests; this is not a full-run, ranked or controller benchmark.

What is saved where

The first-run notice is explicit: guest progress is saved only on this device, so keep the same host and browser profile and do not clear site data mid-run. With an in-game account, progress is stored in the cloud and the notice says you can recover it on another device.

The game menu links the project's Discord server. If the framed window stalls, open the original in its own tab before troubleshooting anything else — an outage on the original site cannot be fixed from this page. (PokéSolaire · official Discord invite ↗)
