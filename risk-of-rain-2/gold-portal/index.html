INDEPENDENT PLAYER GUIDE · RISK OF RAIN 2

Gold Portal → Halcyon Seed → False Son

Pay an Altar of Gold, finish its Teleporter event, then enter the Gold Portal. At Gilded Coast, activate all seven beacons and defeat Aurelionite for Halcyon Seed. For Purified Freedom, keep that seed and defeat False Son at Prime Meridian with Seekers of the Storm enabled.

By Megabonk.org · checked 5 November 2026 · document-based, not a playtest

A Gold Portal is the entrance, not the unlock. Your checkpoints are Gilded Coast → seven active beacons → Halcyon Seed → False Son with the seed. A portal visit without collecting the seed leaves the final prerequisite incomplete.

At Gilded Coast: all seven beacons

  1. Locate the seven Halcyon Beacons and gather gold for their activation costs before committing to the fight.
  2. The fourth activated beacon summons Aurelionite, but it does not make her vulnerable. Activate all seven.
  3. Damage Aurelionite while all seven remain active. If the beacons reset, pay to reactivate them and reopen the damage window.
  4. After winning, pick up Halcyon Seed. Check your inventory before choosing an exit.

These rules come from the community Wiki’s Gilded Coast documentation, with the basic sequence also described by the independent Gold Portal guide. We did not play this fight ourselves. The official image above is a historical beacon-rendering comparison, useful for recognizing the objects, not a current spawn map.

Pick the exit for your goal

With Seekers of the Storm enabled, the challenge documentation describes a green exit toward the Path of the Colossus when Gilded Coast was entered from a stage 1, 2 or 3 environment. Keep track of where you entered; do not plan around every Gilded Coast visit producing that same continuation.

If you only wanted the boss item, collecting it completes that goal. If you want the False Son survivor, preserve the seed and continue along the Colossus route to Prime Meridian. Do not convert the seed away as if it were spare loot. This guide does not promise a guaranteed altar, Bazaar offer or fixed map sequence.

Keep taking the green route, not the ordinary Teleporter: its normal sequence is Reformed Altar → Treeborn Colony (or its loop variant Golden Dieback) → Prime Meridian. A later entry can skip earlier route stages. On Reformed Altar or Treeborn Colony, finish the event and enter the Green Portal to stay on the Colossus path; choosing the ordinary Teleporter sends you back to the normal stage route.

Complete Purified Freedom

Defeat False Son at Prime Meridian while carrying the Halcyon Seed obtained from Gilded Coast. This is the Seekers of the Storm challenge that unlocks the playable False Son. Merely beating Aurelionite, opening the green route, or visiting Prime Meridian is not the same result.

The community challenge guide says False Son takes the seed in the third phase and summons an enemy Aurelionite. Plan for that extra threat rather than expecting your Gilded Coast ally to protect you. Its stated completion condition is defeating False Son; the summoned Aurelionite need not be killed first and can remain dangerous afterward.

This final-phase behavior is a single community reference, checked through its indexed text on 5 November 2026; it was not independently reproduced here. Verify the challenge notification after the fight before treating the survivor as unlocked. We do not promise a particular damage build, difficulty setting or multiplayer credit outcome.
