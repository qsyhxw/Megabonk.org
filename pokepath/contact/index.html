Contact the PokéPath TD guide editors

Reviewed 22 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A unit ability, enemy rule or item that changed in a newer patch;
  • A save export or import step that works differently on your device or browser;
  • A reward code or redemption field that behaves differently from the code guide;
  • A wrong screenshot, link or Pokémon name on a PokéPath TD page.

Include the page URL, your device and browser, the version shown on your play page and what happened. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not the developer or the game’s support team, so we cannot restore a save, grant a reward or change how the game works. Use the original PokéPath site ↗ to reach the project.
