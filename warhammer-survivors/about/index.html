About the Warhammer Survivors guides

Megabonk.org editors · Reviewed 13 November 2026 · Checked against Steam v2.1.6

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not Auroch Digital, Games Workshop or game support, and nothing here is an official announcement. Warhammer names, artwork and in-game images belong to their owners.

What it covers

The section answers the questions players hit in their first weeks: which weapon + companion pairs evolve, which builds carry a run, how to beat each Extremis boss, how to unlock champions and stages, and what co-op and cross-save support actually exist on each platform.

Where the answers come from

  • The game itself: evolution requirements, objectives and map relics are read from the in-game level-up screen, Objectives list and pause map; the screenshots on each page are frames from that UI.
  • Gameplay recordings: boss timings, rescue routes and build orders are checked against full-run videos, which are credited where they appear and load only when you click play.
  • Official channels: release status, platforms, patch notes and the single-player answer come from Auroch Digital's FAQ, the Steam store page and Steam announcements, and the Xbox and mobile store listings.

Where two sources disagree, the page gives the answer the in-game UI supports. Platform-specific limits (Switch, mobile, Xbox) are stated on the page they affect.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was last updated and the release it was checked against. When a patch changes weapons, bosses or unlocks, the affected pages are rechecked first; the current baseline is Steam v2.1.6 (11 November 2026 hotfix), whose console changes are scheduled to reach Switch a week later.

Found something wrong?

Send the page URL, your platform and game version, and what you saw in game through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or a public source are usually applied in the next update.
