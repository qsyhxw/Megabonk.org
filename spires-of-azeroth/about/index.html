About the Spires of Azeroth guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 21 November 2026 · Checked against browser build 20261103e

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org. We are not Chrisl252, who makes Spires of Azeroth, we do not run TheWoWDB where the game is hosted, and we are not Blizzard Entertainment. Warcraft names and artwork belong to their owners.

The game is a fan project inspired by Classic World of Warcraft. It is a turn-based card roguelike, not Blizzard’s MMO and not the survival game Azeroth Survivors.

What it covers

The guide home loads the original game in a window on click and explains the first run, the card controls and how the nine classes differ. The download page separates the browser, the author’s desktop and Android packages and the iPhone / iPad Safari route, and explains why saves do not move between them.

It does not rank classes or publish a best-deck list.

Where the answers come from

  • The original game: menus, the “How to fight” tutorial, the Download panel and the title screen are captured from the browser game at thewowdb.com ↗.
  • The author’s repository: platform packages, first-launch behaviour and version numbers come from Chrisl252/spiresofazeroth ↗ and its latest release.
  • Our own checks: a short run to the second turn in a browser window, desktop and mobile viewport sizes. We have not cleared a full run, installed a package, moved a save between hosts, tried the Co-op mode or used a physical phone, and the pages do not claim to.

How pages stay current

Each page shows the date it was checked. The version line on the download page follows the author’s browser build and latest GitHub package automatically; the walkthrough steps are rechecked when a new build changes the menus. The current baseline is browser build 20261103e, inspected 4 November 2026.

Found something wrong?

Send the page URL, the host you used (the original site, our game window or an app package), the build or version shown and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections with a screenshot or a public reference are usually applied in the next update.
