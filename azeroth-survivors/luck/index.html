What does Luck do in Azeroth Survivors?

Luck improves the chance of rarer upgrades when you level up, according to the original Run Stats description. It is a choice about future offers: compare it with damage, coverage or recovery that can solve the current fight. No exact rarity formula is verified here.[1]

Read the displayed bonus before changing race or talents

Gnome’s Engineering Specialization states +20% luck in the inspected original text. Race availability and unlocking it are separate from choosing an upgrade during a run. Read the automatically checked racial values →[1]

The original snapshot also contains rank-dependent Luck percentage descriptions. Read the actual allocated talent or offered item for its value; do not substitute the unresolved template with a guessed percentage. A racial bonus, a talent and a run item can have different persistence rules. The Rewards screenshot shows the race-unlock location, not your current total or an observed rarity outcome.

Compare a Luck choice without inventing a drop rate

  1. Record host, client version, class, race and permanent talents.
  2. Inspect the current Luck entry in Run Stats; preserve the displayed value and the chosen offer.
  3. Compare otherwise similar attempts and record level-up rarities, not just a memorable legendary.
  4. Keep upgrade-card outcomes separate from boss gear and critical-hit counts.
  5. Treat a small sample as an observation, not proof of a percentage-per-point formula.

No controlled sample is supplied on this page. [1]

Does LuckyOdds reveal the exact formula?

Cadoky’s LuckyOdds offers an odds panel and minimap helpers. Its author explicitly presents the odds as a rough guide rather than the exact game roll. That makes it an optional display to compare, not evidence for a precise probability calculator on this page. [2]
