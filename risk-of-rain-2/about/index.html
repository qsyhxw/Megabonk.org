About the Risk of Rain 2 guides

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 22 November 2026 · Sources last checked 5 November 2026

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not Hopoo Games, Gearbox or 2K, and nothing here is endorsed by them.

What it covers

The guide home sends you to the page for your next job: the crossplay copy check, the Gold Portal route to the False Son unlock and, in Russian, the Bulwark’s Ambry artifact trial.

Where the answers come from

  • Publisher support articles: 2K’s crossplay instructions ↗ and console notice ↗ supply the Steam and Epic route, the Steam setting and the console-generation limits.
  • The store listing and announcements: the Steam page ↗ and the Steam Community hub ↗ supply editions, patch notes and the artifact announcement.
  • Community reference: the Risk of Rain 2 Wiki ↗ supplies Gilded Coast and challenge details. Where a page rests on a single community source, that page says so.
  • Images: gameplay images are frames from the official 1.0 launch trailer and an official patch-note comparison. The guides rely on the sources above and on no complete playthrough of our own.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was checked; the section as a whole was last reviewed on 22 November 2026, with source facts last confirmed on 5 November 2026. When 2K or the developers change crossplay rules, consoles or the unlock route, the pages that depend on them are rechecked and each date changes only when its answer was confirmed again.

Which game this guide is about

These pages cover Risk of Rain 2 from Hopoo Games, published by Gearbox Publishing and 2K, including its Seekers of the Storm content where a page needs it. Advice for one store or console edition is marked as such.

Send a correction

Send the page URL, your store or console edition and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or a publisher statement are reviewed first.
