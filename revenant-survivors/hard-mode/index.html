Revenant Survivors Hard Mode rewards and World 5 unlock

Hard Mode increases item drop rates, and clearing it is required to unlock World 5 and later worlds. The developer confirmed both points in the Steam discussion. Choose it for that progression gate or additional item drops once your build can finish the run.

Megabonk.org editors · Checked 12 November 2026 · Early Access

What Hard Mode rewards

The developer answered the player asking whether Hard Mode was only a challenge by confirming increased item drop rates and the World 5 progression requirement. The reply provides no percentage and leaves the separate question of increased Gold gain unresolved. Plan around the confirmed item benefit when comparing modes. Developer reply on Hard Mode rewards and unlocks

Choose a mode for your next run

  1. You are trying to reach World 5 or beyond. Check your stage-selection unlock condition and work toward its Hard Mode clear. Repeating a lower-tier clear alone does not satisfy the stated Hard Mode requirement.
  2. You want equipment. Consider Hard Mode if you can complete it consistently. Higher drop rates alone do not establish the best items per minute, since failed or longer runs change that comparison.
  3. Your immediate bottleneck is Gold. Use your own completed-run rewards and time to compare. A Gold multiplier was not specified in the developer reply.
  4. You are struggling with a boss. Review your retained equipment and upgrades, then retry after confirming your installed build. The balance changed again on November 11.

Match difficulty advice to the current patch

Update 0.6.2 reduced the World 1 boss jumping-slam area and adjusted World 6 patterns. The November 11 hotfix 0.6.2.1 changed final-boss health and attack power in Tiers 2–4, reduced regular-enemy health in Tier 4, and changed the Hard Mode final-boss health multiplier. These announcements provide no numerical multiplier. Mongma Studio patch announcements

For an unlock problem, record the world, tier, mode and result shown after the run. The developer’s short reply establishes the progression gate, while the stage-selection screen supplies the specific condition for your selected world. We have not independently played through that chain.
