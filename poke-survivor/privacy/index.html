Privacy notice

Updated October 26, 2026

This notice covers the independent guides at megabonk.org/poke-survivor/. It is separate from the original game’s own account and privacy practices.

Hosting and delivery

Cloudflare provides hosting and security. It may process technical request data such as IP address, browser details and the requested URL. Cloudflare may automatically add Web Analytics or performance measurement to pages, including error responses, and process technical request and performance data. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab).

Advertising and website scripts

The section homepage and ordinary gameplay guides load this website’s Google AdSense code. Google and its advertising partners may make requests and use cookies or device information for advertising and measurement. About, Contact, this Privacy page and error pages do not load our advertising code. This section adds no Google Analytics tag or other new analytics service.

Fonts, diagrams and title-screen media are hosted by this website. The guide navigation and collapsible menus use native HTML. The Play page uses a local script for click-to-load, expanded view and hiding/restoring the same game frame. These display controls do not store your game collection. The third-party game does not load until you press Play. Once loaded, pokesurvivor.pro, r2.pokennection.org and jsDelivr supply the game and its resources. The embedded provider also requests Google Tag Manager and Cloudflare performance code; these are inside the third-party game, separate from our guide scripts. The provider may contact account and game-data services and use browser storage, cookies and technical request information. Its embedded sign-in and guest features are operated by that provider, not Megabonk.org. Read the host’s legal/privacy notice inside the game before signing in; account and save compatibility with pokesurvivor.com is not established. Opening an external game or download page also connects to that provider under its own practices.

Advertising choices

Review Google’s advertising privacy information ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab) and Google My Ad Center ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab). Where provided, Google’s consent messages offer applicable choices. This notice is not a promise that the whole domain makes no third-party requests.

Email

Email sent to qsyhxw@gmail.com is processed through Gmail. The publisher receives the sender address and details you include to handle the request. Send no passwords or account tokens. You can request deletion of correspondence at the same address, subject to necessary record retention.

Other sections and external services

Other Megabonk.org sections can load Google Analytics or different functional scripts and have a site-wide privacy policy. External original-game accounts, cloud saves, download hosts, GitHub and Reddit are not controlled by this guide publisher. Check the destination’s notice before signing in.

Questions: contact the publisher. This notice will change if services in this section change.
