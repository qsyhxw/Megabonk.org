Unlock your next PokeBonk League partner

Choose a target, satisfy every listed condition, then capture that eligible partner. The 79 League starting records and the 151-species Pokédex count different things. A challenge, an in-run evolution and a permanent League capture are separate steps.[1][2]

Challenge first, capture second

  1. Open League and inspect the locked partner’s challenge. Where several requirements appear, finish all of them.
  2. Check whether the counter is cumulative or must be reached in one run. Returning with 500 twice does not meet a “1,000 in one run” condition.
  3. Use an eligible solo run for League progress. Co-op has its own results and does not substitute for solo League/Look challenges.
  4. Once eligible, watch for the partner’s actual capture opportunity and follow its capture prompt. A full challenge bar alone is not the completed capture.
  5. Confirm the partner appears in your League before changing browser/account.

Quick routes: your initially chosen starter comes through first-time selection; the two other starters require two completed runs (losses count); Pidgey requires four. Eevee needs 15 League members and ten different partners played. Mewtwo needs 60 members, the three birds captured, and Rocky Desert Hard cleared. Mew waits for the other 78 members. These are eligibility checks, not guaranteed next-run spawns.

Sleep and Burn: make the correct counter move

A Reddit question by SimonTheCrab, displayed one day old when sampled October 26, asks how to finish sleep unlocks. A separate skin question by shazamtamp asks about Burn. These are two reports of questions, not measured success rates.[3][4]

TargetA source-supported routeCheck after the run
Cumulative Sleep: Jigglypuff 1,500; Poliwag 4,000; Slowpoke 8,000Use a sleep-applying move such as Sleep Powder, or Awakening: 2.5% sleep chance on hits in the checked item definition (max 3 stacks).Compare the Sleep counter, not total KOs. The client counts each enemy once per status; reapplying sleep to the same target does not repeatedly add progress.
One-run Sleep: Drowzee 1,500Build around a sleep source from the beginning of an eligible solo run. Keep the in-run target separate from lifetime totals.The best single-run total must meet 1,500; smaller runs do not add to it.
Cumulative Burn: Vulpix 6,000Use a burn-applying move or Flame Orb: a listed 5% hit chance plus 10% status damage (max 5 stacks). Fire typing alone does not establish a burn application.Read Burn progress; do not confuse Fire damage dealt with enemies burned.
One-run Burn: Magmar 6,000; Moltres 8,000Meet the League-size and map gates too. Preserve the status source through your build decisions.A full Burn target cannot replace another unmet prerequisite.

The status counter counts distinct affected enemies within the run, once per enemy for each status. Do not assume adding two status sources doubles progress, or that chance × stack count is the final probability. Those interactions were not measured here. For Mastery skins, the chosen family and the specific Look condition are additional restrictions; use the skin-progress checklist.

Challenge complete but still locked?

First distinguish eligible from captured. Then check the exact account, all prerequisites, solo/mode restrictions and whether the displayed result was accepted. Record the target, before/after counter, game version and end screen for support. No spawn deadline or account repair is promised here.

After the requirement table

A completed challenge makes an encounter eligible; it is not the entire capture procedure. Use the timed capture workflow to distinguish League joins from Alpha support and Looks. Mewtwo uses a special Classic encounter; Mew instead waits for the other 78 League members. Those are different targets.

New task-specific additions reviewed October 28 against the original v0.8.62 client; recommendations remain source-based.[5]

Separate a legendary League join from its shiny

For Mew, Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres and Mewtwo, the .63 notes and current secure() handler separate membership from shiny capture: an eligible League legendary joins when weakened. A Poké Ball is still required to keep its shiny. Other League partners retain their normal capture workflow; prerequisites and encounter eligibility still matter.
