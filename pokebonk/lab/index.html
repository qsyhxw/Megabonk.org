Spend Stardust on the upgrade you need

The fourth move slot costs 1,275 Stardust in the checked v0.8.55 build; the fourth level-up choice costs 2,550. They solve different problems. Use the full cost table and budget examples below before spending on a permanent upgrade.[2]

Checked October 26, 2026 · Beta v0.8.55 · Source-based guide

Check your version first

Version boundary: this guide covers the original pokebonk.com v0.8.55, checked October 26. Our Play window uses a separate third-party v0.8.27 source with separate progress. Newer co-op, Treasures and Archive changes may be absent there. Mechanics are SINGLE_SOURCE; no full-run, reward-claim or multiplayer completion test is claimed.

Archive capacity changed in v0.8.55

The six paid levels supply five slots each (30 paid slots). Once you own at least one Archive level, v0.8.55 adds one slot per two unlocked items from the 59-item unlockable catalog: floor(unlocked catalog items ÷ 2). The maximum is 30 + 29 = 59 TOTAL slots, not 59 extra. With no paid Archive level, the bonus is zero. For example, Archive level 1 and 20 qualifying unlocks gives 5 + 10 = 15 slots. The live Lab card separates the two sources; discovered-only items do not count.

Discovered, unlocked into your item pool, and archived are different states. Archiving an unlocked item removes it from normal Poké Ball/shop selection; restoring it returns it to the pool. Avoid archiving something needed for your next Build plan.

Earn and spend Stardust without mixing currencies

Run rewards, claimable challenges, daily/weekly tasks, event rewards and shiny rewards are distinct routes. Check the available task before choosing a farming run. In the checked definitions, the three daily rewards are 60/100/160 and weekly rewards 300/450/700 Stardust; shiny captures list 120 first-time and 50 repeat rewards. These are listed rewards, not dust per hour.

  1. Inspect near-complete tasks and whether they require one run, a specific mode or a particular map.
  2. Choose a run that can advance the task without sacrificing your immediate survival goal.
  3. Finish and inspect accepted results, then claim rewards where the UI requires it.
  4. Return to the Lab and budget from your actual balance.

Bonkoins are a separate currency for Treasures. Run-money and Stardust are not interchangeable balances. No farming rate, guaranteed payout or purchase test is claimed.
