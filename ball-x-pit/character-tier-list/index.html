Ball x Pit character tier list: picks with conditions

Choose a character for the run’s task: a conventional pick for learning, a compatible pair for your build, or extra capacity for collecting recipes. Published tiers and community preferences disagree. The table below makes those conditions explicit instead of presenting an untested best-to-worst score.

Why the published tiers disagree

Dexerto places Flagellant lower than several standard shooters, while respondents in the pairing thread recommend it enthusiastically. That does not establish a patch change or a measured error: the sources do not share a controlled difficulty, build or input setup. Treat it as a reason to ask what the rank is optimizing.

Before following a tier, write down your goal: first clear, a longer run, an encyclopedia entry, or a trophy clear with a particular character. A collector’s extra slots and a manual player’s preferred bounce pattern are different advantages. Our shortlist organizes those decisions without fabricating a combined score.

When you can bring two characters

First confirm that you can select the partner and have built the Matchmaker. Match the pair’s rules to the balls and items you want to use. A no-baby-ball partner invalidates a baby-ball plan; a character with no passives invalidates a passive-recipe plan. The pairing table → gives reported combinations and their constraints.

If a recommended character is missing

Use the character and blueprint checks → to identify the corresponding house, inspect the stage’s blueprint indicator and finish construction back at town. This page recommends among options you can use; it does not guarantee a particular blueprint will drop in the next run.
