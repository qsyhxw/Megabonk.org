Warhammer Survivors maps: every stage and how to unlock it

Stages unlock in a chain by reaching a character level on the previous map: 20, then 40, 50 and 60. Each universe has five main stages, a bonus stage from 'Eavy Metal mode, a Hobby Table and a final special stage.

Unlock tips

  1. Level, not survival time, opens the next map. A run that reaches level 40 on Gallowdark unlocks Ork Stronghold even if you die afterwards.
  2. Pick up the map relics: the Signum Array (Ork Stronghold) reveals rescue targets on every 40K map; the Auspex (Desert Wasteland) and Arcanoscope (Gnarlwood) reveal hidden passives.
  3. Turn on 'Eavy Metal once on any stage to open Desert Wasteland (40K) and Gnarlwood (AoS).
  4. Rescues on each map are listed with directions on the characters page; Extremis bosses on the bosses page.
