Secret characters through gameplay

Use the Secrets hint to identify a character, then check that character’s prerequisites and gameplay action. This guide covers the base-game routes below, plus one Emerald Diorama DLC route, with hints separated from spoilers. It is a starting checklist, not an all-characters list.

By Megabonk.org · checked 10 November 2026 · Vampire Survivors is by poncle.

Open the permanent Secrets menu

Work toward Yellow Sign first. The Bone Zone opens after Hyper has been unlocked for three normal stages. Defeat Sketamari, the moving bone ball marked by a question mark south of the start, and collect the Forbidden Scrolls of Morbane it drops. That relic permanently opens Secrets.

Unlocking the menu and solving a character’s condition are separate tasks. The menu also accepts built-in spells, but the routes below focus on gameplay. Castlevania’s later secret hints have their own relic prerequisites.

Choose a route you can already start

Leda — explore below Gallo Tower

Start Gallo Tower and travel south. The edges darken as you approach the hidden area. Look for Leda in the center of a room and defeat her. Do not race past her once she is visible. A fast character or Hyper can help cover the distance; Hurry shortens the available real-time exploration window. Return to character selection to purchase the unlocked character.

Minnah — the Dairy Plant cheese

After Yellow Sign, enter Dairy Plant and collect the special Cheese pickup. Its map marker uses a Floor Chicken icon. Mad Groove can bring it to you if that Arcana is available. Collecting it spawns seven large werewolves. Defeat all seven, then check character selection.

Peppino — heal the plants in Il Molise

Use Celestial Dusting on the plants in Il Molise. It heals them instead of damaging them. Restore a total of 100,000 health to unlock Peppino. O’Sole begins with Celestial Dusting, so that character provides a direct setup once unlocked. Killing plants with an unrelated weapon does not satisfy the healing task.

Mask of the Red Death — defeat the Reaper

Prepare the two evolution sets in our Red Death guide, defeat the Reaper, then purchase the character. The White Hand ending is separate from the unlock condition.

Toastie — uppercut the clue

The Secrets hint “2) Uppercut a non-red reaper” is a prompt, not a combat move, because Vampire Survivors has no manual attack input. First unlock Exdash Exiviiq by any method. Then kill The Stalker, The Drowner or The Trickster with a weapon or pickup that instantly kills enemies. While Toastie shows briefly in the bottom-right corner, press Down Arrow and Enter together; S does not count. On console, use D-Pad Down plus the confirm button. Once unlocked, Toastie costs 777 Gold Coins, scaled by characters bought.

Torino — the 17th Colossus

The Secrets hint reads “Witness the 17th Colossus wander to new horizons.” Start a run on Mazerella and take the maze path toward the room with the Colossus. It stays asleep until it is reduced to 90% health. Once awake, it walks down through the map, so stand in the path it has destroyed and let it leave the southern edge. Sources differ slightly on the exact trigger; if the in-game route fails, check the wiki entry before retrying. Once unlocked, Torino costs 5,000 Gold Coins.

Smith IV — the “ask for help” clue

The Secrets hint reads “3) Sometimes you just need to ask for help.” Guides list Exdash and Toastie as earlier steps, so unlock those first. Older guides solve this clue with a spell or typed input rather than a stage route. This page does not reproduce those methods; check the wiki’s Smith IV entry for the current version before using them.

Gazebo — survive 20 minutes in The Coop (DLC)

Gazebo comes from the Emerald Diorama expansion, which the wiki dates to patch 1.13 in April 2025. The wiki’s current requirement is surviving to 20:00 on The Coop. Older guides describe unlocking him by evolving Gaze of Gaea instead, so check the version note before following one. His passives include +50% Move Speed, and each overheal of 30 grants a random stat bonus. Confirm your edition owns the pack first; see our DLC comparison.

When the unlock does not appear

Check the named stage, prerequisite relic, character or weapon, and final trigger. A hint being visible is not proof the condition was completed. An unlocked character can still require a purchase. For other secrets, open the linked character’s full unlocking details in the Wiki. The routes here leave other base-game secrets and DLC secret rosters uncovered.
