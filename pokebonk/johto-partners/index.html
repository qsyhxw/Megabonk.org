How to read the evolution column

The evolution column uses official species data. PokeBonk can use different level thresholds, so its numbers are shown separately as unverified candidates from the public client. Do not copy the official levels into PokeBonk plans. Partners marked “not in public client” have no PokeBonk level candidate yet.
