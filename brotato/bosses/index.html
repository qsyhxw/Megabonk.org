Brotato Abyss Bosses — Dead Whale and Eel Stats

Dead Whale and Eel both reach 31,625 HP at wave 20 and gain 875 HP per wave from 15,000 at wave 1. Dead Whale moves at speed 200 and Eel at speed 150. Both are Abyssal Terrors bosses, so check your Item Sets before planning a run around them.

The wiki labels these as Mutation 0, 1 and 2. They are boss phases, not the Mutation path in the Evolution system. Behaviour descriptions come from the Brotato Wiki Enemies page only.

Plan around speed and phases

Dead Whale is faster than Eel on the source tables (200 against 150), so keep a movement lane open when it charges. Below 40% HP, Eel’s phase repeats every 55 seconds on the wiki timing, so plan burst damage around that interval rather than a fixed kill window.

Use the Evolution guide to decide whether your build supports the damage needed for these HP values.

What this page does not cover

We have not confirmed the exact wave at which bosses appear in every run, the drop rules beyond the Materials value, or any version after the checked date. Check the in-game Codex when a stat differs from this table.
