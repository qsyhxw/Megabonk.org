Lugia: passive, kit and evolution

Lugia is a Johto League partner in PokeBonk v0.10.18. Its passive is Guardian of the Sea; its evolution levels come from the public client and differ from the official games.

Megabonk.org · Updated October 23, 2026 · v0.8.25

Type and role

Lugia is a Guardian of the sea partner. Type: Psychic Flying.

Portrait from the existing PokeBonk sprite set.

Passive: Guardian of the Sea

Legendary: +10% damage, max HP and speed. Every 7 s a sea whirlpool (5 m) opens under the densest group: it pulls it in, wets it and hits it. With weather, Weather Ball deals ×1.4 damage and gives you its status; Whirlwind pushes the horde back.

Kit: the four listed moves

Base values from the public client. Upgrades, vitamins and fusions change them in play.

MoveTypeClassCooldownStatusClient description
Weather BallNormalSpecial1.29 s—Throws an orb that explodes on impact.
Rain DanceWaterSpecial28.8 swetSummons rain: boosts Water and Electric, soaks enemies.

Evolution

Lugia has no level-based evolution in the public client.

Unlock requirement

  • Have 40 Johto members in your League.
  • Capture Raikou, Entei and Suicune.
  • Win in Ecruteak City on Nightmare with Johto Pokémon.

Challenge text from the public client.

Season 3 rating

Rated A in the Season 3 list. This rating is not re-checked for v0.10.18.

What this page does not cover

Based on the client text only. No in-game run was completed, so no damage, win-rate or clear-time claim is made.

No in-game screenshot is included yet. The only images are the sprite portrait and the original text type badges; screenshots will be added when they are available.
