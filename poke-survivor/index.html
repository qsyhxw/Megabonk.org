About the game

PokéSurvivor is a free survival game by JPTER78 that you play in a browser on PC or phone at pokesurvivor.com. You pick a Pokémon partner, roam a top-down field while your active move fires on its own, and choose a card each level: a new move, an upgrade or a boost. Between runs you spend tickets in the gacha, collect partners and team up with up to three friends. It is a fan project, separate from PokeBonk and from the Megabonk Steam game; see games with similar names.

PokéSurvivor FAQ

Short answers; each links to the full guide.

Is pokesurvivor.doofcraft.com the original PokéSurvivor?

No. PokéSurvivor is JPTER78’s game at pokesurvivor.com, and the game’s own login panel names pokesurvivor.com as the official website (checked 16 November 2026). Who runs other similarly named addresses, and whether their accounts or saves match, is not established, so check the full address before you sign in.

How do you play PokéSurvivor?

Move with WASD or the arrow keys (drag on a touch screen) and stay out of the crowd. Only your active move attacks, and it fires on its own; switch the active move with 1–4 or Q/E, and pause with Esc.

Can Pokémon evolve in PokéSurvivor?

Yes. At levels 12 and 24 the game asks whether your partner evolves or keeps its look; keeping the old look still raises its stats.

How do gacha tickets work in PokéSurvivor?

A ten-pull costs 10 ordinary tickets or one ×10 ticket, and a hundred-pull costs 100 ordinary tickets or ten ×10 tickets. The two balances are counted separately, and the rarity guarantee promises a five-star result, not a particular Pokémon.

Is there a PokéSurvivor APK for Android?

You do not need one: open pokesurvivor.com in your phone’s browser and start in landscape. A home-screen shortcut is not a native app, and native ports such as JayElDragon’s Poke Survivors are separate builds with their own rules and saves.

Is PokéSurvivor multiplayer?

Yes. The online game has a Friends menu for group play with up to four players. Both players need online accounts on the same game address; add your friend by their public nickname, then invite them to a room.

Does PokéSurvivor save guest progress?

No. The login panel says guest progress is not saved; signing in to an account saves your game in the cloud so you can continue from another computer. Read the save status before you change device.

Why is PokéSurvivor lagging or freezing?

First find what stopped. If only teammates lag, check the co-op host and room; if the clock and inputs freeze while grass still moves, note the time and upgrade you picked; if everything is slow, return an unusual window size to normal and lower the load.
