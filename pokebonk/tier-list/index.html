What this tier-list comparison measures

Editorial method: match a documented passive and kit to a concrete goal, then account for unlock cost and activation conditions. We recommend a role fit, not a universal best Pokémon. No controlled same-version benchmark, DPS simulation or population-wide win-rate sample was collected.

Do not treat the species dataset’s main-series base stats as a proven in-run damage score. A player’s permanent upgrades, move offers, difficulty and survival change the result. The 79-row reference below exposes its raw comparison fields and omits hidden numerical weights.

Eight role recommendations with a trade-off

NeedInspectWhy we suggest itTrade-off
Recovery openingBulbasaurGrass-hit healing, regen and healing bonuses support a forgiving sustain plan.The per-hit healing is rate-limited; it is not immunity to contact.
Defensive openingSquirtleArmor and shield are available without deliberately entering low HP.The damage boost is conditional; do not force it by taking hits.
Focused Fire planCharmanderA coherent Fire kit makes type-specific offers easier to evaluate.The low-HP damage condition adds risk; no faster-clear claim is made.
Ultimate/recovery alternativeClefairyIts listed regen and Ultimate-related recovery offer another sustain direction.Recovery depends partly on Ultimate timing, not only passive HP.
Contact mitigation alternativeGastlyListed damage reduction and evasion provide a different defensive mechanism.Mitigation is not the same as restoring HP after damage.
High-HP/healing alternativeChanseyIts kit emphasizes HP and healing, including a timed pickup mechanic.Unlock prerequisites and reaching the pickup still matter.
Later flexible pickMewtwoMultiple general bonuses and a periodic shield support flexible plans.Substantial League/capture/map gates make this a later option, not a first-start recommendation.
Later broad-move pickMewBroad stat bonuses and access to moves of any type expand planning.Requires the other 78 League members; offer flexibility does not prove the highest clear rate.

Make your own comparison useful

Keep map, difficulty, version and permanent upgrades fixed. Record the moves actually offered, when the run ended and why. Compare a role hypothesis—for example, whether sustain reduces your common chip-damage losses—before declaring a global strongest pick. This guide has not performed those runs.
