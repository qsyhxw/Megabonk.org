About the PokéPath TD guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 22 November 2026 · Play page checked: 1.7.2 (9 November 2026)

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style and Pokémon-themed games. The original game and its website come from other people: the original legal page ↗ names Khydra as the game developer and Nox as the site maintainer. We work with neither, and nothing here is endorsed by them or by the owners of the Pokémon names and artwork the game uses.

What it covers

The guide home sends you to the page for your next job: team roles and upgrade choices, phone limits and save transfer, Route 1-3 and Lunala and reward codes and redemption fields.

Where the answers come from

  • The original site’s own pages: the Pokédex ↗, bestiary, map pages, the device FAQ ↗, the non-shop unlock guide and the 1.7.2 patch notes ↗ supply unit abilities, enemy rules, save steps and listed codes.
  • The browser client: the profile-creation screen and the Route 1-1 tutorial were opened on 9 November 2026. A full route clear, a save transfer and a code redemption were not completed, and the pages say so where it matters.
  • Public player threads: Reddit discussions are used as symptom reports and labelled with the build they describe; they are not treated as proof of a working setup.
  • Images: the profile screenshot is our capture of the original client; the route terrain and Pokémon artwork come from the original project’s own pages, checked 9 November 2026.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was checked and the play-page version it was checked against. The section as a whole was last reviewed on 22 November 2026; the content was last checked against play page 1.7.2. The game changes abilities, items and attackers between patches, so the pages that depend on those numbers are rechecked after a new patch, and a page’s date changes only when its answer was confirmed again.

Which game this guide is about

These pages cover PokéPath TD, the route tower defense game at pokepath.gg. PokeBonk, PokéSurvivor, PokéRogue and PokéClicker are separate projects with their own progression, and other sites and hosts may add their own features to a copy of the same game, so guidance here follows the original site.

Send a correction

Send the page URL, the game version shown on your play page and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or the original site’s own statement are reviewed first.
