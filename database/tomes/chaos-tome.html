Megabonk Chaos Tome: Effect & Unlock

Chaos Tome increases one random stat every time it gains a level. Unlock it by fully charging every Charge Shrine through all three stages of a Tier 3 run without stepping out of a shrine's charge zone.

Current effectOne random stat boost per level
UnlockAll Charge Shrines, Tier 3, 3 stages
Max level99
Patch historyReward bonus +50% in v1.0.12

Current-data review: July 27, 2026. Silver Gain and Jump Height exclusions reflect v1.0.49.

Quick Answer

Chaos Tome is a high-variance flex Tome. Each upgrade selects one eligible stat at random and applies a bonus to that stat for the current run. It does not reroll your whole build, and you cannot pick the result. Use it after your build already has the guaranteed Damage, Attack Speed, Crit, survivability or economy stats it needs.

How the Random Mechanic Works

1. Gain a levelChoose or receive a Chaos Tome upgrade.
2. Roll a statOne eligible stat is selected randomly.
3. Set magnitudeThe offered upgrade quality affects the bonus size.
4. Apply itThe rolled bonus becomes part of this run.

Important: the v1.0.12 change increased Chaos Tome's reward bonus by 50%. It did not give a 50% chance to roll a better stat and did not let Luck select the result.

How to Unlock Chaos Tome

Start a Tier 3 run.Lower tiers do not satisfy the unlock requirement.
Find every Charge Shrine in Stage 1.Use the minimap and shrine counter, and sweep unexplored edges before leaving.
Fully charge each shrine without leaving its zone.Stepping outside during the charge breaks that attempt, so clear nearby threats first.
Repeat in Stages 2 and 3 in the same run.The requirement covers all three stages, not just one stage with every shrine found.
Finish the final required charge.Chaos Tome then enters the game's normal unlock progression.

Wrench speeds Shrine charging and improves Shrine rewards. Beacon adds Charge Shrines to later stages, so taking it can increase the number you must find and charge during this unlock attempt.

Best Builds for Chaos Tome

Chaos Tome is strongest when many different results are useful. It is weaker when a challenge or speedrun depends on hitting one exact stat on schedule.

More capacity does not mean Chaos Tome is automatically the next pick. A reliable dedicated Tome is usually better when your build still lacks a required breakpoint.

Common Mistakes

  • Leaving the charge circle: clear enemies first and stay inside until the shrine completes.
  • Missing one shrine: check the map and stage counter before entering the portal.
  • Doing the route below Tier 3: the complete three-stage route must be done at Tier 3.
  • Using the old reroll requirement: rerolling upgrade offers 100 times is not the current unlock.
  • Building around one hoped-for roll: Chaos Tome provides variance, not a targeted substitute for a dedicated Tome.

Chaos Tome FAQ

What does Chaos Tome do?

Each upgrade increases one randomly selected eligible stat for the current run. It has a maximum level of 99.

How do I unlock Chaos Tome?

Fully charge every Charge Shrine in all three stages of one Tier 3 run, staying inside each shrine's charge zone until it completes.

Can it still roll Silver Gain or Jump Height?

No. Both were removed from the current result pool in v1.0.49.

Does Luck choose a better Chaos Tome stat?

No. Luck can improve upgrade quality, but the selected Chaos Tome stat remains random.

Is Chaos Tome always worth taking?

No. It is a useful flex choice for builds that benefit from many stats, but a dedicated Tome is more reliable when you need a specific breakpoint.
