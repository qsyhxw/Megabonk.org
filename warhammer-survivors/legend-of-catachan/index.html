WARHAMMER SURVIVORS · PLAYER GUIDE

Unlock the Legend of Catachan

Get the Signum Array relic on Ork Stronghold, then rescue him in the top-right of Imperial Bastion and buy him for 1,020 coins. All three steps are in the Warhammer 40,000 universe.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 12 November 2026 · Release v2.1

Unlock routeFrag Grenade bugDemo progress

Unlock route

  1. Unlock Ork Stronghold by reaching level 40 on Gallowdark.
  2. Pick up the Signum Array. Start Ork Stronghold with any character, pause, open the Map tab and head to the green ? in the bottom-right corner. The relic adds extra markers to every 40K map.
  3. Rescue him on Imperial Bastion. With the relic collected, a new ? appears near the top-right of the Imperial Bastion map. Travel north-east, kill the enemies around him and walk up to him; he joins your run. The Heavy Metal modifier speeds up the walk.
  4. Finish the run, then buy him in Character Select for 1,020 coins. The objective "Find and rescue the character on Imperial Bastion" completes at the end of the run.

Stats: 108 Wounds, +20% Strength, +20% Sustain, +25% Tithe, +16% Move, +4 Skip. His passive applies Battleshocked to nearby enemies when his Wounds are critically low, and he finds more explosives.

Frag Grenade and the rescued-character bug

His starting weapon is Frag Grenade. Steam hotfix v2.1.6, published 11 November, fixes a rescued Legend incorrectly using a Chainsword. The developer schedules the changes on other platforms from the following week. Check your installed version if the rescued ally still carries the wrong weapon.

For the Frag Grenade evolution, match Bandolier in the recipe finder. The companion unlock and the evolution attempt are separate checks.

If you already played him in the demo

The October demo offered the Legend as a playable hero. Demo progress does not transfer into the released game, so rescue him in your full-game save.

Continue with first-run planning or Secrets (the catachandevil incantation is an alternative unlock).
