Contact the D2 Survivors guide editors

Reviewed 3 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A setup, save or menu step that no longer matches the original’s current build;
  • A host or build label on our Play page that has changed;
  • A wrong skill, stat or Druid rule, with the screen where you saw it;
  • A broken link, image or translation on a D2 Survivors page.

Include the page URL, the host (original, our Play window or another site), the build shown on the menu and your browser or operating system. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not framara or game support, so we cannot recover a lost save, read the storage of another website, install tools for you or change how the game works. For those, use the project’s own FAQ ↗ and keep the backups described in the saves guide.
