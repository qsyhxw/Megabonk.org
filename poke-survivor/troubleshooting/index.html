PokéSurvivor not working: freezes, lag and loading

Most stutter and freezes go away with lower graphics settings. Reload pokesurvivor.com in its own tab, then set Quality to Low and Pokémon effects to “Only my partner”.

PokéSurvivor guide · Updated November 16, 2026

  1. Open pokesurvivor.com in its own browser tab and wait for the title screen.
  2. Settings → Graphics: set Quality to Low (or Auto), Pokémon effects and Ground trails to “Only my partner”.
  3. Turn on Show FPS; in co-op also Show ping to see whether it is your device or the connection.
  4. On a phone, hold it in landscape, close other tabs and set an FPS limit of 60 or 30.
  5. If a run is stuck on a choice screen, reload: progress from finished runs is saved with an account.

Stuck runs and rooms

  • “X is choosing…” forever in co-op: fixed 14 November; reload if it still happens.
  • The host left mid-run: the run now continues for the others.
  • Tickets from a run that closed halfway arrive when you come back.
  • Saves and missing progress: save and sync checks; co-op setup: friends and co-op.

Report a bug to the creator

Use the Support and Discord links inside the game menu at pokesurvivor.com. Give your browser and device, the run time on the screen and what you pressed just before the problem.
