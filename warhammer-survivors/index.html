Before you start

Demo progress, platforms and launch-day questions.

Try the PC demo & understand save transfers
  1. On Windows PC, open the official Steam page ↗ (opens in a new tab) and look for Download Warhammer Survivors Demo. The full game and the demo are separate choices.
  2. For Xbox PC, use the Xbox demo listing ↗ (opens in a new tab). Check the device compatibility on that listing.
  3. Start the current October demo if you want to judge the controls and solo combat. Keep any recipe notes tied to that build.

Warhammer Community names Steam and Xbox PC as demo routes. Its console launch list does not establish that every console has a demo. Warhammer Community demo announcement ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The earlier demo's progress does not carry into the updated demo, and this demo's progress does not carry into the full game. For permanent progression, begin again in the released game. Auroch October 6 release announcement ↗ (opens in a new tab)

iPhone & iPad availability

The developer-linked App Store listing is by Auroch Digital Ltd. The US listing showed $7.19 on 11 November, alongside the developer's $7.99 base price and platform-dependent launch discount. Check your local storefront price before purchase. Apple App Store listing ↗ (opens in a new tab)

  1. Open the listing on the device you plan to use.
  2. Expand Compatibility and compare its iOS or iPadOS requirement with Settings → General → About. The current listing requires version 15.0 or later.
  3. Confirm that your device and store region offer the purchase, then check available storage and the current download size.

Apple lists iPhone and iPad support. Cross-store save transfer and device-specific performance have not been verified here. If continuing a PC save is essential, confirm that support before buying a second copy.

Tor Garadon & launch content

Arka Sarkar's launch guide reports 10,000 enemies defeated across multiple runs as Tor Garadon's unlock condition. Check the 40K Objectives entry and cumulative progress between runs. A single-run kill target would lead you to spend effort on a different task. The guide does not provide a recommended Tor build or a precise claim about which other modes contribute. Destructoid character unlock report, 10 November ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Launch issues & support

After seeing the first chest of each rarity, the developer says you can skip that rarity's animation using right-click, Esc or gamepad B. On Switch 2 with a Switch Pro Controller, use the joystick if menu d-pad input fails. These checks address the two symptoms documented in the 10 November FAQ. For the separate Failed to load il2cpp error, consult the developer's current instructions and report form before changing Windows security settings. Official known issues and reporting form ↗ (opens in a new tab)

The game, its developers & the two universes

Auroch Digital develops Warhammer Survivors in partnership with Warhammer and poncle. It is a standalone purchase. Existing Vampire Survivors ownership does not supply this game's license. The developer describes a game built on the Vampire Survivors foundation, with its own champions, weapons, stages and secrets. Developer game FAQ ↗ (opens in a new tab)

Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar are represented here. Select the universe that matches your run when using the reported recipe table. Several similar-looking weapon names lead to different recipes.
