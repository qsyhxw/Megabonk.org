PokéSurvivor save and sync: keep your progress

Progress is only kept with an account. If the menu says “Guest mode: not saved”, sign up before you close the game; then log in with the same account on any other device.

PokéSurvivor guide · Updated November 16, 2026

  1. Look at the partner card in the menu: “Guest mode: not saved” means nothing is kept when you leave. Press Sign up or log in now.
  2. Finish the current run normally: coins and tickets from a run arrive when it ends.
  3. On the second device, open pokesurvivor.com and log in with the same name or Google account.
  4. Compare the Pokémon count (for example 1 / 1082) and coins before buying anything.
  5. Stay online while you buy upgrades, pull the gacha or hatch eggs: with an account these need a connection.

Protect your account

  • Link a Gmail account when the game asks “Afraid of losing your account?”, so you can log in with Google if you forget your password.
  • The login panel has a “Forgot your password?” link.
  • Do not play the same account in two tabs while buying; the 14 November update fixed items lost that way.
  • Never share your password; the creator will not ask you to pay.
