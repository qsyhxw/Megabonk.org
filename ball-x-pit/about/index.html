About the Ball x Pit guides

Reviewed 21 November 2026

This is an unofficial guide section for players of Ball x Pit, written by the Megabonk.org editors. It is not made by Kenny Sun and Friends or Devolver Digital, and it is not game support.

Sources: the Steam store page and update notes, the official site, and attributed community recipe charts and reports. Each guide lists the sources it relies on and the date we checked them. We have not benchmarked builds or played every route, and we say so where it matters.

Checked against the Naturalist update (6 Aug 2026) and the Risen Ballbylon store listing on 21 Nov 2026. The recipe tools cover the base game and the free updates; Risen Ballbylon recipes are not included yet.

Covered here: evolutions and passive evolutions, new recipes from the free updates, builds, the character tier list and unlocks, Ghostly Shield, the trophy checklist and a beginner guide. To suggest a correction, send the page URL and a public source through the contact page. Game names belong to their owners.
