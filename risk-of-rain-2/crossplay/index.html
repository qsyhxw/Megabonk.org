Risk of Rain 2 crossplay: can your copies connect?

Steam and Epic can play together on PC, but Steam crossplay settings must match the session. Console generations have separate limits. Choose both copies below; a shared game title or room code does not settle compatibility. [1][2]

Connect Steam and Epic

  1. On Steam, open the game and check its crossplay preference in settings or the first multiplayer prompt. Choose ON when an Epic player is joining.
  2. Restart after changing that preference. Other Steam friends must also opt in to join that crossplay session.
  3. To invite Epic friends, use the account-linking choice in Options. The publisher describes a browser step linking Steam and Epic accounts.
  4. Use the in-game invitation, Epic overlay, or the other player’s copied Lobby ID where that lobby is public.

These are the publisher’s instructions, updated 4 September 2025 and rechecked 5 November 2026. They establish the supported route, not a guarantee that a particular network session works. [1]

If only Steam friends are playing, both may choose OFF instead. Turning it off on one PC while the other stays ON separates them; turning it off also removes the Steam-to-Epic route. Treat “disable crossplay” advice according to who actually needs to join. [1]

PS4, Switch and newer consoles

2K’s console notice, updated 8 October 2026, says future content and updates focus on PC, PS5 and Xbox Series X|S. Xbox One, PS4 and the original Switch can still play the content they have, but a newer DLC appearing in a bundle does not establish that it runs on an older console. [2]

The same notice says PS4–PS5 and Xbox One–Series multiplayer will separate with the next console update, while retaining conditional wording about when it happens. Check the actual installed version on both machines before planning cross-generation play. We have not tested console matchmaking or confirmed the rollout on each platform. [2]

For PlayStation, the official product page grants PS4 and PS5 versions to the purchaser’s same PSN account. That entitlement is different from two players being able to connect across versions. [3]

A compatible pair still cannot join?

  1. Confirm the store and installed edition on both devices. A Windows PC does not identify its storefront.
  2. Compare Steam settings, restart after changes, then try the publisher’s lobby/invite route again.
  3. Record both versions and the exact point of failure: missing lobby, failed join or disconnect after the run begins. Those are different symptoms.
  4. If the documented pair still fails, send those details to 2K · support request ↗. Avoid deleting saves or removing game DLLs based on an unrelated old post.

This page gives confirmed compatibility checks, not a universal disconnect fix. The check does not install mods or change your game. [1]
