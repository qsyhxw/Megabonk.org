Prepare a team without importing solo milestones

Select Survival in a co-op room using a registered account. Team Survival uses a shared boss every four minutes and returns fallen players when the surviving team beats the next boss. It is separate from solo Survival’s five-minute schedule.[1][2]

Checked October 29, 2026 · Beta v0.8.75 · Source-based guide

Prepare the room and the declarations

  1. Check that each trainer can access current co-op with an account.
  2. Choose a friends’ room or public matchmaking, then Survival, team size, map and difficulty.
  3. Agree who calls objectives and use the G / L3 signal wheel for locations or danger. The crowned public leader starts the room.
  4. Save each trainer’s own declared plan. This website does not synchronize a live team.

Check the Team Survival fusion-machine interval

Enter elapsed time; no live game clock is read.

The .72 notes say Bill’s Machine works again eleven minutes after use. The check below only evaluates elapsed time. It cannot prove a machine is present, usable in your current state or offered with the right ingredients. Fusion ingredients and slot choices are still checked separately.

[2]

Make the next attempt answer a specific problem

Choose practical responsibilities before starting: objective calls, watching incoming pressure and signaling recovery or movement. These are planning suggestions, not mandatory game classes. If a run ends, record whether the problem was an unmet mechanic, a missed signal, an unavailable item or an unhelpful kit before replacing the whole build.

The release names Cataclysm and Apocalypse, but this guide does not invent unverified stage timings or claim a completed team clear.
