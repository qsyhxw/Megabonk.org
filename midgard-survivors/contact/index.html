Contact the Midgard Survivors guide editors

Reviewed 12 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A download, Android, Mac or co-op step that no longer matches the current client;
  • A changed platform file, renderer option or update note on the developer’s pages;
  • A gear or progression rule that differs in your build, with the screen where you saw it;
  • A broken link, image or wrong game identification on a Midgard Survivors page.

Include the page URL, your device or operating system, the client version shown in the launcher and what happened. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not the developer or game support, so we cannot recover a lost save, fix an account or change how the game works. For those, use the developer’s own channels linked from the itch.io listing ↗ (opens in a new tab).
