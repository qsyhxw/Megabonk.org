About the Survivor Ace guides

Updated 2026-11-21

Who these guides are for

These are independent, unofficial guides to Survivor: Ace, the mobile aerial roguelike that Google Play lists under Gorilla Studio. We are not Gorilla Studio, its publisher or its game support, and we cannot access or restore accounts.

Where the information comes from

The game’s identity, platforms and price model come from its official website and store listing. Weapon, upgrade and equipment decisions are compared across published beginner guides and gameplay accounts in Japanese, Traditional Chinese and English. Code lists come from public Taiwanese and Japanese articles. When two sources describe different editions or give different rewards, the page says which source says what instead of picking one silently.

What was checked, and when

Store listing and official links: November 21, 2026. Guide and code sources: compared between October 22 and October 30, 2026. None of the sources states a game build number, and we have not redeemed any code, so every code is labeled as reported, not verified.

What this section covers

  • Survivor: Ace hub: first-run steps, weapon-choice checklist, run picks versus equipment.
  • 繁中新手攻略 and 日本語・序盤攻略: beginner decisions in each language.
  • 繁中禮包碼 and 日本語・コード: reported codes, deadlines and where to enter them.

Corrections

Found an outdated code, a weapon description that does not match your edition or a wrong translation? Tell us on the contact page and include the page URL and your region.
