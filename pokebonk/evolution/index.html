PokeBonk evolution, explained

Use the League’s run-evolution thresholds rather than main-series levels or stone requirements. The current Season 2 Eevee entry offers Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon at level 36; completing a run evolution is separate from permanently unlocking a League partner.

Read your next evolution before spending upgrades

  1. Check the starting League partner, not a different form’s profile.
  2. Read the next-evolution indicator during the run.
  3. Earn run experience until its displayed threshold. A branch opens a choice rather than requiring a random reset.
  4. After evolving, inspect the new form and its Ultimate stage.

Choose among the current Eevee branches

The current League entry lists Vaporeon, Jolteon and Flareon at level 36. Raw all-generation files can contain later forms that are not active Season 2 choices. Do not treat an unfiltered species file as the playable branch list.

How the thresholds and baselines are derived

For a first evolution that still has a later form, the client adds 8 to the next form’s raw level and clamps to 16–30. For a final evolution, it rounds 22 + 0.6 × the raw level and clamps to 36–50. League overrides take priority. A later evolution has a minimum three-level gap.

The lookup filters species through the current Season 2 set before deriving routes. Same-form growth at levels 24 and 44 can increase HP and damage for partners without a remaining evolution; it does not create another species. Form baselines and growth are different from a completed gameplay test.

Keep run growth and League progress separate

Evolution changes the partner in the current run. A League unlock uses its challenge and capture process. If the evolution indicator does not match this dated snapshot, record the host and version before applying another guide’s rule.
