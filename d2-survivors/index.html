About D2 Survivors

D2 Survivors is a fan-made survival game: pick a Diablo II hero, move through the horde and let skills attack on their own while each level-up shapes the next upgrade. The checked original menu displayed build 20261102.2 and a class selector, while current reference pages also describe a camp and waypoint; the beginner guide covers both so you do not hunt an older menu for a newer control.

The original offers browser and desktop routes. Its setup warns that temporary hosted files are going away, and its FAQ currently recommends a computer; older phone reports can describe a different transition state. A mobile app request, a home-screen shortcut and a verified native app are different things. Choose the supported setup path.

FAQ

Quick answers; each links to the full guide.

Is D2 Survivors the same as Diablo II or Diablo II: Resurrected?

No. It is an unofficial fan project by framara that takes its look from Diablo II. It is not made or endorsed by Blizzard, and it is not a patch, remake or version of Diablo II or D2R.

Full guide →
Do I need my own Diablo II files to play?

For the original, yes: prepare your own Diablo II: Lord of Destruction 1.14 or Diablo II: Resurrected installation. The Play window on this site is a separate third-party edition, so it does not show what the original needs.

Full guide →
Can I play D2 Survivors on a phone?

The original's FAQ, checked 3 November 2026, says phones are not currently supported and recommends a computer. Older mobile-bookmark reports describe an earlier state of the project.

Full guide →
Why does my imported save show a new game?

Go back to the same host and browser profile that made the save, then check the class and Softcore or Hardcore mode; another website does not see it. Export a backup from both sides before importing anything.

Full guide →
How do I stop a skill from casting?

During a run, right-click the skill slot to rest it and right-click again to enable it. The slot and learned level stay. Resting is not a respec; a rebuild goes through Akara's Re-draft after that hero's first Baal kill.

Full guide →
Do Diablo II attack speed breakpoints apply?

No. In the checked original reference each point of item Increased Attack Speed adds half a point of Faster Cast Rate, and there are no D2R-style breakpoints. Compare the displayed total and the affected skill.

Full guide →
Is there a Chinese or Korean version?

The original offers English and Korean. Traditional Chinese comes from a community-maintained tool rather than a built-in language, and both have their own page here.

Full guide →
Is the Play window the same game as the original?

No. It loads play.d2survivors.org, a third-party edition whose menu and run show older build labels than the original's 20261102.2. Its progress is separate from the original's.
