WARHAMMER SURVIVORS · PLAYER GUIDE

Warhammer Survivors tips for your first runs

Start as Sister Superior Amalia Novena, spend coins on Strength in Enhancements, and aim every run at one Objective. Max one weapon early and hold its companion passive so the first boss chest evolves it.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 12 November 2026 · Release v2.1

First hour: what to do

  1. Buy Strength first in Enhancements: +4% damage per rank (max +40%), and ranks count in both universes. Refund All is free, so you can respec any time.
  2. Play Amalia Novena early. She gains +10% Range every 10 levels (max +30% at level 30), and surviving 5 minutes with her unlocks Shield of Faith.
  3. Clear the cheap Objectives: survive 1 minute (Tape Measure), survive 10 minutes (Hourglass), take 250 damage (Glue).
  4. Evolve one weapon per run: max it, hold its companion and open the next boss chest (all 38 recipes, best builds).

Choose a run with a concrete goal

  1. Open Objectives in the universe you plan to play. Read whether progress is cumulative or must happen in one run.
  2. For an early survival milestone, choose a character you already control reliably. Surviving one minute with any character unlocks Tape Measure.
  3. For a rescue goal, choose the named stage. Catachan needs an Imperial Bastion rescue.
  4. After the attempt, check completion and available rewards before repeating it.

Check unlocks before planning an evolution

Choose one target pair in the evolution finder and filter the universe. If its passive is locked, make that unlock your immediate goal. For Dice Cube, follow the universe-specific objective. Once both items are available, follow the weapon-upgrade and chest checks in the recipe guide.

For a follower goal, the two-follower route explains the required weapons and prerequisites. Followers supply attacks during a solo run. For human player count, see co-op status.

Check the platform before applying a fix

Steam hotfix v2.1.6 makes chest sequences skippable with any button and fixes unreachable chest drops on Imperial Bastion and Gallowdark. Its 11 November announcement schedules other platforms from the following week. Developer patch notes

Keep your platform and installed version with a bug report. Check Switch 2 access and controls or Secrets menu access for your next task.
