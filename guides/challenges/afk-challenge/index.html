Megabonk AFK Gaming Challenge Guide

Reach the Final Swarm on Forest Tier 1 while the Challenge disables movement and jumping. You may still aim, let weapons attack, and choose upgrades; the real test is building damage, pickup range and survival without repositioning.

Quick Answer

How to complete AFK Gaming

Select AFK Gaming on Forest Tier 1, start the run, and survive until the Final Swarm. Movement and jump controls are disabled. You can rotate or aim with the mouse, use menus, select upgrades and let weapons attack automatically.

No portal boss required

The objective is reaching the Final Swarm. Do not plan around finding or entering the boss portal because you cannot route across the map.

AFK Challenge Rules: What Is Allowed?

The in-game rule is “No moving or jumping allowed.” AFK does not mean you must stop touching every input; it means your character cannot use movement or jump controls.

Defeat a portal bossNot requiredThe completion goal is reaching the Final Swarm, not clearing the portal encounter.

Do not confuse “AFK Gaming” with a hands-off run

You should still watch the early offers, aim when a directional weapon benefits, and make deliberate upgrade choices. The Challenge removes navigation, not decision-making.

AFK Challenge Setup Priority

This is the minimum decision order needed to pass the Challenge. For a full low-input build with weapon alternatives and long-run scaling, use the separate Semi-AFK Build page.

Secure pickup range

Attraction Tome is the cleanest early answer. Without pickup range, nearby kills do not translate into enough levels.

Build local coverage

Aura and Aegis work without navigation. Add Cooldown or Attack Speed only when it improves the weapons actually carrying the run.

Add one survival layer

Shield, Armor, recovery or a compatible defensive weapon keeps one bad wave from ending the run. Do not split early upgrades across too many weak options.

Why the AFK Challenge Fails

You stop leveling

Pickup range arrived too late or kills happen outside collection range. Prioritize Attraction Tome and avoid expanding the kill radius far beyond your collection radius too early.

The first dangerous wave reaches you

Your early offers were spread across too many weapons or utility stats. Concentrate upgrades into one coverage weapon plus one defensive layer.

A directional weapon misses

Rotate and aim with the mouse. Aiming is allowed; only movement and jumping are disabled.

You are planning for the portal

The Challenge ends at the Final Swarm. You do not need to move to the portal or kill the portal boss.

You keep restarting too late

If the opening levels provide neither pickup range nor enough local damage, restarting early saves more time than watching an under-leveled run collapse.

Enemies approach from bad terrain

A wall can reduce approach angles, but cliffs and edges can also drop enemies onto you. Prefer a clear starting area over a gimmick that depends on enemy pathing.

AFK Gaming Challenge FAQ

What exactly completes the AFK Gaming Challenge?

Reach the Final Swarm on Forest Tier 1 with AFK Gaming active. The listed objective does not require finding the portal or defeating a portal boss.

Can you use the mouse?

Yes. You can rotate and aim with the mouse, and you can select level-up choices or other menu options. Movement and jumping are the restricted controls.

Can weapons attack?

Yes. Automatic attacks continue normally, and aim-dependent weapons can still be directed with the mouse. Passive or wide-coverage weapons are simply easier to manage while stationary.

Is Attraction Tome mandatory?

It is not part of the Challenge rule, but pickup range is usually the most important early requirement because you cannot walk to XP. An equivalent source of reliable collection range can fill the same role.

Is Aura + Aegis the only valid setup?

No. It is a reliable low-input core, not a mandatory combination. The winning requirements are enough nearby damage, enough XP collection and enough survival to reach the Final Swarm.

Does a Semi-AFK Build automatically complete the Challenge?

No. Semi-AFK Build guides assume you may reposition for drops, chests or bosses. AFK Gaming disables those movement choices, so the early upgrade plan must work from the starting position.
