Turn your first runs into permanent progress

Use early runs to fund permanent upgrades and learn one focused damage plan. After a clear, choose whether you need a safe return to spend resources or a harder continuation; you do not have to push a deeper mode every time.

What to inspect before spending

  1. Open the global Skill Tree and read affordable nodes that solve a present problem. Distinguish account-wide effects from a particular character’s progression.
  2. Check the weapon screen for requirements and materials. A weapon is useful when its skill and bonuses fit the route you want to try, not merely because it is new.
  3. Review the runes you actually own. Copying a guide that depends on a locked rune can hide the reason its skills work.
  4. Keep enough information to compare runs: character, weapon, mode, Curse setting and the main skill.

The official skill-tree screenshot documents the interface, not a recommended node path. Its displayed counts and values are promotional-state data; use your own game’s text. (Game Smithing · Official game website ↗)

Pick a first-clear goal

Aim to finish a run with a damage core you can describe. Start on a difficulty where you can learn enemy warning areas and keep a route through the crowd. When you repeatedly reach the same boss safely, increase one challenge at a time. If you keep dying before the build forms, permanent upgrades and a simpler setup are more useful than copying a late-game damage record.

For skill-slot decisions, use the build checklist. For the Frozen Wastelands circles, use the separate Bumble route; that is a hidden-task procedure, not a requirement for every normal clear.

At the portals: choose by your next task

Read each portal label and decide whether you are banking progress or continuing into a mode with different demands. Choose a normal return when your purpose is spending materials or changing equipment. Choose a continuation when your current setup is stable and that mode advances the unlock you need. We do not attach a universal time threshold to all versions or modes.

The 2025 beginner recommendations are useful context, but their character preferences are not a current strength ranking. The right next step depends on your unlocks and the task you are completing.
