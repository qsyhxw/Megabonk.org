Contact the PokéSurvivor guide editors

Updated November 16, 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A ticket cost, rift timing or Judgment of Arceus rule that differs in your game;
  • Controls, menus or level-up cards that no longer match a guide;
  • A broken link, image or the Play page not loading on a PokéSurvivor page.

Include the page address, the game address you play on (pokesurvivor.com or another edition), your device and browser, and a screenshot of the menu or card. Rights holders can use the same address for image or name requests.

What we cannot help with

We are not JPTER78 or game support, so we cannot restore accounts, recover progress or fix game servers. For the original game, use the links in its own menu at pokesurvivor.com; other hosted editions and ports have their own maintainers. Our save and sync guide explains what to check before you change device. Do not send passwords, account tokens or cookies. There is no promised reply time; the PokéSurvivor privacy notice explains how email is handled.
