About the Revenant Survivors guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 22 November 2026 · Game version checked: update 0.6.4

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not Mongma Studio, who makes Revenant Survivors, and nothing here is endorsed by the studio.

What it covers

The guide home sends you to the page for your next job: moving a Demo save, retained equipment and the Forge, Hard Mode unlocks and rewards, controller and auto-attack checks and reported fixes for freezes after a run.

Where the answers come from

  • The developer’s announcements and replies: update notes, the launch announcement and the studio’s answers in public threads on the Steam Community hub ↗ supply the save-transfer sequence, progression rules and fix lists.
  • The store listings: the full game ↗ and free Demo ↗ pages supply the edition split, release stage and controller support listing.
  • Public player reports: older playtest reports are kept apart from the current release and labelled with the version they describe.
  • Images: the gameplay images are Mongma Studio’s Steam promotional screenshots with the capture build unspecified. The guides rely on the sources above rather than on our own complete playthroughs.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was checked; the section as a whole was last reviewed on 22 November 2026, against update 0.6.4 of 17 November 2026. When the studio ships a new update, the pages that depend on its unlock rules, rewards or fixes are rechecked and the date on each page changes only when its answer was confirmed again.

Which game this guide is about

These pages cover Revenant Survivors from Mongma Studio, Steam app 4617500, in both its full Early Access release and its separate free Demo. Advice written for one edition is marked as such, because the two have their own version numbers.

Send a correction

Send the page URL, your edition and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or the developer’s own statement are reviewed first.
