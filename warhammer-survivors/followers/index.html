How to get two followers in Warhammer Survivors

On the 40K side, try Malum Caedo with the unlocked Lasgun and Shield of Faith. These weapons bring their users into your squad. Having two followers unlocks Bugman’s Tankard. Keep the two-follower objective separate from the later five-follower target.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 11 November 2026 · Release v2.1

Prepare the two weapon choices

  1. Rescue Trooper Bartosz Kozlowski on Imperial Bastion and unlock his Lasgun for future runs.
  2. Unlock Sister Superior Amalia Novena in Character Select.
  3. Unlock Shield of Faith by surviving five minutes with Amalia (Sister Superior). Check the associated Objective after that run.
  4. Start a new 40K run as Malum Caedo. Take Lasgun and Shield of Faith when they are offered.

Check the two-follower objective

Take Lasgun (recruits Kozlowski) and Shield of Faith (recruits Amalia). Check your follower count after taking both. If one weapon has not appeared, check its unlock Objective first. A completed unlock makes the item eligible for future choices rather than guaranteeing the next offered selection.

Bugman’s Tankard is listed against the requirement to have two followers. Check that objective in your selected universe after reaching the count. Age of Sigmar has its own follower objective.

Extend the route toward five followers

For five followers, keep the same Malum Caedo loadout and upgrade Lasgun to level 6: Kozlowski’s extra soldiers plus Amalia complete the target and unlock Figure Case. Use the displayed objective count to distinguish the initial two recruits from the later expanded squad.

Use the unlocked companion in a recipe

Bugman’s Tankard evolves Shield of Faith in 40K and Nebulous Sea Fog in Age of Sigmar. Use the evolution table for your selected universe.

For human player count, open the co-op guide. For missing Dice Cube, use the universe-specific unlock checks.
