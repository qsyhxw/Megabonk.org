About this game window

PokéRogue is a fan-made, turn-based Pokémon roguelite by the Pagefault Games project. This independent page loads the original pokerogue.net ↗ (opens in a new tab) client; Megabonk.org does not operate the game, accounts or saves. It is a different game from PokeBonk and other similarly named Pokémon projects.

Checked 6 November 2026 against the original client and project repository ↗ (opens in a new tab). The game loads only after your click and may change independently of these guides. Pokémon names and artwork belong to their respective owners.
