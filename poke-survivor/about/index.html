About these guides

Updated October 26, 2026

This is the PokéSurvivor guide section of Megabonk.org, maintained by this website’s independent publisher. We help readers identify JPTER78’s browser game and make practical choices about controls, moves, tickets, devices, saves and co-op.

Our method

Guides use the creator’s public pages, fixed source revisions and dated original player reports. Creator pages and source files are one author’s evidence, not independent double confirmation. Data transformations explain their method and avoid presenting base-stat fields as measured win rates.

The October 26 review uses snapshot 680c3e12c670. A title-screen inspection is recorded separately from play. We did not complete controlled runs, physical-phone benchmarks, multiplayer sessions or account recovery. Advice keeps those limits beside the relevant claim.

Separate projects and rights

This website is not run by Jonathan Piera Torregrosa (JPTER78), Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company. PokeBonk and Megabonk have separate facts and guides. Game images and names belong to their respective rights holders. Our beacon/keyway mark identifies this guide, not the game.

Corrections

Use the contact page with the guide URL, game edition and supporting source. We can correct this website; we cannot change the game or access player accounts. Our privacy notice explains website services.
