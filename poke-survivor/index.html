First, the right project

PokeSurvivor is its own game.

This section covers JPTER78’s PokéSurvivor at pokesurvivor.com. PokeBonk is Charcher’s different browser game; Megabonk is a different Steam game. Controls, accounts, partners and build recipes do not transfer between them.[3]

Looking for Pokémon Survivor Burst? ClessioTV’s separate project has its own download and progression. Use ClessioTV’s Eevee Expo project page ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab), rather than applying this browser guide to it.[4]

Other similar domains, including the Doofcraft address in search queries, are not established here as the author’s original edition. Its operator, build and save compatibility were not verified. Check the full address before signing in; this guide does not manage game accounts. Our Play page loads a third-party edition from pokesurvivor.pro; its accounts, saves and rules must be checked separately.
