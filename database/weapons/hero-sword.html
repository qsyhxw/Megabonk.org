Megabonk Hero Sword: Effect & Unlock

Hero Sword slashes through multiple enemies and fires a piercing ranged slash. Defeat a Stage Boss with no ground Items, no temporary powerups and no Shrine use, then purchase it for 30 Silver.

EffectMulti-enemy swing + ranged slash
ChallengeNo Items, powerups or Shrine use
AllowedXP, weapons and Tomes from levels
Purchase30 Silver after completion

Current-data review: July 27, 2026. Also searched as Hero's Sword and Heros Sword.

Quick Answer

Kill any Stage Boss without collecting a ground Item or temporary powerup and without using a Shrine. You may collect XP, Gold and normal weapon or Tome choices from leveling. After the objective completes, open Unlocks and buy Hero Sword for 30 Silver.

Safest interpretation: never open a chest, buy from a vendor or walk through a temporary powerup. Walking past an unused Shrine is fine; activating it is not.

Hero Sword Effect and Current Behavior

Forward melee swing

The front arc can damage multiple enemies, giving it real close-range pack coverage.

Ranged slash

Each attack also fires a piercing slash projectile for mid-range coverage.

Upgrade stats

Damage, Projectile Count, Projectile Speed and Size are its native weapon upgrades.

v1.0.17 fix

High Size now scales correctly with the ranged slash's travel behavior instead of making the projectile incorrectly slow.

Base values: Damage 11, Projectile Count 1, Projectile Speed 30 and Size 100%. Common upgrades add Damage +2, Projectile Count +1, Projectile Speed +4 or Size +15%.

Why the Unlock Did Not Register

  • You touched a temporary powerup hidden inside an XP pile.
  • You activated or completed a Shrine, even though it did not award an Item.
  • You accepted a chest or vendor Item; collecting it is separate from collecting a powerup.
  • You broke a pot and automatically collected the powerup that appeared.
  • You killed the Boss correctly but forgot that Hero Sword still needs to be bought from Unlocks.

Hero Sword FAQ

How do I unlock Hero Sword?

Defeat any Stage Boss without ground Items, temporary powerups or Shrine use. XP and level-up weapons or Tomes are allowed. Then purchase it for 30 Silver.

Can I collect XP or Gold?

Yes. XP, normal level-up choices and Gold are allowed.

Can I break pots?

Breaking a pot is allowed, but collecting a spawned powerup fails the attempt. Avoiding pots is the safer route.

Can I use a Charge Shrine or Boss Curse?

No. The restriction covers Shrine use, including Charge, Greed, Succ, Moai, Challenge and Boss Curse interactions.

Does Sticks & Stones complete the challenge automatically?

No. It helps remove Item temptation, but you must still avoid all temporary powerups and every Shrine interaction.

What does Hero Sword do?

It hits multiple enemies with a forward melee slash and also launches a piercing ranged slash projectile.
