Check the phone route before moving your save

Try the original wow.aethus.pl in a current WebGPU-capable browser on your phone or tablet. A browser name or OS version alone does not guarantee a usable graphics adapter or successful play. Export your existing save before switching clients.

Winlator closes at startup: separate the failure layers

A Windows executable closing in Winlator is a different failure from the original browser reporting no WebGPU. In the starsforfeelings thread, Zigarahz reports a one-second launch followed by closure on a Samsung Fold 7; doctor-ase reports the same on an S25+. These are two replies in one thread, not two independent compatibility tests. Re-read 2 November 2026; the page and search-cache dates disagree, so exact comment dates are unconfirmed.

Browser checks, in order

  1. Open Aethus’s original game ↗ directly, outside an embedded frame.
  2. If the page reports no WebGPU, update the browser and inspect whether the device exposes the required GPU feature. Do not use a PC-only flag as a blanket phone fix.
  3. Let the initial download complete; note whether its progress counter moves. A server quota message is a different branch from a GPU warning.
  4. Inspect the actual touch/input controls before committing to a long run. A larger display does not prove that all inputs work on your device.
  5. If the original browser route is unsuitable, retain the PC save and check current original-author/community guidance for your device. Do not install a purported mobile port just because it uses the game’s name.

Your PC and phone do not share storage automatically

Export from the client where your progress exists; export any phone progress separately before importing. Import replaces the destination. Keep both original files until heroes, equipment and zone unlocks match your intention. Browser addon installation is separate again.
