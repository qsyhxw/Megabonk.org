Check the phone route before moving your save

Try the original wow.aethus.pl in a current WebGPU-capable browser on your phone or tablet. A browser name or OS version alone does not guarantee a usable graphics adapter or successful play. Export your existing save before switching clients.[1]

Browser checks, in order

  1. Open Aethus’s original game ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab) directly, outside an embedded frame.
  2. If the page reports no WebGPU, update the browser and inspect whether the device exposes the required GPU feature. Do not use a PC-only flag as a blanket phone fix.
  3. Let the initial download complete; note whether its progress counter moves. A server quota message is a different branch from a GPU warning.
  4. Inspect the actual touch/input controls before committing to a long run. A larger display does not prove that all inputs work on your device.
  5. If the original browser route is unsuitable, retain the PC save and check current original-author/community guidance for your device. Do not install a purported mobile port just because it uses the game’s name.

[1][3]

Your PC and phone do not share storage automatically

Export from the client where your progress exists; export any phone progress separately before importing. Import replaces the destination. Keep both original files until heroes, equipment and zone unlocks match your intention. Browser addon installation is separate again. [4][5]

What has and has not been checked

We inspected the original launcher and client text and read the public mobile report. We have not completed a run on a physical Android phone, iPhone or iPad, benchmarked Winlator, or verified every cross-version save transfer. Device model, GPU, browser, game version and the exact warning are more useful troubleshooting details than “mobile does not work”.
