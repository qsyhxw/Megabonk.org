Contact the Soulstone Survivors guide editors

Reviewed 21 November 2026

Found an outdated mod version, a wrong skill or rune name, or a dead link? Email contact@megabonk.org with the page URL, your game version and loader, and a public source or a short description of what you saw.

Useful reports: a co-op mod release that no longer matches the game version, a skill chain that behaves differently in your build, a circle or portal step in the Bumble guide that failed. Please do not send passwords, purchase keys or save files.

We cannot restore saves, fix a mod install or give in-game items. For purchase or account problems use the support link on the store where you bought the game; for a mod bug, use that mod author’s issue tracker. There is no promised reply time. Steam · Soulstone Survivors store and support links ↗
