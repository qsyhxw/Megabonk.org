The Gold Portal route in four checkpoints

Taken from the Gold Portal guide, checked 5 November 2026. The portal is the entrance, not the unlock.

Altar of Gold
Pay it, finish the Teleporter event, then take the Gold Portal
Gilded Coast
Activate all seven beacons; the fourth only summons Aurelionite
Halcyon Seed
Pick it up after Aurelionite and check your inventory before leaving
Prime Meridian
Defeat False Son while carrying the seed, with Seekers of the Storm enabled

Common questions

Can Steam and Epic players play Risk of Rain 2 together?

Yes, on PC. Steam players must choose crossplay ON and restart after changing it, and Epic friends join through the account link and an in-game invitation or the copied Lobby ID. This follows the publisher’s instructions, rechecked 5 November 2026.

Full answer →
Can PlayStation and Xbox copies play together in Risk of Rain 2?

The reviewed sources establish no route between console families. 2K’s October 2026 notice also separates PS4–PS5 and Xbox One–Series multiplayer with the next console update, so check the installed version on both machines.

Full answer →
Is the Gold Portal the False Son unlock?

No. The portal only leads to Gilded Coast. You need the Halcyon Seed from Aurelionite, then defeat False Son at Prime Meridian while carrying it, with Seekers of the Storm enabled.

Full answer →
How many beacons do I activate at Gilded Coast?

All seven. The fourth beacon summons Aurelionite but does not make her vulnerable; damage her while all seven are active.

Full answer →
What if the Gold Portal never appears?

Pay the Altar of Gold and finish that stage’s Teleporter event before leaving; the portal is separate from the normal exit. Without an altar, check the Lunar Seer in the Bazaar for a Gilded Coast offer.
