Contact the PokeRogue guide editors

Reviewed 22 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A Dark Deal or Trash to Treasure rule, cost band or wave window that differs in a newer release;
  • A browser, home-screen or community-app step that works differently on your device;
  • An offline-save or transfer detail that no longer matches the wiki;
  • A wrong screenshot, link or encounter name on a PokeRogue page.

Include the page URL, your device and browser, how you play and what happened. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We do not run PokéRogue, so we cannot restore an account or save, fix a login problem or change how the game works. Use the original game ↗ (opens in a new tab) for account questions or the project repository ↗ (opens in a new tab) to reach the developers.
