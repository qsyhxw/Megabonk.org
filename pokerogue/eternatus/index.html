How to fight and catch Eternatus in PokeRogue

At wave 200 of ordinary Classic, fight for the clear unless every other starter is unlocked. Your starter collection determines whether a capture is possible.

Plan the last twenty waves

The Elite Four fight on 182, 184, 186 and 188. Their Champion follows on 190. Once you identify the first member, use that region to narrow down the remaining opponents. Heal before the next trainer if a key teammate is down.

Save a healthy answer for the rival’s Rayquaza on 195. It belongs to a trainer, so reserve your balls for wild encounters. At 200 the opponent changes to Eternatus.

Check whether a ball can work

What you seeWhat to do
Other starters still lockedPlay to defeat Eternatus. Even a Master Ball cannot bypass the starter requirement. Check your starter selection for missing species, including egg exclusives.
Every other starter unlocked, ordinary ClassicA Master Ball can skip the shield check. For other balls, reach the final HP segment of the second form first. Leave it alive for the throw.
An active challengeA Master Ball does not skip the final boss shield check. Full Fresh Start blocks this capture entirely.
Paradox Pokémon in the End before wave 200In ordinary Classic you must already have that species registered as caught or hatched. Hatch the missing species first. If two opponents are on the field, reduce the battle to one target.
Endless Eternatus or a trainer’s PokémonKeep your ball. These encounters block capture regardless of its strength.

Prepare for the change to Eternamax

When the first form reaches its final shield it transforms and refills its health. Bring a second battle-ready Pokémon for the double battle. Save your status move for after the transformation or plan to reapply it. Earlier stat changes carry over, but effects such as Leech Seed are cleared.

A Steel-type can cover poison attacks but still needs an answer to Flamethrower. A Fairy-type covers dragon attacks but risks Cross Poison. Keep a switch available instead of spending every healing item on a single immunity.
