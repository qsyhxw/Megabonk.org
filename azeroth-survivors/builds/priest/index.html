Priest build: choose a route that fits

Choose Discipline when you need a shield-based survival route; choose Shadow when you want Shadow Word: Pain to spread after kills. The shield and damage-over-time routes depend on different talents and should not be merged into one automatic recipe.[1]

Choose the route for your current character

These are conditional routes assembled from the original client’s talent descriptions. Check prerequisites and the points you have already spent in the game before following an endpoint. [1]

RouteTalents to inspectWhat makes it workCondition that changes the choice
DisciplineDivine Aegis → Rapture → PenanceDivine Aegis changes the start to Power Word: Shield and adds damage when a shield breaks. Rapture can immediately raise a broken shield again, with a 15-second limit. Penance adds free casts.A shield-break effect requires the shield to break. It is not the same as damage gained while a shield stays intact.
ShadowShadow Word: Pain support → ShadowformShadowform lasts for the run; a death with Shadow Word: Pain creates an explosion and passes the pain to the nearest enemy.The propagation requires a death with the effect present. It does not prove that an isolated boss will die quickly.

What to prioritise during the run

  1. If repeated contact is ending runs, first check whether the shield-start milestone and reapplication talent are allocated.
  2. Add damage that works while you move. A defensive setup still needs a spell capable of finishing enemies.
  3. On Shadow, get Shadow Word: Pain functioning before taking choices whose value assumes its spread.
  4. After the engine is working, use the actual failure point to choose health, recovery or damage; do not replace a shield problem with another conditional death explosion.

This is an editorial choice order, not a guaranteed card sequence. The offered upgrades and your current permanent investment still determine which step is available. [1]

When the intended build is not ready

Before the shield milestone is open, Smite remains the baseline start. Before Shadowform, treat Shadow Word: Pain as a DoT rather than guaranteed propagation. Do not spend future talent benefits in advance when deciding whether the present character can survive a harder zone.

Before changing the whole plan, identify the failure: no room to move, too little recovery, or too little damage on the target. Change the part that addresses that failure, then compare another run with similar talents and equipment.

Check the starter and trigger before copying a build

Baseline starter: Smite. Talents can replace it; use the route conditions above rather than treating this as the opening for every developed character. [1]

Siphon Life belongs to the Warlock route described elsewhere. Do not assume a Priest gets the same per-tick healing merely because both classes use damage over time.

Record the game version, zone, difficulty, selected capstone, permanent equipment and the card that changed the run. Comparing two different progression states cannot establish that a single spell was better. Use the meter carefully →
