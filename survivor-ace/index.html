Start here: one useful first loop

Start with the mobile client linked from Survivor: Ace official website ↗. Check the name before following instructions for the older game.

  1. Move first. Drag to steer while attacks fire automatically.
  2. Read the draft. Choose among the upgrades actually offered, rather than expecting a fixed build order.
  3. Review the failure. Separate being hit from leaving enemies alive, then decide what the next attempt needs.

This sequence combines the authors’ examples into a review method; it is not a tested clear route.

Read a weapon choice by the problem it solves

What you noticeDecision to review
Your attacks miss moving targetsCheck whether an offered attack tracks targets or requires a forward line.
Your current attack connects, but enemies remainCompare an upgrade to that weapon with a new attack; more weapon names alone do not settle the choice.
You lose HP before damage becomes the problemCompare the available survival effect with another damage pick.

This is a conditional reading checklist, not a weapon tier list. The Japanese examples and Inven’s cannon example describe different starts; neither establishes everyone’s default weapon.

Keep the two upgrade decisions separate

During a runBetween attempts
A choice from the current randomized draftAn equipment change whose displayed cost and effect you can inspect
Useful for the situation on screenA separate decision before flying again

Do not turn a missed random offer into an assumed equipment problem. First note what failed, then inspect the relevant effect instead of spending across every system.

Survivor: Ace FAQ

Is Survivor: Ace the same game as Survivor.io?

No. Survivor: Ace is a separate mobile aerial roguelike from Gorilla Studio. Codes, player IDs and redemption steps written for Survivor.io do not carry over, so check the Android package name com.survivor.ace before following an older guide.

Is Survivor: Ace free, and which platforms is it on?

The official website links to the App Store and Google Play. The Google Play listing shows no up-front price and lists ads and in-app purchases as of Nov 21, 2026.

Where do I enter gift codes in Survivor: Ace?

Published guides describe the lobby’s top-left menu, then Settings, then Gift Code, and checking the in-game mailbox for the result. These steps come from the sources, not from our own redemption. Language-specific steps are on the 繁中禮包碼 and 日本語・コード pages.

Do the listed Survivor: Ace codes still work?

We cannot confirm that. Codes here are reported by published lists and not redeemed by this site. ACE777 and DAY777 are past the deadlines the sources give, and the Nov 8 deadline reported for ACEWIN has passed too. Compare the deadline before you type anything.

Which weapon should I pick first in Survivor: Ace?

There is no single confirmed answer. A Japanese iPhone-edition guide lists a high-energy laser, a blazing beam and a frost blade as example opening choices, while an English-language account uses a cannon. Compare range and tracking against what actually hits you; see the weapon checklist.

Should I upgrade my current weapon or take a new attack?

Read what the offered draft fixes. If your attack connects but enemies remain, compare an upgrade to that weapon with a new attack. If HP runs out first, look for a survival effect before another damage pick. The checklist is under weapon choices.

Are in-run upgrades and equipment the same decision?

No. In-run picks come from a randomized draft during a flight; equipment changes happen between attempts and show their cost and effect first. Decide them separately, as laid out under run picks versus gear.

Is there a Traditional Chinese or Japanese guide?

Yes. There are beginner guides in 繁體中文 and 日本語, plus code guides in both languages.
