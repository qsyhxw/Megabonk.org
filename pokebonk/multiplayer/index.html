PokeBonk multiplayer: what is confirmed?

PokeBonk now documents a Co-op Beta for 2–4 trainers, with room creation, invites and code-based joining. Access can be restricted or paused by the server. Use the current Co-op screen to check availability; we inspected the client and notes, not a completed shared run.[1][2]

Checked October 26, 2026 · Beta v0.8.55 · Source-based guide

What co-op includes now

The v0.8.48 client has a dedicated Co-op flow and a 2–4 trainer label. It offers Create a room and Join with a code. Its availability states distinguish closed Beta, maintenance and a temporary pause. Friends, clans and leaderboards remain separate social systems.[1]

Create or join a room in order

  1. Open the original project. Use pokebonk.com — original game ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab) and keep the account/session with your existing progress.
  2. Check Co-op availability. If the screen says closed Beta, maintenance or paused, follow that message. A working solo menu is not proof that new co-op rooms can be created.
  3. Choose who creates the room. The creator selects the available destination and difficulty, then invites friends or shares the room code/link.
  4. Join the same room. Other trainers use the invitation or Join with a code. Inspect the room’s player cards; change your own partner through your card and the League selection.
  5. Resolve version or connection warnings before Start. The client checks player-to-player readiness. When a version mismatch appears, everyone should reload the original page and check the version again.

The client first attempts browser-to-browser connections and documents relay fallback when direct connections are blocked. We have not tested router, VPN or corporate-network fixes; do not install an unofficial client or disable device protections on the strength of a generic connection tip.[1]

Pause, menus, reviving and current team rules

Pause does not stop a shared run. The client warns that you remain exposed with pause open. Upgrade/menu choices use a distinct protective bubble; the v0.8.46 notes say you do not deal damage while choosing, and the guardian can move to teammates. Do not rely on opening pause as a safe break.[1][2]

For a downed teammate, the release notes describe going beside them and holding E on desktop to revive. The v0.8.45 notes give a one-minute revival window. Later fixes address full revival bars and reviving when a team crosses a portal. These are documented rules, not a latency guarantee.

Do not follow the superseded v0.8.45 team-puzzle instructions. Version 0.8.46 removed the boss seals, changing team weakness, Wrath and linked-elite mechanics introduced in that update. Gengar, cursed and Rhydon altars returned to individual use. Version 0.8.47 adds shared discoveries on the map and a way to visit the Lab from the room and return to it.

Is this the Megabonk multiplayer mod?

No. This guide covers the separate PokeBonk browser project. Megabonk mod-manager instructions apply to the Steam game. They do not create a PokeBonk room or change your Beta eligibility.

Room prerequisites and the latest replay correction

Version boundary: this guide covers the original pokebonk.com v0.8.55, checked October 26. Our Play window uses a separate third-party v0.8.27 source with separate progress. Newer co-op, Treasures and Archive changes may be absent there. Mechanics are SINGLE_SOURCE; no full-run, reward-claim or multiplayer completion test is claimed.

Use the four-character room code or share link from the room’s actual invite controls. At least two trainers are needed to start, and the room supports up to four. Every participant needs the selected destination and difficulty unlocked; the host’s unlock alone is insufficient. Compare player-card readiness and versions before Start.[3]

The project describes relay fallback when a direct browser connection is blocked. This is a fallback, not a guaranteed fix for every network. The v0.8.51–.52 notes reduce room polling/relay traffic; .54 corrects skipped zones when replaying with the same team. A previous room warning should be rechecked against the current build.[4]

League and Look progress require the applicable solo flow. For clan membership use the clan entry checklist; joining a clan does not launch co-op. No cross-play or real multiplayer completion test is claimed here.
