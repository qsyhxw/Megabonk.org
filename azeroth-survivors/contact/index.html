Contact the Azeroth Survivors guide editors

Megabonk.org editors · Reviewed 16 November 2026

Game data checked against the latest original client · What changed

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A class build or talent route that no longer works after an update;
  • A tier placement, zone unlock or boss that differs in your game;
  • A broken link, image or tool on an Azeroth Survivors page, including the build planner.

Include the page address, your class and the version shown in the bottom-right corner of the game menu. A screenshot of the talent tree or tooltip helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not Aethus or game support, so we cannot restore saves, recover progress from another host or fix server problems. For those, use the community channel linked from the original launcher at wow.aethus.pl; our save backup guide explains how to keep an export before you change browser or device. Do not send passwords or save files you need to keep private. There is no promised reply time.
