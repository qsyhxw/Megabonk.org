Controller Support Does Not Mean Console Support

The Steam version has full controller support, including PlayStation controller support. You can connect a compatible controller to a PC or play on Steam Deck, but this still uses the Steam version of the game.

  • Use the official Windows or Linux Steam build.
  • Connect the controller before launching if Steam Input does not detect it immediately.
  • Do not buy a similarly named PlayStation, Xbox, or Nintendo game assuming it is an official port.
