Class choice comes before a “best race” label

  1. Open Armory → Character and inspect which race is available to the selected class.
  2. For repeated early contact deaths, compare a health/defence option such as Tauren or Dwarf when available.
  3. If survival is already stable and repeat casts drive the build, compare Gnome, Troll or Orc for the actual engine.
  4. For a shield or maximum-health scaling route, read the talent’s scaling condition before assuming every damage racial is equivalent.
  5. If the desired race is locked, inspect Rewards → Races. Do not build the whole first-run plan around a purchase you cannot make.

We inspected the race and reward panels, not every class/race pairing or a performance benchmark. Race changes do not grant missing capstones or zone clears.[1]

A permanent race purchase is not a talent respec

Rewards states that each race purchase opens it for every class, with its racial and passives, and is kept when perks are reset. Read the current price and unlock display on your save before confirming. A cosmetic appearance and a race capability should not be inferred from the same icon alone.
