Contact the Brotato guide editors

Reviewed 18 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A balance number, character change or Evolution rule that differs from a newer patch note;
  • A Primal Dread platform, price or release window that no longer matches the store;
  • A co-op or Remote Play step that works differently on your setup;
  • A wrong screenshot, link or character name on a Brotato page.

Include the page URL, your platform, the game version and branch, and what happened.

What we cannot help with

We are not the developer or the game's support team, so we cannot restore a save, fix a store purchase or change how the game works. Use the developer-linked bug forum ↗ external link, new tab for reproducible game bugs, or your platform's support for account and purchase problems.
