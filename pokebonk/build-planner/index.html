    Use a goal, then keep a fallback

    Survival: compare move class/type and whether you can keep hitting while moving. Fix the last failure before chasing a late pair. Fusion: reserve two ingredient slots; do not assume a fourth move is free. Status: read the actual source of the required status; type alone does not establish applications.

    The planner uses the partner’s listed kit. It does not model Mew’s broader pool, later evolution-specific offers, archived choices or shared game progress. Selected items show their original descriptions and copy limits; vitamins show the affected stat. These are checklists, not combined effectiveness scores. An unmatched pair uses the fallback rule; a recipe match still requires the actual machine and eligible moves. Share links contain only the version, partner, selected moves, capacity, goal, items, vitamins and declared owned Lab levels. They do not log in, upload a save or change the game.

    Record move levels and offered upgrade cards

    Choose moves above, then expand a move to enter its level and the effects offered by each observed or hypothetical upgrade card. A level alone does not determine the complete result: cards choose distinct random effects. Incomplete cards count only the guaranteed level increment.

    Normal moves cap at level 8; TM Case permits 10. Fusion ingredients still each need level 8. Formed fusions cap at ★5, or ★6 with TM Case. The star records an intention; use the recipe finder to verify the pair. Recipe-specific fusion damage is excluded.

    Set targets and balance in the Lab calculator →, then return a copy of this draft. Target prices use the dated 24-upgrade schedule; recheck the actual game before spending.

    Unchecked uses declared owned levels. Checked uses the greater of owned and target for a hypothetical panel, including support capacity. This does not buy an upgrade or change your owned ledger. Selected normal Alpha types and the separate shiny now form one included support group; the shiny doubles its support bonus and Poké Doll scales the group.
