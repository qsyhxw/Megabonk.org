Choice rule: an A-tier item that directly completes your build is usually better than an unrelated S-tier item. For example, Turbo Skates can outrank Giant Fork in a Movement Speed build that has not invested in Crit.

Best Items by Build

Crit / Overcrit

Giant Fork first, then Demonic Blade for sustain and Overpowered Lamp when the build also carries on-hit procs.

Long Run / Leaderboard

Joe's Dagger, Anvil, Pot (Stainless Steel), Sucky Magnet and Soul Harvester scale with time, levels or kill density.

Movement / Economy

Turbo Skates converts speed into offense; Golden Sneakers converts travel into Gold. Take both only when you will keep moving.

Stationary / AFK

Idle Juice supplies the damage multiplier while Sucky Magnet prevents missed XP. Add Holy Book or Chonkplate when sustain is the weak point.

Poison / Amog

Toxic Barrel is valuable when taking controlled hits is acceptable. Pair it with poison application and sustain instead of forcing it into a fragile ranged run.

Lifesteal / Vlad

Chonkplate is the broad defensive anchor. Bloody Cleaver and Demonic Blade add offense or Crit-based healing once Lifesteal is already reliable.

Item Tier List FAQ

What is the best item in Megabonk?

Giant Fork is the strongest broad pick once Crit is established. Joe's Dagger is better for dense long runs, Sucky Magnet protects XP collection, and Pot or Anvil can win high-level weapon-scaling runs.

Are Legendary items always better?

No. Rarity controls availability, not whether the effect fits your build. Turbo Skates can be the best pickup in a Movement Speed run, while Idle Juice can outperform a Legendary in a stationary setup.

Should I always take an S-tier item?

No. Take the item that solves the build's current problem. An A-tier sustain item is worth more than unrelated S-tier damage when the run is already killing quickly but cannot survive.

Why is Golden Ring unranked?

Its practical effect is not consistent enough across available descriptions and tests to treat it as dependable build power. Use its detail page for the current status instead of routing a run around it.

What belongs in the Items Database?

The database is the complete lookup for names, rarity, effects, unlocks and detail pages. This page is the opinionated decision layer for choosing between items during a run.
