Choose an account reward before spending points

Achievements feed account-wide reward points. Use Rewards to inspect the requirement, plan and cost before confirming. These points are separate from class talent experience and gear marks.[1]

Megabonk.org editors · Reviewed 30 October 2026 · Original v0.130.0 / e7d9dd47 · Menu and client-text research, not a completed-run test

A practical spending order

  1. Open Achievements and inspect a reachable requirement; every class contributes to the account.
  2. Open Rewards and choose the benefit you need: draft control, collection help, race access or appearance.
  3. Read both the achievement gate and the point cost. Having points does not open an offer whose achievement is missing.
  4. Preview the plan, then confirm only the purchases you intend. Keep an export if you are experimenting with a limited reset.

For a player repeatedly losing useful drafts, compare reroll, skip and banish first: reroll changes the offered hand, skip passes it for gold, while banish removes a card from later offers that run. This is an editorial task order, not a mandatory purchase build.[1]

Companion purchase versus companion perks

The captured Rewards → Companions panel has a Companion Perks view with range, speed, instincts and a second companion. The basic follower fetches coins and experience wisps; it is not a combat pet and does not fetch every object. Instincts are one per companion, assigned in purchase order. Choose range if pickups remain out of reach; compare speed/rest improvements when repeated trips are the constraint. An appearance alone is not the follower purchase.[1]

Check what a reset keeps

The current panel says a perk reset returns spent perk points, keeps tabards and races and has a limited number remaining on the save. Our fresh profile displayed two resets; read your own remaining counter instead of treating that as unlimited. Talent respecs are a different, free class operation. Race purchases open a race to every class and are retained by reward resets. Choose a race by the problem it solves →[1]
