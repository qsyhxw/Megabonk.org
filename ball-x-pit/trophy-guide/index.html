Ball x Pit trophy guide: route & clear checklist

Build your permanent upgrades and the Matchmaker, then track stage clears by character. The base-game trophy guide reports that a two-character clear counts for both. Save named evolution and missing-encyclopedia checks for targeted runs rather than treating every clear as a collection run. [1]

A route that reduces repeat clears

  1. Progress through the stages while collecting resources and blueprints. Finish useful construction between runs.
  2. When available, complete the Matchmaker so you can select two characters.
  3. Use the matrix below to record your actual clears. Pair characters still missing the same stage instead of repeating a completed pair.
  4. Review the in-game trophy description and encyclopedia for the remaining tasks. Use a deliberate ingredient route for Satan or Nosferatu, and a separate appearance-condition check for hidden passives.

This sequence combines the published base-game route with a planning checklist. The matrix is your record, not a connection to the game or a trophy-unlock service. [1]

How long does completion take?

Dayngls estimates 25–30 hours for its base-game trophy route. That is an author estimate for that route, not a promise for a first clear, every platform or the forthcoming expansion. Your unlocks, building progress and repeated clears can change the workload. [1]

Base-game scope and checklist storage

This checklist uses the original 16-character roster and eight stage names. It deliberately excludes free-update/DLC characters from the trophy matrix because the original trophy requirements must be checked in your version. A checked cell is a manual note, not verified completion.

Ticks are saved only in this browser’s local storage. They do not sync to Steam, a console or your game save. Use Reset to remove this checklist from the device; private browsing or cleared site data may erase it. The page works as a printable table when JavaScript is unavailable.
