About the PokeRogue guides

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 22 November 2026 · Game release checked: v1.12.2.4

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style and roguelite games. We are not the Pagefault Games project that makes PokéRogue, and nothing here is endorsed by it or by any Pokémon rights holder.

What it covers

The guide home sends you to the original game and to the page for your next decision: mobile versions and the original entry, whether to accept Dark Deal and whether to dig or investigate in Trash to Treasure. We do not host the game, accounts or saves.

Where the answers come from

  • The original game: the account screen and links at pokerogue.net ↗ identify the official entry point.
  • The project code: encounter rules, wave windows and cost bands are checked against release v1.12.2.4 in the project repository ↗. Code inspection supports the rules; it is not a live run.
  • The community wiki and player guides: the PokéRogue Wiki ↗ and a named community mobile guide supply controls, home-screen steps and the online-to-offline save direction.
  • Images: the account screen was captured from the original client and encounter sprites come from the project’s assets revision for v1.12.2.4. They identify the game or encounter and are not gameplay screenshots.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was checked; the section as a whole was last reviewed on 22 November 2026, with rules last confirmed on 6 November 2026. When an encounter or balance update changes a rule, the page that depends on it is rechecked and its date changes only when the answer was confirmed again.

Which game this guide is about

These pages cover PokéRogue, the browser roguelite at pokerogue.net. It is a different game from PokeBonk and other similarly named Pokémon projects, which have their own pages on this site.

Send a correction

Send the page URL, how you play (browser, shortcut or community app), the game version if you know it and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or the project’s own code are reviewed first.
