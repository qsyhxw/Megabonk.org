Risk of Rain 2 item tier list: where the 2026 rankings agree

Short answer: Tougher Times and AtG Missile Mk. 1 are top tier in all three sources, and 57 Leaf Clover is top tier in two. Ukulele is A tier everywhere. Gasoline, Mocha, Soldier's Syringe and Safer Spaces are contested. This is a summary of published rankings, not our own playtesting.

How these rankings were chosen

Only three sources were compared. Tierspire combines six published lists, with letter grades averaged. PixelNitro is one author's judgement. GameBrief ranks items for first-pick value in a solo run on Rainstorm or Monsoon. The three use different criteria, so a tier in one does not directly equal a tier in another.

None of these sources is a patch-by-patch test, so check the current patch notes before relying on a specific item.
