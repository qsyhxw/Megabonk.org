Route 3-3: how to beat Regigigas

Plan for two different failures on Route 3-3. Groups can overwhelm your coverage before the final wave. Regigigas arrives alone with a billion armor, a billion HP and increasing speed.

Megabonk.org editors · Build 1.7.3 · Updated 25 November 2026

Choose your tiles before the late waves

There are eight field tiles, six grass tiles, two mountain tiles and no water tiles. Check the terrain before copying a roster from a different map. Wave 87 brings twelve Walrein alongside Sealeo and Spheal. If the pack leaks here, keep group damage aimed at a stretch several enemies occupy together.

Regigigas at wave 100

Wave 99 contains ten Walrein. Once those fall, wave 100 replaces the pack with Regigigas. Slow Start makes it accelerate. Put your armor specialist where it can begin hitting early. Excadrill can use field or mountain terrain and deals double damage against armor.

Regigigas resists Stun, while its bestiary entry lists Slow, Burn and Poison as effective. If the boss leaves range with armor remaining, try Slow within reach of the armor attacker. If armor falls early but the boss survives, compare sustained damage along the remaining path. A slot spent on Stun alone contributes little to this fight.

How to recruit Regigigas

To recruit Regigigas, select Tough Enemies at +50% and defeat it. A normal kill or the +25% setting misses that condition. Keep your first-clear roster as a baseline before attempting the larger health and armor pools.

For other collection requirements see hidden Pokémon unlocks. For roster substitutions use the team role guide.

For the rest of the world, compare the Regi bosses before carrying this team over to another map.

Map and boss data
