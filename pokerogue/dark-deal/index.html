PokeRogue Dark Deal: accept or refuse?

Refuse if your run cannot survive losing an essential team member. Accepting removes a randomly selected Pokémon and opens a catchable boss fight. You get five Rogue Balls, not a guaranteed safe upgrade.

These recommendations are a way to weigh the documented costs, not a claim that one choice wins more runs. Before accepting, identify the jobs your team needs for the next boss: damage, survival, status control or catching. If losing any one of those jobs breaks the plan, refusing preserves the team you already built.

What accepting actually does

  1. A random party member is removed. This is not a screen where you nominate a sacrifice.
  2. You receive five Rogue Balls and a new boss encounter is generated.
  3. The removed member’s non-form-changing held items are carried into the opponent configuration. A well-equipped donor can therefore leave you facing an equipped opponent.
  4. Fight and attempt to catch the new Pokémon if that is your goal. A catchable encounter is not a guaranteed catch or a free replacement already added to your party.

Do not plan on getting the original member back by refusing the battle afterward. Refusing the initial offer is the choice that leaves your team intact, with no reward or encounter EXP.

Read the odds as cost bands, not catch chances

The checked release rolls 35% for cost 6, 50% for cost 7, 10% for cost 8 and 5% for cost 9–10. Those are selection bands in the source, not the probability of catching the result or a guarantee of a particular species.

The selection tries to match the removed member’s typing. A Mono-Type Challenge supplies its required type instead. If the requested combination has no eligible result, the selection helper can fall back; do not promise yourself one legendary from a type alone. The exclusion list includes Mythicals, Ultra Beasts, Paradox Pokémon, Eternatus and certain special forms.

Older articles list different percentages, and the Wiki summarizes the highest band as 9. This guide uses the explicit 9–10 band in release v1.12.2.4. Offline copies on older game files may differ.

When the encounter is eligible

Dark Deal starts at wave 30 within the Mystery Encounter window ending at 180, requires at least two party members, and is disabled for Hardcore and Mono-Generation Challenges in the checked release. Eligibility does not mean it must appear. Mystery Encounters belong to Classic and Challenge runs, not Daily or Endless.
