About the Vampire Survivors guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 10 November 2026

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not poncle, who makes Vampire Survivors, and the guides are not endorsed by the developer or by any platform that sells the game.

What it covers

Weapon evolution recipes, unlock routes, the Collection 1.0 checklist, DLC pack comparison, co-op compatibility and the original browser demo. Each page answers one job a player has during a run or before buying, and the DLC and recipe pages say plainly where coverage stops.

Where the answers come from

  • The developer's own pages: the itch.io demo page ↗ (opens in a new tab) and its FAQ describe the browser demo and its save; the online co-op FAQ ↗ (opens in a new tab) and the Steam store page ↗ (opens in a new tab) supply supported modes, editions and DLC listings.
  • The community wiki: recipe levels, passive conditions and unlock requirements are checked against the Vampire Survivors Wiki ↗ (opens in a new tab), and each guide links the entry behind its claim.
  • Demo screenshots: the browser-demo images are frames from one opening run in desktop Chrome on 5 November 2026. The full-game guides rely on the sources above rather than on our own complete playthroughs.

How pages stay current

Every guide shows the date it was checked; the section as a whole was last reviewed on 10 November 2026. When poncle releases an update or a new DLC, the pages that depend on its recipes, unlock conditions or storefront rules are rechecked first.

Which game this guide is about

Searches for the name also return similarly named browser games and clones. These pages cover poncle's Vampire Survivors and its original itch.io demo only. Browser games inspired by it are compared on a separate page, with their own saves and hosts.

Send a correction

Send the page URL, your platform and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or the developer's own statement are reviewed first.
