Mythic Survivors evolution requirements and missing picks

Check your mode first. Pixery’s 1.0.27 Wiki limits ultimates to campaign. Then max the one or two abilities named by the intended ultimate and check later picks for it.

Check the mode before spending more picks

The Pixery abilities reference ↗ (opens in a new tab) says ultimates are campaign-only. Normal abilities reach five stars there, while Boss Battle limits them to two and Eternal Tower to three. If you are checking an ultimate recipe in either capped mode, switch to a campaign run before evaluating whether the evolution works.

Use this order to separate eligibility from a possible fault. First record the mode, then the intended ultimate, its prerequisites and their stars. That makes a useful report if the option still fails to appear.

Check the intended ultimate’s prerequisites

  1. Open the ultimate entry in the developer’s ability list ↗ (opens in a new tab) and note the one or two base abilities listed for it.
  2. In campaign, raise each required ability to its maximum stars.
  3. Check subsequent ability choices for the ultimate. The developer describes it as becoming eligible to appear, so reaching the requirement does not promise the next pick.
  4. If the base ability is missing from your choices, check the class or chapter label on its entry before committing the run to that plan.

For a simple recipe check, the Wiki lists Eclipse Pyre → Tempest Avatar and Arcane Cut → Dragon’s Ashes, with Arcane Cut restricted to Vanguard. Use the second example only on that class. Consult the full ultimate entry when it specifies two ingredients.

A generic instruction to collect a passive catalyst and open a gold chest is insufficient for this check. Use the named ultimate’s current prerequisites and campaign eligibility as your starting evidence. Permanent equipment merging is a separate progression task from evolving an ability during a run.

If the requirements appear satisfied

A player’s 2 September Google Play review reported maxed abilities failing to evolve despite a matching passive. Pixery’s 7 September reply requested more detail if it happened again. That report establishes a player problem, while leaving its cause and a working fix unresolved. It predates the Wiki’s current data.

Before retrying, write down the installed version, campaign chapter, class, exact ultimate, required abilities and their stars. Capture the choices shown after reaching the prerequisites. Include these details and your player ID through the support route on the Google Play listing ↗ (opens in a new tab) if the discrepancy persists.

The recipe and mode checks here use the developer’s Wiki marked game version 1.0.27, reviewed 11 November 2026. We have not reproduced the reported issue or tested a guaranteed fix. If your installed build has different card requirements, retain a screenshot of those requirements when reporting the mismatch.
