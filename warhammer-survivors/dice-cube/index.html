How to unlock Dice Cube in Warhammer Survivors

In 40K, get D6 Dice to level 6. In Age of Sigmar, hold four weapons at once. Each universe has its own Dice Cube objective. Check the Objective in the universe you are currently playing.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 11 November 2026 · Release v2.1

Follow the condition for your universe

UniverseReported objectiveAction
Warhammer 40,000D6 Dice reaches level 6Take the D6 Dice weapon and invest upgrades into it. Check the unlock after completing the condition.
Age of SigmarHold four weapons at onceTake four weapon slots in one run. Check the Dice Cube objective afterward.

Choose the weapon that uses Dice Cube

Dice Cube is the passive companion for Boltgun → Heavy Bolter in 40K and Boltstorm Pistol → Typhoon Crossbow in Age of Sigmar. D6 Dice is the weapon used in the 40K unlock condition. After unlocking the passive, you still need to choose Dice Cube in the run where you want the evolution.

Search Dice Cube in the recipe finder to see both pairings. For an evolution attempt, assemble the weapon/passive combination and check the weapon upgrade and chest conditions on that page.

If the passive still does not appear

  1. Check which universe is selected.
  2. Check the exact Dice Cube Objective and its completion state.
  3. Distinguish the D6 Dice weapon from the Dice Cube passive in your loadout.
  4. Check later level-up choices after the unlock. The requirement does not establish a guaranteed drop time.

Exact selection odds and a guaranteed appearance time have not been established.

For a different unlock, continue to two followers and Bugman’s Tankard.
