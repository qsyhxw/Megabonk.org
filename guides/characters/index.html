Megabonk Characters FAQ

How many characters are in Megabonk?

Megabonk currently has 21 playable characters in the reviewed v1.0.69 roster.

Which characters are available immediately?

Fox and Sir Oofie are available immediately as starter characters.

How do I unlock more characters?

Complete the listed challenge, map tier, enemy defeat, item interaction or purchase requirement. Use the table above and each Character Guide for exact steps.

What is the difference between a Character Guide and Best Build?

A Character Guide explains unlocks, the passive and starting weapon. A Best Build page focuses on Weapons, Tomes, Items and stage-by-stage upgrade order.
