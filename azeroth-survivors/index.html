Which game did you mean?

Looking for the Warcraft-themed game with automatic spells? This guide covers Aethus’s Azeroth Survivors at Aethus · original browser game ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab). Descriptions such as “WoW roguelike”, “WoW Vampire Survivors” or “WoW Megabonk” can point to this style of game; they do not make it Blizzard’s World of Warcraft, Vampire Survivors or Megabonk itself. Choose the actual game and host before using a build or moving a save. [1]

Who is Aethus? What does “zuzia miau miau” refer to?

The original character-menu screenshot shown on this page credits “Made by Aethus (zuzia miau miau)” at the bottom left. This is the visible creator credit in the English v0.130.0 menu captured on 30 October 2026 and re-read on 2 November. It identifies the game credit; it does not establish a legal name, another account’s identity or a separate download site. [1]

For the game itself, use Aethus · wow.aethus.pl ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab). Check the full spelling, including the .pl ending, when returning from a search result. The original WebGPU browser route and our separately labelled third-party Play window have different providers; keep your save with the host that created it.
