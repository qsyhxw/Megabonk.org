Who Is Zorro in Megabonk?

Zorro is not a separate playable character. The name points to Bandit and the masked, fast execution playstyle built around his starter weapon.

Quick Answer

Select Bandit in game. Bandit is the official character, starts with Dexecutioner, and gains +1% Attack Speed per level. His visual design references Zorro, which is why players use “Zorro” in searches and discussions.
