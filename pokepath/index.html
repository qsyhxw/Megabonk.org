Open the original PokéPath TD game

Play on the original PokéPath site ↗

The destination is pokepath.gg/play/ in a new tab. Its page links the current browser client at play.pokepath.gg and the creator’s desktop downloads on itch.io. The original site identifies Khydra as the game developer and Nox as its site maintainer. PokéPath TD is an unofficial Pokémon fan game.

The browser client reached profile creation and the Route 1-1 tutorial during our 9 November check. Choose a profile and starter, then follow that tutorial. A full route clear and save transfer have not been tested here.

Make the first session manageable

  1. Read the starter’s displayed ability. The checked profile screen offers Charmander, Treecko and Froakie. Charmander’s description gives Burn, so it is a straightforward first choice if you want to follow one damage effect while learning placement.
  2. Place for path coverage. Compare where an enemy enters and leaves the attacker’s range. A legal tile with a poor firing window can waste an otherwise useful Pokémon.
  3. Inspect the next wave. Armor and invisibility call for different team jobs. Use the role checklist to decide what is missing.
  4. Back up before a device change. Download an exported save before clearing browser data or moving to another host.

The project introduction ↗ documents persistent Pokémon progress after a defeat, free route switching and Auto Mode for farming. For the first boss, see Route 1-1 and Shaymin. Once you clear it, the Bicycle and Wave Manager can help you revisit a wave. For an early session, learn your placement and check a failed wave before leaving automation running.

The original play page lists 1.7.3 on 25 November 2026. The 1.7.3 notes fix Mega Evolution loading and Mega Stone eligibility. The earlier 8 November patch notes change abilities, item eligibility and several attackers. Check the version on your own host before copying an older team. For example, the update removes Focus Band from Spinda and changes Decidueye’s ability. An older setup depending on either interaction needs another review.

Common questions

Why is the Wave Manager missing after buying the Bicycle?

Defeat the wave-100 boss on that map first. On a cleared map, look at the lower left beside your team.

Full answer →
How do I move my PokéPath TD save to another device?

Open Settings → Data → Export data → Download on the device that has the progress, then use Import data → Upload .txt → Load save on the new one. Keep the source device until the team and route progress are visible.

Full answer →
Does PokéPath TD work on a phone?

The original FAQ calls mobile support limited and recommends PC. It reports Android working fairly well and Safari failing to load on iOS. Physical-phone performance is untested here.

Full answer →
Which Pokémon helps against armored enemies?

Excadrill is the armor pick in the original Pokédex: Armor Break doubles damage to armored enemies, and it can use field and mountain tiles.

Full answer →
Why does my team lose to Lunala on Route 1-3?

Check invisibility coverage before adding damage. Lunala regains invisibility and its shield four seconds after losing invisibility, and it resists Stun and Poison.

Full answer →
Where do I enter a PokéPath TD code?

Open Menu → Data, paste the string into Paste redeem code and choose Redeem. The original unlock guide lists BOOPKABOO; current redemption is untested here.

Full answer →
Is PokéPath TD the same game as PokeBonk or PokéRogue?

No. PokéPath is a route tower defense game with its own progression. PokeBonk, PokéSurvivor, PokéRogue and PokéClicker are separate projects.
