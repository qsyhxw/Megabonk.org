About the Survivor Ace guides

Updated 2026-10-28

Megabonk.org publishes these independent, source-based task guides to Survivor: Ace. We are not Gorilla Studio, the publisher or game support. The hub and Chinese and Japanese beginner guides compare source-based upgrade decisions and edition limits. The Chinese and Japanese code articles distinguish reported strings, source deadlines and unresolved reward differences; we do not claim to have redeemed them.

We review the cited public pages and keep their dates and limits next to the relevant statements. Code availability can change between reviews. No account, game login or redemption service is offered here.
