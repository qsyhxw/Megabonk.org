What Is Megabonk?

Megabonk is a 3D roguelike survival game with automatic attacks, free 3D movement and randomized upgrade offers. Each run changes as you combine a character passive with weapons, Tomes and items.

This independent player Wiki organizes practical game information without pretending to be the developer or an official download source.

Megabonk Quick Answers

What is Megabonk?

Megabonk is a 3D roguelike survival game where players fight hordes on randomly generated maps, collect loot, level up, unlock characters and assemble different weapon and item Builds.

Who made Megabonk?

Megabonk was developed and published by vedinad. It released on Steam on September 18, 2025.

Is Megabonk.org the official Megabonk website?

No. Megabonk.org is an independent player Wiki. Use the official Steam page for the game, developer information and purchasing.
