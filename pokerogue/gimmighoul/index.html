How to evolve Gimmighoul into Gholdengo

Keep Gimmighoul in your party while collecting treasure, reach ten points, then level it up. Track the reward items themselves. You can spend your shop money normally.

Megabonk.org editors · Checked 25 November 2026, no full run tested

Two Relic Gold rewards and two Big Nuggets give 3 + 3 + 2 + 2 = 10. One of each gives five points. Cash earned from winning a battle does not turn into points just because your wallet grows.

If it still has not evolved

  1. Check when Gimmighoul joined. Nuggets collected before it entered your party did not add to its tracker.
  2. Check the holder of Golden Punch. A teammate earning money with it does not give Gimmighoul that held-item point.
  3. Read the number on the evolution tracker when available. It combines treasure reward progress and eligible persistent items. Let Gimmighoul gain a level after reaching ten.

Evolution rules

The Gholdengo evolution entry ↗ lists values for both Chest and Roaming forms. The game’s treasure tracker ↗ updates the Pokémon present when a money reward is collected.
