How to beat the 30-minute Red Death

Prepare Infinite Corridor and Crimson Shroud before the Reaper arrives. This base-game route combines repeated health reduction with protection. It requires Yellow Sign progression. Other strategies exist, but this guide follows this specific two-evolution route.

By Megabonk.org · checked 10 November 2026 · Vampire Survivors is by poncle.

Prepare both evolutions

EvolutionWeaponPassives
Infinite CorridorClock Lancet level 7Silver Ring level 9 + Gold Ring level 9
Crimson ShroudLaurel level 7Metaglio Left level 9 + Metaglio Right level 9

After preparing each set, collect evolution-capable chest rewards. Have both finished before the normal stage timer expires. Start with Yellow Sign and ring prerequisites if these map items are missing.

Plan the run around the map items

  1. Choose a normal non-Endless run with time to collect the four map items. Inlaid Library provides a straightforward horizontal route.
  2. Take Clock Lancet and Laurel along with weapons that can keep you alive while gathering experience.
  3. Collect both rings and both Metaglios. Their guardians and Curse effects add pressure, so plan movement and experience collection before committing.
  4. Check the exact 7/9/9 levels for each recipe, then secure the two evolution rewards.
  5. Stay close enough for Infinite Corridor’s effect to reach the Reaper while maintaining protection and avoiding unnecessary contact.

Laurel and Clock Lancet both stop at level 7. Treating every weapon as an eight-level weapon makes the checklist misleading.

Read the result correctly

Infinite Corridor repeatedly reduces enemy health, while Crimson Shroud caps incoming damage and provides retaliation. Defeating the Reaper unlocks Mask of the Red Death for purchase. It does not require owning the Castlevania DLC.

When the White Hand sequence follows, the zoom and bells signal an ending event. It cannot be handled as another normal enemy to damage or outrun. Dying in that sequence does not undo the Reaper unlock.
