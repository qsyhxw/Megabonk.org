Route 5-1: how to beat Tapu Koko

Bring a stun source and place your strongest attacker where their ranges overlap. Above half health, Tapu Koko can take damage only while stunned.

Megabonk.org editors · Build 1.7.3 · Updated 25 November 2026

Choose your tiles before the late waves

Route 5-1 has five field tiles, four grass, two water and two mountain. There is one path. Look for a stretch where your stun source and your damage dealers can all reach the same target. Wave 99 mixes Clawitzer, Corsola, Cramorant, Wiglett and Staryu. Group coverage that clears this pack can still leave a gap against the lone boss.

Tapu Koko at wave 100

Tapu Koko starts with 50,000 HP and no armor. Electrogenesis doubles stun duration. Above 25,000 HP, watch for damage during each stun. If the boss stops moving but its health barely changes, move a damage dealer into that part of the path. If it never stops, check that your control ability actually applies Stun. The boss resists Slow.

Once the boss reaches half health, the stun-only damage condition ends. Keep an attacker covering the exit if it routinely gets through with a sliver of health. Compare attempts at the same difficulty before changing levels or adding a second stun source.

How to recruit Tapu Koko

Recruiting Tapu Koko requires a kill with at most three stuns. Count applications from every team member. If you consume the allowance early, narrow the stun coverage to the part of the path with the highest damage, then rely on ordinary attacks for the second half.
