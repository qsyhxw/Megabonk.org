INDEPENDENT PLAYER GUIDE · VAMPIRE SURVIVORS

Secret characters through gameplay

Use the Secrets hint to identify a character, then check that character’s prerequisites and gameplay action. This guide covers four base-game routes, with hints separated from spoilers. It is a starting checklist, not an all-characters list.

By Megabonk.org · checked 10 November 2026 · Vampire Survivors is by poncle.

Open the permanent Secrets menu

Work toward Yellow Sign first. The Bone Zone opens after Hyper has been unlocked for three normal stages. Defeat Sketamari, the moving bone ball marked by a question mark south of the start, and collect the Forbidden Scrolls of Morbane it drops. That relic permanently opens Secrets.

Unlocking the menu and solving a character’s condition are separate tasks. The menu also accepts built-in spells, but the routes below focus on gameplay. Castlevania’s later secret hints have their own relic prerequisites.

Choose a route you can already start

Leda — explore below Gallo Tower

Start Gallo Tower and travel south. The edges darken as you approach the hidden area. Look for Leda in the center of a room and defeat her. Do not race past her once she is visible. A fast character or Hyper can help cover the distance; Hurry shortens the available real-time exploration window. Return to character selection to purchase the unlocked character.

Minnah — the Dairy Plant cheese

After Yellow Sign, enter Dairy Plant and collect the special Cheese pickup. Its map marker uses a Floor Chicken icon. Mad Groove can bring it to you if that Arcana is available. Collecting it spawns seven large werewolves. Defeat all seven, then check character selection.

Peppino — heal the plants in Il Molise

Use Celestial Dusting on the plants in Il Molise. It heals them instead of damaging them. Restore a total of 100,000 health to unlock Peppino. O’Sole begins with Celestial Dusting, so that character provides a direct setup once unlocked. Killing plants with an unrelated weapon does not satisfy the healing task.

Mask of the Red Death — defeat the Reaper

Prepare the two evolution sets in our Red Death guide, defeat the Reaper, then purchase the character. The White Hand ending is separate from the unlock condition.

When the unlock does not appear

Check the named stage, prerequisite relic, character or weapon, and final trigger. A hint being visible is not proof the condition was completed. An unlocked character can still require a purchase. For other secrets, open the linked character’s full unlocking details in the Wiki. The four routes here leave other base-game secrets and DLC secret rosters uncovered.
