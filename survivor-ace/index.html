Start here: one useful first loop

Start with the mobile client linked from Survivor: Ace official website ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab). Check the name before following instructions for the older game.

  1. Move first. Drag to steer while attacks fire automatically.
  2. Read the draft. Choose among the upgrades actually offered, rather than expecting a fixed build order.
  3. Review the failure. Separate being hit from leaving enemies alive, then decide what the next attempt needs.

This sequence combines the authors’ examples into a review method; it is not a tested clear route.[2][3]

Read a weapon choice by the problem it solves

What you noticeDecision to review
Your attacks miss moving targetsCheck whether an offered attack tracks targets or requires a forward line.
Your current attack connects, but enemies remainCompare an upgrade to that weapon with a new attack; more weapon names alone do not settle the choice.
You lose HP before damage becomes the problemCompare the available survival effect with another damage pick.

This is a conditional reading checklist, not a weapon tier list. The Japanese examples and Inven’s cannon example describe different starts; neither establishes everyone’s default weapon.[2][3]

Keep the two upgrade decisions separate

During a runBetween attempts
A choice from the current randomized draftAn equipment change whose displayed cost and effect you can inspect
Useful for the situation on screenA separate decision before flying again

Do not turn a missed random offer into an assumed equipment problem. First note what failed, then inspect the relevant effect instead of spending across every system.[2][3]
