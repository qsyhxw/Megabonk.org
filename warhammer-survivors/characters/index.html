Warhammer Survivors characters: all 40 champions and how to unlock them

21 champions in Warhammer 40,000 and 19 in Age of Sigmar. Most unlock by rescuing them on a stage, finishing an Objective or collecting Paint Pots; a few are bought with coins. Each universe tracks its own unlocks.

Megabonk.org editors · Updated 13 November 2026 · Release v2.1

Fastest unlocks first

  1. Buy Amalia Novena (400 coins) in 40K: strong early and needed for the follower objectives.
  2. Rescue the easy captives: Kozlowski and the Legend of Catachan on Imperial Bastion, K9 Mastiff on Gallowdark; Armand Callis and Hanniver Toll on The Great Parch.
  3. Grab Abaddon Black on Imperial Bastion and Leadbelcher on The Great Parch: two Paint Pots unlock Black Templars Castellan and Maleneth Witchblade straight away.
  4. Let totals run in the background: Tor Garadon (10,000 kills) and the 10,000-kill Paint Pots finish on their own over several runs.

How rescues work

  1. Start the named stage in the right universe with any champion.
  2. Pause and open the Map tab: captives show as a green ?. Some only appear after you pick up a map relic (the Legend of Catachan needs the Signum Array from Ork Stronghold).
  3. Clear the enemies around the captive and walk up to them; they join your run.
  4. Finish the run. Rescued champions appear in Character Select, some with a coin price.
