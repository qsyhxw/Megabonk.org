Warhammer Survivors bosses: all 6 Extremis bosses and how to beat them

Each Extremis boss arrives at 25:00 and can only be beaten with one specific evolved weapon. Without that evolution its health bar does not go down. Bring the counter below, then survive to the 25-minute mark.

Recipes and max-level passives: all 38 evolutions. The counter weapons belong to specific champions: how to unlock them.

How an Extremis fight works

  1. Pick the champion who carries the counter's base weapon (for example Hanniver Toll for Flintlock Pistols).
  2. Evolve it before 25:00: max the weapon, hold the companion passive, open an elite/boss chest.
  3. Survive to 25:00. The boss spawns with its own health bar at the bottom of the screen.
  4. Win for 500 coins. Beating all three bosses of a universe opens its special stage.

Mode traps: Endless War removes the final boss, and Fast Attack doubles the mission clock. Turn both off while learning a boss.

Boss-by-boss tips

  • Norn Emissary: Immune to stun, Vulnerable and Instant Kill. Fast and hits hard up close: kite in wide circles, away from walls. Codex Astartes is a hidden passive on Death World Invasion; bring the Auspex relic to reveal it.
  • Great Unclean One: Highest health of the six. Max Tactical Rock before the 25-minute mark.
  • Ghazghkull Thraka: Power Klaw belongs to Commissar Yarrick (rescued on Ork Stronghold).
  • Vizzik Skour: Immune to stun and Instant Kill. Flintlock Pistols belong to Hanniver Toll (rescued on The Great Parch).
  • Skarbrand: Immune to burning, poison and stun; weak to Frost, Null and Holy damage. Stormcharged Warhammer belongs to Rostus Oxenhammer.
  • Trugg the Troggoth King: Lowest health. His club slam covers a large telegraphed area and knocks you back: step out of the warning zone. Fyrestorm Greataxe belongs to Gotrek Gurnisson.

Watch it

Mortarion and Nagash

Defeating all three 40K Extremis bosses opens Perils of the Warp (Mortarion, 30-minute stage). Defeating all three Age of Sigmar Extremis bosses opens Necropolis (Nagash). Both are immune to stun and Instant Kill. Stage unlock order: all maps.
