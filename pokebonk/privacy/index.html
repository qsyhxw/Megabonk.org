Privacy notice

Updated October 23, 2026

This notice covers the PokeBonk guide section at megabonk.org/pokebonk/. It is separate from the privacy policy of the original game at pokebonk.com.

Website delivery

The website is delivered through Cloudflare. Delivery and security services may process request data such as IP address, browser information and the requested URL. Cloudflare may apply security checks at its edge. See Cloudflare’s privacy policy ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab).

Scripts and browser storage

These guide pages contain no advertising or analytics tags added by this section. The starter selector runs locally to show a role comparison; it does not submit your selection or store it. Fonts and images are hosted with this website. Edge-delivery services may add their own operational behavior.

Other Megabonk.org pages use Google Analytics and Google advertising services and have a separate site-wide privacy notice. Following a link out of this section can therefore load different services. This notice does not claim that the entire domain is free of third-party scripts.

Contact email

If you email qsyhxw@gmail.com, Gmail processes the message and the publisher receives the sender address and whatever details you include. Messages are used to handle the request. Do not include game passwords or unnecessary personal data. You may email the same address to request removal of correspondence, subject to any necessary record retention.

External websites

The original game, Discord and Steam apply their own terms and privacy practices. Opening the original game may create game-related browser storage or a server account; this guide does not operate that system. See the original project’s privacy notice ↗ (external website, opens in a new tab).

Questions or corrections

Use our contact page. This notice will be revised if this section adds services that change the practices described here.
