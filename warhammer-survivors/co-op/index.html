WARHAMMER SURVIVORS · LAUNCH GUIDE

Can you play Warhammer Survivors in co-op?

No launch co-op has been announced: Warhammer Survivors is single-player. Its squad followers are not a multiplayer mode. If playing with friends is the deciding factor, compare the local and online alternatives below before buying extra copies.

Warhammer Survivors co-op: the launch answer

Plan for one human player. Auroch Digital's official FAQ ↗ says the launch focuses on single-player and that the community will be told if multiplayer is added. That covers both offline and online co-op: there is no announced launch option for a second person on the couch or across the internet.

The Steam listing ↗ also identifies Single-player. As checked on 11 November 2026, the game has launched and the official FAQ continues to describe a single-player experience. A future possibility is not a confirmed release plan.

If playing together is essential, do not buy two copies or a console online subscription specifically for Warhammer Survivors co-op. Neither supplies the missing game mode.

Several characters on screen do not mean several players

Warhammer's squad system adds followers to a solo run. The developer's Squad System announcement describes Keywords that determine who can wield a weapon: a weapon your champion cannot use can bring a follower who uses it for you. Those allies are part of your loadout, rather than a friend joining with another controller.

Use the distinction when reading trailers or looking for a join button: a larger squad changes the solo build; it does not change the human player count.

Keep multiplayer and save transfer separate

Being able to move a save does not let two people share a run. Likewise, Steam Cloud on the product listing is not a promise of cross-platform progression. Do not assume that buying a console or mobile copy will preserve your PC unlocks.

Auroch's 6 October release announcement explicitly says the earlier demo does not transfer progress to the updated demo, and demo progress does not carry into the full game. If your only reason for grinding now is to enter launch with completed unlocks, that effort will not accomplish it. Use the demo to judge whether you enjoy the solo loop instead.

Before you invite anyone

  1. Decide whether Warhammer characters or simultaneous group play is your priority.
  2. For Warhammer, plan separate solo sessions. Recheck the developer FAQ if a later update announces multiplayer.
  3. For Vampire Survivors, write down each person's store/platform and whether they share a screen; check those against the co-op compatibility guide before buying extra copies.

Warhammer Survivors is a standalone Auroch Digital game, not a Vampire Survivors DLC. We do not provide an unofficial network patch or claim a working co-op mod.
