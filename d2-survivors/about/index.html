About the D2 Survivors guide

Megabonk.org editors · Facts reviewed 3 November 2026 · Checked against original build 20261102.2

Who runs this section

This is an unofficial fan guide published by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not framara, who makes D2 Survivors, and we are not Blizzard Entertainment; nothing here is an official announcement. Diablo II names and artwork belong to their owners.

The game is a fan project inspired by Diablo II. It is not a patch or remake of Diablo II or Diablo II: Resurrected, and the original’s own menu says it is unaffiliated with Blizzard.

What it covers

The section answers the questions players hit before and during a first run: which edition they are looking at (edition check), how to play in a game window, how to prepare the original’s files, how to find and back up a saved run, and two skill questions: resting or re-drafting a Druid skill and attack speed against Faster Cast Rate. Traditional Chinese and Korean entry points are on the Chinese localization page and the Korean start guide.

It does not rank classes or publish a best-build list.

Where the answers come from

  • The original game and its documentation: menus, the class selector, the SAVES dialog, the skill tree and the character panel are captured from the original’s browser game; platform, file and save rules are read from its FAQ ↗ and wiki ↗.
  • The third-party Play window: the edition on our Play page comes from a different host, whose displayed build labels are older than the original’s. We checked that it starts and accepts movement, nothing more.
  • Community tools: the Traditional Chinese tool is maintained by another player. We link to its author and have not installed or run it.

Where the original’s current documentation and an older playable build disagree, the page says which one a step describes. We have not completed a full run, moved a save between hosts or tested on a physical phone, and the pages do not claim to.

How pages stay current

Each guide shows the date it was checked. When the original publishes a new build, the pages that depend on its menus and save rules are rechecked first; the current baseline is build 20261102.2, read on 3 November 2026. A new build does not automatically validate every older answer.

Found something wrong?

Send the page URL, the host you used (original, our Play window or another site), the build shown on the menu and what you saw through the contact page. Corrections backed by a screenshot or a public reference are usually applied in the next update.
