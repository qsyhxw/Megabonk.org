PokéSurvivor beginner guide: your first run

Open pokesurvivor.com, pick a partner and keep moving: your active move fires on its own while you dodge. Take one card per level, decide on evolution at levels 20 and 40, and spend PokéCoins on your main partner’s upgrades between runs. Sign up first if you want to keep your progress.

  1. Open pokesurvivor.com in a browser (turn a phone to landscape). Sign up or log in: guest progress is not saved when you leave.
  2. Answer the partner questions, then keep the suggested partner or press Change.
  3. Press Play on Normal. Move with WASD or the arrow keys; only the active move attacks.
  4. At every level, pick one of four cards: a new move, an upgrade for a move you have, or a boost.
  5. At levels 20 and 40, choose to evolve your partner or keep its look; its stats rise either way.
  6. Beat the bosses at 2, 6, 10, 14 and 18 minutes and enter the rifts at 4, 8, 12 and 16 minutes.
  7. After the run, buy Magnet, Vitality and Strength levels for the partner you will keep using.

Which level-up card to take

Each card shows whether a move is Physical or Special and how well it fits your partner, for example “With Scorbunny it deals 84%: it hits harder with physical moves”. Take moves on your partner’s strong side.

  • Levels 2–6: get a second damaging move on your strong side, then upgrade your main move.
  • Max a move to level 5 and take the upgrade card it asks for (Power, Vigor, Reflexes, Sprint…): a golden evolution card appears, such as Ember into Fire Blast or Bubble into Surf.
  • Buffs deal no damage (Harden, Agility, Swords Dance, Nasty Plot): they work while they are the active move, so take them after your attacks are in place.
  • Sitrus Berry restores 45% of your HP at once; Move Deleter swaps out a move you regret; Reroll (R) redraws the four cards.

The full move-by-move advice is in the PokéSurvivor builds guide.

Before you travel to Eternatus or Arceus, collect the experience, coins and heals on the ground: they disappear when the boss arena loads. See rifts and bonds and the Judgment of Arceus.

Your first permanent upgrades

Upgrades are bought with PokéCoins in the menu and belong to one Pokémon only, so put your coins into the partner you play most. Each level costs more than the last.

Magnet50+12% pickup range: more experience and coins every run
Vitality60+3 HP per level for early mistakes
Stride70+1.5% speed to outrun the horde
Strength80+1.5% damage on every move
Repertoire1,500Start every run with 2 moves

The creator’s own tip: Repertoire and the move ban (Veto) change runs the most. Maxing every upgrade costs 93,470 PokéCoins per Pokémon and makes it hit and endure about 1.3 times more, so good movement still matters most.

Keep your progress

The partner card in the menu says Guest mode: not saved until you sign in. With an account your game is saved online and you can continue on another computer or phone; coins and tickets from a run arrive when the run ends. Save and sync checks covers moving between devices.
