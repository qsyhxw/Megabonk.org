PokéSurvivor gacha: tickets, pull costs and pity

A ten-pull costs 10 tickets or one ×10 ticket; a hundred-pull costs 100 tickets or ten ×10 tickets. Every ×10 pull guarantees at least one Epic, and after 70 pulls without a Legendary on a region’s banner the next one is Legendary. You earn tickets by playing: defeated Pokémon drop them now and then, and bosses, rift legendaries and the weekly challenge give ×10 tickets.

Tickets and ×10 tickets are two separate balances shown at the top of the screen: 9 tickets plus one ×10 ticket does not pay for the first button.

How to get tickets

Tickets cannot be bought with real money. Tickets you pick up in a run are saved when the run ends; if the game closes halfway, you get them when you come back.

  • Each ×10 pull guarantees at least one Epic.
  • The legendary counter is per region: “Pulls without a legendary in Kanto: 0 / 70”. Switching regions does not carry it over.
  • Duplicates turn into PokéCoins; shinies never repeat.
  • Every 10 ×10 pulls on a region give an egg that hatches in the Bag.

How to spend tickets

  • Choose the region whose legendary or Pokémon you want and stay on it until its 70-pull counter pays out.
  • Save ×10 tickets and use the ×10 button so every ten results include the Epic guarantee.
  • With an account, the gacha needs a connection; the 15 November update fixed balances that showed tickets you did not have.
  • Spend duplicate PokéCoins on upgrades for your main partner.
