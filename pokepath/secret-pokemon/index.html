Where to find hidden Pokémon in PokéPath TD

For scenery secrets, leave Challenge mode and wait between waves. For a hidden boss map, turn wave repeat off and close the victory screen after winning to collect the Pokémon.

Start with a route you already have

Greavard · Route 1-3
Click the open grave between waves. You can look for it independently of the Lunala fight.
Cacnea · Route 2-2
Click the cactus in the upper-left corner.
Dhelmise or Manaphy · Route 3-4
The boat gives Dhelmise. The cave leads to Manaphy, which you must defeat inside.
Ducklett · Route 4-2
Look in the water for the egg.
Sandygast · Route 5-1
Click the sandcastle on the beach. Beating Tapu Koko is a separate unlock.

For Calyrex, click the chessboard on Route 8-2 between waves, then win the Board Room fight. On Route 8-1, Galarian Farfetch’d has a different entrance condition: let Klefki escape during wave 50, enter the castle and defeat Sirfetch’d.

Three secrets you can check away from the path

  • Chatot. Outside Challenge mode, set Master volume to 0, Music to 4, Interface to 4 and Effects to 1. Note your own settings before changing them.
  • Luvdisc. Open Profile and click the Ho-Oh or Keldeo achievement badge. A locked badge still works.
  • Tyrunt. Open the Secrets tutorial in the Compendium and click its picture.

If you are looking for a shiny instead

Deploy Smeargle and let it hit enemies. Its ability can affect final evolutions you own, including Pokémon in the box. The project FAQ ↗ (new tab) gives the chance per hit as about 1 in 9,000, rising to 1 in 3,000 with Shiny Charm. An unevolved target cannot benefit. Each hit is another chance, so the published odds do not set a deadline for a particular shiny.

If a reward is missing

If clicking scenery does nothing, check the route number, mode and whether a wave is running. If you won a cave fight but the Pokémon is missing, turn off repeat and dismiss the victory screen. For MissingNo. and other website rewards, use the Player code from the save that will receive the reward. The redemption guide shows the two fields.

If the target is a regular route boss, look up its extra condition before replaying 100 waves. Regigigas needs a specific difficulty, while Shaymin checks equipment. The project’s 1.7.3 unlock list ↗ (new tab) includes later routes, puzzles and special evolutions. These instructions were checked against the list; individual rewards have not been claimed here.
