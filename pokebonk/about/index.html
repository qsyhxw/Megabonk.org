About this guide

Updated October 23, 2026

This PokeBonk section is maintained by the independent publisher of Megabonk.org. It helps players identify the original browser game and understand entry, starter choices and online features.

How we work

We inspect the original project’s public pages, current menus and publicly loaded game data. Guides distinguish source-based facts from editorial advice and retain dates, versions and material limits. A client definition is not a measured win rate or a phone compatibility test.

The October 23 review inspected Beta v0.8.25. We did not complete a controlled gameplay benchmark, a real-device mobile test, a Guest recovery or a shared co-op session. We do not label those as tested.

Who owns the game?

PokeBonk is a fan project led by Charcher. This guide is not operated by the game developer and is not affiliated with Nintendo, The Pokémon Company, Creatures or GAME FREAK. Game images and names belong to their respective rights holders. Our seed-compass navigation mark identifies this guide, not the game.

Corrections and image requests

Use our contact page with the URL, the disputed detail and a current source. Rights holders can use the same address for image or content requests.
