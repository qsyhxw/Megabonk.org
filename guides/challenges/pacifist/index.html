Forest Tier 1 Challenge

How to Beat the Pacifist Challenge in Megabonk

Weapons are disabled, but you are not helpless. Build around legal damage from character passives, Tomes and Items, then defeat every boss.

No weapons Goal: Kill all bosses Active bonus: 2x Silver Gain Verified July 27, 2026

Quick Answer

Select Pacifist in Forest Tier 1, then kill every boss without weapons. The challenge disables weapons automatically. Damage from character passives, Tomes and Items still counts, so the easiest plan is Amog with a poison Item engine or Sir Oofie with Thorns and heavy defense.

Important: Aura, Aegis and other weapon recommendations are not Pacifist solutions. You cannot equip them during this challenge.

Damaging TomesYesThorns Tome retaliates when an enemy hits you.
Damaging ItemsYesContact, retaliation, periodic and on-death Item effects can supply damage.
Defensive / utility TomesYesArmor, Shield, Regen and Attraction keep the damage engine working.

Amog still needs a way to poison or kill the first targets before Plague can chain. A passive that improves damage does not replace the initial damage source.

Best Characters for Pacifist

1. Amog — Best Offensive Pick

Amog's Plague makes poisoned enemies explode on death and spread poison. Once a poison Item starts the chain, packs clear much faster than with pure retaliation.

Tradeoff: weak before the first useful poison or damage Item appears.

2. Sir Oofie — Safest Pick

Sir Oofie gains Armor every level, making a Thorns-based run more forgiving. Stack sustain and let contact damage feed retaliation.

Tradeoff: bosses can take longer without a strong Item damage engine.

Best Pacifist Build

Damage engine — take at least one early

Cursed Doll, Electric Plug, Bob (Dead), Slurp Gloves, Toxic Barrel, Moldy Cheese, Moldy Gloves, Ghost and Spiky Shield can contribute without occupying a weapon slot. Favor effects that keep triggering during a boss fight.

Tome priorities

  1. Thorns Tome: the most direct repeatable retaliation source.
  2. Armor Tome: reduces the cost of taking contact hits.
  3. Regen Tome: restores health between heavy hits.
  4. Shield Tome: provides another defensive layer for bosses.
  5. Attraction Tome: keeps XP collection reliable while you kite.
Do not fill slots with weapon stats. A stat is only useful when it improves your actual passive, Tome or Item engine. Defense and pickup utility are usually more consistent than weapon-only scaling.

Step-by-Step Pacifist Strategy

  1. Activate Pacifist. Open Forest Tier 1 Challenges, select Pacifist, and confirm the no-weapons restriction before launching the run.
  2. Choose Amog or Sir Oofie. Amog has faster chain-clear potential; Sir Oofie offers the safest defensive foundation.
  3. Find damage immediately. Break pots, open early rewards and look for Thorns or a damaging Item. Resetting a run with no damage engine is faster than trying to scale nothing.
  4. Build around the source you found. Poison favors Amog; contact retaliation favors Armor, Regen, Shield and Sir Oofie.
  5. Preserve health before bosses. Kite normal enemies, collect XP and avoid spending all your sustain just to speed up trash clears.
  6. Keep boss damage reliable. Retaliate only against safe attacks, maintain poison or periodic Item effects, and dodge telegraphed heavy hits.
  7. Defeat every boss. Do not leave the run after one boss dies; the listed objective is to kill all required bosses.

Pacifist FAQ

Can Items deal damage in the Pacifist Challenge?

Yes. The restriction is no weapons, not no damage. Damage from Items, Tomes and character passives is allowed.

Does Thorns Tome work in Pacifist?

Yes. It is a Tome, so it remains available. Pair it with Armor, Regen or Shield rather than taking repeated hits with no sustain.

What is the best character for Pacifist?

Amog is the best offensive option when a poison Item starts Plague chains. Sir Oofie is the safer option for a defensive Thorns build.

Can I use Aura in the Pacifist Challenge?

No. Aura is a weapon, and Pacifist disables weapons. Passive area damage from an Item or character effect is different.

What do you get for completing Pacifist?

Pacifist has a 2x Silver Gain multiplier while active. Like other completed challenges, it also contributes the permanent +1% Silver Gain completion bonus.
