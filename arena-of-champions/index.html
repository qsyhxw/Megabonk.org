From guest sign-in to the arena

  1. Enter as a guest. Wait for the sign-in screen on the original site, then choose Play as Guest.
  2. Set up your controls. Pick a hero and review Controls and Key Bindings. Try movement, targeting and a few abilities in town.
  3. Choose a bracket. Use the game’s queue interface for 1v1, 2v2 or 3v3. Match availability depends on who is queuing.

Windows download and guest progress

Play in a desktop browser for a session without installation. For the desktop client, choose Download for Windows from the original sign-in screen.

To move a guest session to the Windows app, the game instructs you to use Save Progress in town, then sign in with that account in the app. Keep your browser’s guest data until you can confirm the account opens with your progress.

If the game stalls

Start at the original game link on a desktop browser. If the original site shows a browser verification screen, wait for it to finish before looking for the game’s sign-in menu. If it stalls, note whether the message appeared during verification, in the game download, or after choosing Play as Guest.

On 18 November 2026 the original site showed browser verification, so guest entry could not be rechecked. The sign-in and town screenshots show the original client on 1 November 2026.

Arena PvP or a survival run?

Choose Arena of Champions for online PvP with manual abilities. Choose Azeroth Survivors for automatic spells and enemy waves. The two games use separate characters, builds and saves.
