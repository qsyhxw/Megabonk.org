WARHAMMER SURVIVORS · BEST BUILDS

Best builds in Warhammer Survivors

Start with Amalia Novena and the Eviscerator Chainsword, or Brother Luca and the Heavy Bolter. Both are cheap champions (400 and 200 coins), evolve with passives you unlock in the first runs, and carry you into the 25-minute bosses.

Five builds that work now

Eviscerator Chainsword (easiest strong start)

Sister Superior Amalia Novena (400 coins)

  • Chainsword → max
  • Tape Measure (any level, max later)
  • Shield of Faith

Add: Nuln Oil, Magnet

Amalia gains +10% range every 10 levels and Shield of Faith blocks lanes, so the evolved chainsword sweeps whole screens. Tape Measure unlocks after surviving 1 minute. Recipe →

Heavy Bolter (best ranged 40K)

Brother Luca Amasius (200 coins)

  • Boltgun → max
  • Dice Cube
  • Shield of Faith

Add: Tape Measure, D6 Dice

Slow until the Boltgun has a few upgrades, then a constant barrage that pushes bosses back. Dice Cube unlocks by getting D6 Dice to level 6 in 40K. Recipe →

Meltagun (boss killer)

Trooper Bartosz Kozlowski (rescue, Imperial Bastion)

  • Lasgun → max
  • Gauntlet

Add: Shield of Faith (second follower)

Short-range beam with bonus damage to bosses and close enemies; Lasgun also counts toward the follower objectives. Recipe →

Whirlwind Axes (Age of Sigmar start)

Neave Blacktalon (start)

  • Tempest Axes → max
  • Sabaton

Add: Bandolier, Hourglass

Available from the first AoS run; Sabaton is a movement passive, so the build also kites well. Recipe →

Sustained Fire Dice (both universes)

Any champion

  • D6 Dice → max
  • Scatter Dice

Add: Dice Cube

Works in either universe; the initial roll splits into extra exploding dice. Recipe →

Upgrade order inside a run

  1. Level the main weapon first until it is maxed; take its companion passive the first time it is offered (level 1 is enough for most recipes).
  2. Take Shield of Faith early if offered: it blocks enemy lanes and is the strongest early defensive pick.
  3. Grab a Magnet before big XP piles: one Magnet can give several levels at once.
  4. Open the next elite/boss chest once the weapon is maxed to evolve it, then start the second evolution.

Builds for the Extremis bosses

The six 25-minute bosses each need a specific evolution (Gauntlets of Ultramar, Weapon of the Matriarch, Bale Eye, Anti-Wizard Flintlock, Uskavar the Sunderer, Zangrom-Thaz). Plan that weapon as your main build for a boss run: boss counters.
