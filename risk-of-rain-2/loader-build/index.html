Risk of Rain 2 Loader build: loadout and items from 2026 guides

Short answer: GameBrief rates Loader S tier for a solo Rainstorm run. Both guides name Charged Gauntlet as the utility. Knuckleboom is the default primary, named in sabo.gg's loadout and called the default swing by Rogue Ranker. Kjaro's Band, Runald's Band and Crowbar are the items both guides name. The secondary and special skills depend on what you have unlocked. This is a summary of two guides, not our own playtesting.

Tier and mode

GameBrief places Loader in S tier and calls her the best general recommendation. Its ranking uses a solo Rainstorm baseline. It also lists Loader among solo picks and mentions her for co-op objectives and Eclipse. Rogue Ranker and sabo.gg do not give a tier.

How these were chosen

Two guides name skills and items; GameBrief gives the tier only. None of the three states a game patch number, so check the current patch notes before relying on specific skill or item behaviour. Only items named by a guide are listed; this page does not add items from our own testing.
