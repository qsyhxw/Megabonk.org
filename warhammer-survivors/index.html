Get into the game

Full release, Game Pass and platform choices.

The developer confirms the full game launched on 10 November 2026. The announced base price is $7.99 / £6.39 / €7.49, with platform and region differences possible. Auroch Digital FAQ

If you have Game Pass Ultimate or PC Game Pass, check your existing access before buying a second copy. Xbox Wire lists Warhammer Survivors for 10 November across cloud, Xbox Series X|S, handheld and PC under those plans. The named device list and membership determine the route available to you. Before you start

Demo progress, platforms and launch-day questions.

Try the PC demo & understand save transfers
  1. On Windows PC, open the official Steam page and look for Download Warhammer Survivors Demo. The full game and the demo are separate choices.
  2. For Xbox PC, use the Xbox demo listing. Check the device compatibility on that listing.
  3. Start the current October demo if you want to judge the controls and solo combat. Keep any recipe notes tied to that build.

Warhammer Community names Steam and Xbox PC as demo routes. Its console launch list does not establish that every console has a demo. Warhammer Community demo announcement

The earlier demo's progress does not carry into the updated demo, and this demo's progress does not carry into the full game. For permanent progression, begin again in the released game. Auroch October 6 release announcement

iPhone & iPad availability

The developer-linked App Store listing is by Auroch Digital Ltd. The US listing showed $7.19 on 11 November, alongside the developer's $7.99 base price and platform-dependent launch discount. Check your local storefront price before purchase. Apple App Store listing ↗

  1. Open the listing on the device you plan to use.
  2. Expand Compatibility and compare its iOS or iPadOS requirement with Settings → General → About. The current listing requires version 15.0 or later.
  3. Confirm that your device and store region offer the purchase, then check available storage and the current download size.

Apple lists iPhone and iPad support. Cross-store save transfer and device-specific performance have not been verified here. If continuing a PC save is essential, confirm that support before buying a second copy.

Tor Garadon & launch content

Tor Garadon unlocks after 10,000 enemies defeated across multiple runs. Check the 40K Objectives entry and cumulative progress between runs. A single-run kill target would lead you to spend effort on a different task.

Launch issues & support

After seeing the first chest of each rarity, the developer says you can skip that rarity's animation using right-click, Esc or gamepad B. On Switch 2 with a Switch Pro Controller, use the joystick if menu d-pad input fails. These checks address the two symptoms documented in the 10 November FAQ. For the separate Failed to load il2cpp error, consult the developer's current instructions and report form before changing Windows security settings. Official known issues and reporting form

The game, its developers & the two universes

Auroch Digital develops Warhammer Survivors in partnership with Warhammer and poncle. It is a standalone purchase. Existing Vampire Survivors ownership does not supply this game's license. The developer describes a game built on the Vampire Survivors foundation, with its own champions, weapons, stages and secrets. Developer game FAQ

Warhammer 40,000 and Age of Sigmar are represented here. Select the universe that matches your run when using the recipe table. Several similar-looking weapon names lead to different recipes.

Android download & APK searches

Use the official Google Play listing for Warhammer Survivors by Auroch Digital, package com.AurochDigital.WarhammerSurvivors. Open it with the Google account and Android device you intend to use, check compatibility and the local purchase price, then install through Play. Official Google Play purchase and installation ↗

Nintendo Switch 2 access & launch fixes

Auroch lists Nintendo Switch 1 and 2 among the released platforms. Choose your console and region through the official game page ↗ and confirm the Nintendo eShop product before purchase. The developer’s FAQ advises using the joystick if a Switch Pro Controller d-pad does not navigate menus on Switch 2. Developer platform and known-issue FAQ

The 11 November Steam hotfix v2.1.6 schedules its changes for Switch 1 and 2 from the following week. Check your installed version before expecting the rescued Catachan weapon fix or the updated chest controls. Developer hotfix notes
