Brotato Shop Guide — Recycling, Locks and Reroll Costs

Use Lock to keep an offer for the next visit, Reroll to refresh the offers for a material cost, and Recycle to convert an owned item or weapon back into materials. Recycle values depend on the current price and on modifiers such as the Recycling Machine.

The layout above matches a wave 2 capture. Item prices rise with each wave. The Brotato Wiki Shop page describes the lock and reroll rules.

Recycle for materials

Recycling returns a share of an item’s value and removes the item or weapon. Sources we checked agree on the base return of 25%. A Recycling Machine raises the return to 60%, and the Entrepreneur bonus adds 25% on top, for 85% in total. The payout follows the current price, so coupons and inflation can change it.

Equipped weapons can be recycled according to a shop guide. Sources conflict on whether items bought from the shop can be sold freely, so check the in-game tooltip before planning a sale. Confirm the percentages on your own copy, since balance patches can change them.

Recycling achievement

The Recycling achievement requires recycling 12 weapons from inventory during a single run. Sources report that it triggers when the 12th weapon is recycled. Plan the count across the run, not only at the end.

Plan the next visit

If you cannot afford the offer you need, lock it, reroll only when the budget allows, and recycle surplus weapons to fund the plan. Use the Evolution guide to decide whether the weapon you are buying belongs to a Mecha or Mutation route.
