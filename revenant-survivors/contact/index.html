Contact the Revenant Survivors guide editors

Reviewed 22 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • An unlock rule, Hard level reward or Mythic set that changed in a newer update;
  • A Demo save or Steam Cloud step that works differently from the transfer guide;
  • A controller or auto-attack behaviour that the current release handles differently;
  • A wrong screenshot, link or item name on a Revenant Survivors page.

Include the page URL, your edition (Demo or full game), the game version and what happened. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not the developer or the game’s support team, so we cannot restore a save, fix a Steam purchase or change how the game works. Use the Steam Community hub ↗ to reach Mongma Studio.
