Sonic Whip unlock and evolution

Evolve Sonic Whip at level 8 with Skull O’Maniac at any level, then open an evolution-capable chest. Sonia starts with it. Survive 8 minutes as Sonia to add it to the normal weapon pool. This is an Ode to Castlevania weapon.

By Megabonk.org · checked 10 November 2026 · Vampire Survivors is by poncle.

Get Sonia, then unlock the weapon

  1. Own and enable Ode to Castlevania in your full-game edition.
  2. Defeat the Giant Bat inside the castle and collect the Stallion Gate relic.
  3. Collect seven Heart Refresh pickups after opening that gate, then purchase Sonia in character selection.
  4. Choose Sonia with her default starting weapon. Survive eight minutes to unlock Sonic Whip for other characters’ level-up choices.

Unlocking a character, starting with her weapon and adding that weapon to the random pool are separate steps. The screenshot identifies Sonia and the blue whip icon, rather than documenting a fresh unlock.

Prepare the evolution

ComponentRequired state
Sonic WhipLevel 8
Skull O’ManiacOwned, any level
ChestAn evolution-capable reward after the components are ready

Do not apply a blanket “all DLC passives must be maxed” rule to this recipe. Chest eligibility depends on the stage and reward type. Waiting ten minutes alone cannot repair a sealed weapon or a missing passive.
