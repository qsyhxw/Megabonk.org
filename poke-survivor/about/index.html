About the PokéSurvivor guides

Updated November 16, 2026

Who writes these guides

This is an unofficial fan guide written by the editors of Megabonk.org, an independent site for survivors-style games. We are not JPTER78 (Jonathan Piera Torregrosa), the creator of PokéSurvivor, and we are not Nintendo, Creatures, GAME FREAK or The Pokémon Company. Nothing here is an official announcement or game support. Pokémon names, sprites and the game’s menu icons belong to their owners and are shown to identify what each guide talks about.

Which PokéSurvivor this section covers

These guides cover JPTER78’s PokéSurvivor at pokesurvivor.com, a free browser game for PC and phones, first released on itch.io on 11 October 2026. On 16 November 2026 its login panel carried the subtitle “Mystery Dungeon” and named pokesurvivor.com as the official website, adding that anyone asking you to pay is not the creator.

  • Other addresses with the same name, including pokesurvivor.doofcraft.com, are not established as the creator’s edition. Their operator, build and save compatibility are unknown, so check the full address before you sign in.
  • The Play page on this site loads a third-party edition hosted by pokesurvivor.pro. Its accounts, saves and rules are separate from pokesurvivor.com.
  • PokeBonk is Charcher’s different 3D browser game, and Megabonk is a Steam game; controls, partners and builds do not carry over. Pokémon Survivor Burst by ClessioTV is another separate download project.
  • Native ports such as JayElDragon’s Poke Survivors V5.1 change endgame rules; the mobile guide says where they differ.

What the section covers

The guides answer the questions players hit between runs: how to play online and handle the first run and controls, how to match buffs to physical or special moves and Bonds, what ticket pulls cost, when rifts can open, what changes in the Judgment of Arceus after minute 30, and how to handle phones and downloads, saves, co-op rooms and freezes or lag. There is also a Chinese beginner guide and a Spanish play and download guide.

Where the answers come from

  • The game itself: menus, the login panel and the title screen are captured from pokesurvivor.com; each capture is dated.
  • The creator’s pages: the guide and FAQ on pokesurvivor.com and the itch.io release page, for controls, devices and core progression.
  • The game’s source: ticket costs, rift timings, the minute-30 Judgment and save states were read from JPTER78’s public source at revision 680c3e12c670 (25 October 2026). That repository was no longer publicly reachable on 16 November 2026, so rules that newer builds change are rechecked against the live game.
  • Dated player reports: issues filed on the creator’s tracker help narrow troubleshooting steps; each is dated beside the step it informs.

We have not completed controlled full runs, phone benchmarks, shared co-op matches or account recovery, so pages describe those parts as checks to make rather than measured results.

How pages stay current

Guides were last reviewed on 26 October 2026 against the source revision above, and the game identity on this page on 16 November 2026. When the creator changes a rule, the affected guide is updated and listed under Latest updates on the guide home page.

Found something wrong?

Send the page address, the game address you play on and what you see in the game through the contact page. A screenshot of the menu or card in question helps us apply the fix quickly. We can correct this website; we cannot change the game or access player accounts. The PokéSurvivor privacy notice explains the services these pages use.
