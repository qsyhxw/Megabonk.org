PokéSurvivor on mobile: Android, iPhone and app

Play in your phone’s browser at pokesurvivor.com, held in landscape; you do not need an APK. For an app icon use “Add to Home Screen”. Sign up to keep your progress: guest progress is not saved when you leave.

Hold the phone sideways

On an upright phone the game only shows a “Rotate your phone” message; turn it to landscape and the menu appears.

Touch controls

  • Put a finger anywhere and drag to move; the joystick appears where you touch.
  • Tap a move icon to make it the active move, or an orb to use it.
  • Pause with the top-right button; after beating Eternatus your power has its own button.
  • Settings → Controls also lists controller buttons if you connect one.

Android and iPhone use the same browser game and the same account.

Smoother play and less battery

  • Settings → Graphics → Quality: Low (or Auto, which lowers it when the game stutters).
  • Pokémon effects and Ground trails: “Only my partner” or “None” in big fights.
  • FPS limit 60 or 30: the game runs just as fast and the phone stays cooler.
  • Close other tabs; if moves stop responding, see freezes and lag.

Downloads to avoid

PokéSurvivor is free, with no ads or purchases, and the only official website is pokesurvivor.com; the game itself says that anyone asking you to pay is not the creator. An APK from another site is not the creator’s app, and its saves and rules can differ. Moving progress between devices: save and sync checks.
