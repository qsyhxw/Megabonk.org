Plan support Alpha slots and first-type bonuses

Normal support capacity starts at two. The Support team Lab upgrade adds one, and S.S. Ticket adds one for the run. A shiny support is separate from the normal team limit. Use the planner to compare first-type bonuses rather than treating support slots as extra move slots.[1][2]

Keep support capture and League capture separate

  1. Find and weaken an Alpha in the actual run.
  2. Use the support/Friend Ball interaction shown by the game; the support is for this run.
  3. If the normal team is full, decide which support to release before assuming the capture adds a free slot.
  4. Recheck capacity when selling S.S. Ticket. The source trims normal supports when the team exceeds the new cap.

A support Alpha does not permanently unlock its League partner. Legendary League membership and shiny capture have their own .63 rules.
