Contact the Hordes of Azeroth guide editors

Reviewed 5 November 2026

Email

Write to contact@megabonk.org. Useful reports for this section:

  • A client requirement, platform or co-op rule that changed in a newer release;
  • An installation message or fix that does not match the stage described on the troubleshooting page;
  • A wrong project identification, link or screenshot on a Hordes of Azeroth page;
  • A Chinese start-page step that no longer matches the launcher.

Include the page URL, your operating system, the launcher version and what happened. A screenshot helps us confirm the fix quickly.

What we cannot help with

We are not the author or the project's support, so we cannot repair an installation, recover a save or change how the game works. Use the launcher's Report a bug option or the author's GitHub repository ↗ (opens in a new tab) for those.
