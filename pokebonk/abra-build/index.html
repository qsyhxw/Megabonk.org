Build Abra around its Psychic kit

For Abra, start from its curated Psychic/Special kit and reserve space for Psybeam plus Future Sight if you want Prismatic Premonition. Magic Guard covers specific ground hazards and reduces projectile/area damage; it does not remove every survival problem. This is a source-based build plan, not a measured best-DPS build.[1][2]

Checked October 28, 2026 · Beta v0.8.62 · By Megabonk.org · Source-based guide

Use the League kit, not the raw Pokémon learnset

Original-game scope: pokebonk.com v0.8.62, checked October 28. Our Play window uses a separate third-party v0.8.27 edition and separate progress. Recommendations here are source-based decisions; no original-game full clear, catch, reward claim or co-op completion was tested.

The League record supplies this four-move kit. A raw main-series Abra learnset focused on Teleport is not the playable run’s complete attack pool. Abra’s listed challenge is reaching level 30 in one run, then completing its capture; meeting that challenge does not itself select Abra for the next run.[2]

At Bill’s Machine, the signature definition pairs Psybeam and Future Sight. Raise both to the required maximum, inspect the actual fusion option, then decide. No fixed offer order is claimed.[1]

Check what each upgrade actually affects

The listed kit and signature result use Special damage and Psychic type. Calcium’s Special target and a Psychic-specific effect describe different scopes; neither means every physical or off-type attack you add receives the same benefit.

The signature’s inspected part definition marks enemy-targeted rays; Area and additional-count behavior affect particular parts. Use the live fusion card after combining instead of importing a general “Projectiles improves everything” rule.

Magic Guard’s description covers damage from specified ground hazards and reduces projectile/area damage by 30%. The passive’s static modifier also lists a Special multiplier. It is not blanket immunity to contact, all boss attacks or poor positioning.[1][2]
