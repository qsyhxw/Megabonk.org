Choose between another region and a faster clear

The project’s Season 2 notes describe Global Time Attack as best wins across regions, prioritizing region coverage before total time. Keep region coverage and a single-map personal best as different objectives.

For an uncleared region, first establish a repeatable clear. For a known route, record avoidable detours and delays between objectives. Our tool cannot predict a finishing time from your draft.

[2]

A medal is not a guaranteed seasonal prize

A personal time medal and a ranked seasonal reward are separate. Rank depends on other submitted runs and the project’s review; this page does not fetch live leaderboards or claim a prize for your account.

Before chasing a seasonal payout, read the current season information and reward notice in the original game. Historical dates or older screenshots are not proof that a reward can still be earned.

[2]
