Choose bonuses that affect your actual kit

Check the move’s damage class and type before choosing its bonus. Protein affects Physical damage; Calcium affects Special damage. A type item and a general damage multiplier can cover different parts of the same kit. The tables expose static modifiers and caps; conditional effects need their own card.[1][2]

Do not multiply a listed proc chance by stack count and publish the result as a verified probability. Static modifiers, trigger scripts and caps are separate pieces of the mechanic.

Read the stat before selecting its source

The tables above list sources and static effects. For a worked decision, compare Luck’s normalized rarity roll with Status Chance’s per-hit multiplier. More rarity is not the same as more Sleep applications, and more Status Damage is not itself another affected enemy.

New task-specific additions reviewed October 28 against the original v0.8.62 client; recommendations remain source-based.[3]

Check the scope of each planned upgrade

Put the selected move beside its class and type in the planner. For example, Bulbasaur’s Razor Leaf is Physical and Solar Beam is Special; a Physical-only bonus does not cover both. Squirtle’s Rapid Spin is Normal and Hydro Pump is Water; Water-only damage does not cover the entire pre-fusion pair. Use the completed fusion’s part descriptions before choosing Area, Duration or Projectiles.

For conditional sources, keep the activation condition in the plan: a low-HP bonus requires low HP, and a status bonus still needs an actual applying hit. The static table is not a complete trigger simulation. Do not add every displayed percentage into one supposed DPS total. Inspect the actual move kit and compare status and rarity examples.
