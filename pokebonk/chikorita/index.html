Chikorita: passive, kit and evolution

Chikorita is a Johto League partner in PokeBonk v0.10.18. Its passive is Soothing Scent; its evolution levels come from the public client and differ from the official games.

Megabonk.org · Updated October 23, 2026 · v0.8.25

Type and role

Chikorita is a Flower guardian partner. Type: Grass.

Portrait from the existing PokeBonk sprite set.

Passive: Soothing Scent

+15% max HP and -8% damage. Every 4 s, a ring of aroma (5 m; Meganium, 7 m) charms enemies for 3 s (they deal 40% less damage) and heals you for 1% HP for every enemy hit, up to 6%.

Kit: the four listed moves

Base values from the public client. Upgrades, vitamins and fusions change them in play.

Evolution

PokeBonk client evolution levels: the first evolution happens at Lv 24 and the second at Lv 41. These are the client values; the official games use different levels.

Unlock requirement

  • Defeat 3,000 Johto Pokémon.

Challenge text from the public client.

Season 3 rating

Rated S in the Season 3 list. This rating is not re-checked for v0.10.18.

What this page does not cover

Based on the client text only. No in-game run was completed, so no damage, win-rate or clear-time claim is made.
