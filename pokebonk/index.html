About the game

PokeBonk is a free, unofficial 3D horde-survival fan game by Charcher, played at pokebonk.com. You pick a Pokémon partner, level four moves during a run, fuse pairs into stronger moves and unlock more League partners between runs. Season 3 added Johto, bringing the League to 138 partners. It is a separate game from Megabonk: PokeBonk vs Megabonk.

PokeBonk FAQ

Short answers; each links to the full guide.

How do you play PokeBonk?

Open pokebonk.com in a desktop or phone browser and choose Play as a guest; no sign-up is needed. PokeBonk is a free, unofficial fan game by Charcher. Pick one of the three starters, then follow the upgrade cards during the run.

What is the best build in PokeBonk?

Celebi is the top Season 3 pick: take Grassy Glide, Magical Leaf, Seed Flare, Leaf Storm, fuse them into Forest of Time and add Miracle Seed, Wise Glasses, Leftovers. Chikorita, Heracross are the next S tier builds.

Is PokeBonk multiplayer?

Yes. PokeBonk has a Co-op Beta for 2–4 trainers with room creation, invites and code-based joining. The server can restrict or pause access, so check the Co-op screen before inviting friends.

Multiplayer & co-op →
Can you play PokeBonk on mobile or download it?

Yes. The browser game runs on Android and iPhone with touch controls. The original game’s Apps menu lists downloads for Windows, macOS, Linux and Android; there is no iOS app, so use the browser on iPhone.

How do you catch Pokémon in PokeBonk?

Complete the partner’s League requirement, then find its encounter and weaken it. Ordinary League partners also need the nearby throw; legendaries join once weakened, and the ball only matters for their shiny.

How to catch Pokémon →
How do you get Mewtwo in PokeBonk?

Have 60 League members, Articuno, Zapdos and Moltres in your League, and clear Rocky Desert on Hard. The 45-minute Mewtwo in Survival is a separate encounter.

Mewtwo →
Is PokeBonk down?

Check where it fails: if pokebonk.com does not load at all, the host is down; if it loads but the game will not connect, read the server message on the entry screen. Keep your Guest session and do not clear site data while you check.
