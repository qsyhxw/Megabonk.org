Warlock build: choose a route that fits

Choose Affliction when you want damage-over-time kills to spread through a crowd. Choose Demonology when you want summons alongside your spells. A late-tree setup changes your opening spell; it is not the same character as a fresh Warlock.[1]

Start from Shadow Bolt on a fresh Warlock. Choose Affliction for spreading DoTs, Demonology for summons, or Destruction for linked direct spells. The strongest choice for every progression state has not been measured.[1]

Affliction: establish DoTs before counting spread

  1. With the default Shadow Bolt start, take usable Corruption or Curse of Agony when offered to establish the DoT engine.
  2. Aim toward Nightfall, Contagion, Siphon Life and Seed of Corruption, checking the actual gate and prerequisite before each purchase.
  3. Once Contagion is active, keep DoTs applied so deaths can pass them on. Do not expect spreading from an isolated target with no deaths.
  4. With Siphon Life active, DoT damage supplies healing. If it is not allocated, use available survival rather than assuming the route already heals.
  5. After Seed changes the starter to Corruption, draft around that opening. Against a lone boss, compare sustained damage rather than a chain-clear peak.

This is a priority framework for the dated original snapshot, not a guaranteed offer sequence or complete point allocation. Check talent budgets and gates →[1]

Demonology: choose the demon you can support

  1. Inspect the summons actually allocated. Demonic Familiar, Soul Devourer and Soul Link describe different pet interactions.
  2. Choose between Soul Devourer and Soul Link; do not count the Felhunter and Voidwalker benefits together.
  3. For the developed Dread Lord branch, Searing Pain has an explicit link to Felseeker. Support that spell if this is the selected engine.
  4. If Butcher is selected instead, inspect the Felguard description. Do not assume the Dread Lord spell-scaling rule applies to it.
  5. If summon damage is working but you still die, take relevant recovery or defence before adding another disconnected attack.

This is a priority framework for the dated original snapshot, not a guaranteed offer sequence or complete point allocation. Check talent budgets and gates →[1]

Destruction: connect Immolate to direct hits

  1. Before the starter-changing milestone, maintain the default Shadow Bolt attack. Shadow Cleave adds a fork interaction when allocated.
  2. Read Conflagrate before drafting: it starts the run with Immolate and makes Shadow Bolt or Searing Pain hits explode on Immolated targets.
  3. Support Immolate plus the direct spell that triggers the interaction. An unburning target does not satisfy the Immolated-target condition.
  4. Rain of Chaos supplies a second automatic Rain of Fire and slowing ground. This is a later talent benefit, not a free effect on a new Warlock.
  5. When a desired card is missing, strengthen the linked spell you have or take survival. Do not treat an unallocated Siphon Life as healing for this branch.

This is a priority framework for the dated original snapshot, not a guaranteed offer sequence or complete point allocation. Check talent budgets and gates →[1]

Current Demonology checks: Dread Lord or Felguard

In v0.130.0, Pact of the Nathrezim summons a Dread Lord; its Felseeker inherits Searing Pain upgrades, hits up to six enemies and increases primary-target damage. The inspected text gives a thirty-second base summon interval reduced by cooldown reduction to a thirteen-second floor, with up to two active. Its duration is a runtime rank value, so we do not copy a fixed twenty-second duration from another guide. It is exclusive with Butcher, whose Felguard cleaves and has a stronger low-health target interaction.[1]

Choose the demon branch before drafting the supporting spell. Searing Pain upgrades have an explicit Dread Lord link; they are not proof of an identical Felguard benefit. Earlier Pit Lord recordings describe a different version. This site’s earlier route table below was generic Demonology, not an allocated Pit Lord recipe; its v0.127.0 scope remains stated. No current damage benchmark establishes a winner.[1]

Other current alternatives also exclude each other: Soul Devourer’s Felhunter consumes part of a damage-over-time duration, whereas Soul Link’s Voidwalker scales with damage reduction/bonus health and supplies shielding. Do not combine the benefits of both as if they were one allocated node. Check prerequisites and exclusive choices →[1]

What to prioritise during the run

  1. With only Shadow Bolt available, use an offered Corruption or Curse of Agony to establish the DoT engine before expecting propagation.
  2. Once Contagion is active, favour damage over time that can remain on several enemies; move so afflicted enemies die near another target.
  3. If incoming damage is the problem, check whether Siphon Life is actually allocated. More targets do not substitute for a missing healing talent.
  4. With Seed selected, adapt around the Corruption start. Do not spend the opening waiting for the default Shadow Bolt to appear.

This is an editorial choice order, not a guaranteed card sequence. The offered upgrades and your current permanent investment still determine which step is available. [1]

When the intended build is not ready

If the spread branch has not opened, use the spells you receive to keep room to move; do not reserve the whole run for a final-tree effect. If packs clear but a boss stalls, prioritise the damage source that is actually hitting that boss rather than counting chain explosions that need adds.

Before changing the whole plan, identify the failure: no room to move, too little recovery, or too little damage on the target. Change the part that addresses that failure, then compare another run with similar talents and equipment.

Check the starter and trigger before copying a build

Baseline starter: Shadow Bolt. Talents can replace it; use the route conditions above rather than treating this as the opening for every developed character. [1]

Do not read “DoT healing” as 1% of your maximum health per tick. The inspected Siphon Life text describes healing from the tick’s damage. Exact rank-dependent damage values are omitted where the client exposes a template.

Record the game version, zone, difficulty, selected capstone, permanent equipment and the card that changed the run. Comparing two different progression states cannot establish that a single spell was better. Use the meter carefully →

Warlock build questions

Is Affliction always the best Warlock build?

It is a clear direction for DoT spread, but its healing and propagation need the named talents. The same chain does not establish isolated-boss performance.

Can I combine the Dread Lord and Felguard endpoints?

Pact of the Nathrezim and Butcher are exclusive in the inspected original snapshot. Plan around the branch actually selected.[1]

Should I restart when the recommended item does not appear?

No named jewelry item is a requirement in these plans. Keep a working engine and compare the offer that solves the current damage or survival problem.
