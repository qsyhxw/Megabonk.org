✅ How Verified Megabonk Leaderboard Runs Work

Evidence requirements, review status and data provenance; submission destinations are covered separately.

Loading current community snapshot…
This page explains evidence rules and what verified status means. For the official Steam distinction and current submission destination, use Official Status & Submit. Megabonk.org uses the independent Leaderboard.gg community dataset because it exposes reviewed runs, video links and complete build data.

🔍What “Verified” Means Here

ℹ️Mirrored review status
A verified record is an approved record present in the Leaderboard.gg dataset. Megabonk.org mirrors that source status; it does not independently approve, reject or remove runs.
FieldMeaning
Review statusApproved in the synchronized source dataset.
Submission timeThe source-created or source-updated timestamp when the board supplies one.
firstSeenAtThe first successful Megabonk.org synchronization that observed the record.
lastSeenAtThe most recent successful synchronization that still observed the record.

🛡️Video Evidence and Prohibited Changes

  • Show the complete run and a clear final score screen.
  • Keep the submitted video accessible for community review.
  • Use the patch currently accepted by the board.
  • Do not use gameplay-changing mods, hacking or exploits.
  • Do not submit edited footage that hides or changes run evidence.
