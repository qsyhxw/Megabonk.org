Brotato Unlocks — Baby, Beast Master, Fisherman and More

Baby, Beast Master, Fisherman, Farmer, Generalist and Gangster have unlock conditions that the checked sites agree on, and Celery Tea unlocks by winning a run with Baby. Pet unlock conditions and a few item entries conflict between sources, so they are not listed here.

Megabonk.org editors · Checked 26 November 2026

Character unlock conditionsItem unlockNot listed here

Character unlock conditions

CharacterUnlock conditionSources that agree
BabyReach level 10 before wave 6 (Fast Learner)brotatodb, Brotator, brotato.app
Beast MasterUnlock the Catling Gun, Bonk Dog, Bot-O-Mine, Blazemander and Lootworm itemsBrotator, brotato.app
FishermanGet 2 Bait during a run (Baited)brotatodb, Brotator, brotato.app
FarmerReach +200 Harvestingbrotatodb, Brotator, brotato.app
GeneralistCollect 2000 materialsbrotatodb, Brotator, brotato.app
GangsterReroll 10 times in a single shopBrotator, brotato.app

Two or three independent sites give each row above. The sites are community databases and the Brotato Wiki; check the in-game unlock screen if a condition does not match.

Item unlock

ItemUnlock conditionSources that agree
Celery TeaWin a run with Babybrotatodb, brotato.app

Celery Tea is the reward for winning a run with Baby. Win the run with Baby itself, not any character.

Not listed here

Pet unlock conditions differ across sources, so this page omits them. Scapegoat also has conflicting entries: one database ties it to a Beast Master win, while another lists it as a base-pool item with no unlock challenge. Check the in-game Codex for those entries before planning a run around them.
